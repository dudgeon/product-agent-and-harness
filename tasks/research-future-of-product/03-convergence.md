# Convergence: your beliefs against the evidence

Step 4 of [the plan](00-plan.md), second version. Your claims from [01-your-beliefs.md](01-your-beliefs.md) are set beside the 20 vision statements in [02-expert-view.md](02-expert-view.md) (V1-V20).

## What changed in the method

The first version marked you down wherever companies do not yet do what you describe. That was wrong: you are describing the future, and today's practice only shows direction, pace and distance. This version uses your separation.

- **The vision** says what will be true.
- **The assumptions** under it say what has to be true to get there, including the work a pre-AI enterprise must do first: writing down what lives in people's heads, giving records owners and keeping them current, holding obligations as data, and ending coordination that runs person to person. These never compete with the vision.
- A claim is marked **trajectory bends** only when the direction of travel points somewhere else, or when something that will not change with better models (law, economics, human nature) argues against it. "Nobody does this yet" is distance, not disagreement.
- The statements are about **product work**, whoever does it. One statement (V18) covers roles.

**Verdicts used.** *Trajectory supports*: practice is moving your way. *Trajectory supports, large assumptions*: moving your way, with a lot that has to be built. *Evidence extends it*: supports you and adds something. *Trajectory bends*: points somewhere else, with the reason. *No evidence yet either way.*

**Overall.** The trajectory supports all five beliefs. It bends away from your wording in four places, each partial and each explained below. The evidence also adds nine statements on things you do not mention.

## 2. What has to be true first

You asked that the starting state be stated as assumptions, plainly, even where obvious. It is now a section at the front of 02 and a line under every statement. In short, all five beliefs assume four things an average large enterprise does not yet have:

1. **What lives in people's heads is written down.** Today: the one person who knows why a rule exists.
2. **Records have owners and are current.** Today: the owner of record left two reorganisations ago.
3. **Obligations are data.** Today: a spreadsheet kept by one analyst, and a different answer from each reviewer.
4. **Coordination does not depend on who you know.** Today: status lives in the Tuesday meeting.

The evidence that this decides the outcome is the most consistent in the research (Shopify's agent went from 36% to 77% of changes accepted in two months with no change of model, only better written context). Agents are starting to do the capture themselves. The cost is unmeasured. One deadline: much of a bank's memory sits with people near retirement.

## 4. Questions for you

Five choices of direction or values. Research cannot settle them.

**Q1. What is the released time for?**
Today it goes by default into more output for the same pay. The options are relief, bigger ambitions, fewer people, or a stated mix.
*What changes:* this decides whether B1.1 comes true. It sets what V1 and V20 say, what the organisation promises people whose work was moving information, and whether the scenarios show shorter weeks, bigger slates or smaller teams.

**Q2. How far out is the vision, and how bold?**
I have set it at about 2032 to 2034 for a large bank, with nearer dates on each statement. The bold voices say two years; the cautious say a decade or more for organisations. The boldest single element is owner agents acting alone on regulated customer processes.
*What changes:* a nearer date makes a sharper call to act and a higher risk of being wrong. A further one is safer and easier to ignore. You also decide whether the final truths state dates at all.

**Q3. Does customer contact stay direct?**
One camp says insight comes from being with the customer, and agent summaries lose the telling detail. Block bets the other way: customers' requests to agents set most of the roadmap.
*What changes:* "direct" adds a statement and a fixed share of every product person's week. "Through agents" makes B1.1's empathy depend on how good agent summaries are.

**Q4. Who decides between slates?**
You asked this yourself under B1. Spotify and Airbnb rank bets at the top. Block removes layers and pushes decisions down. Both are responses to the same capability.
*What changes:* ranking at the top keeps the whole coherent and puts a ceiling on speed. Deciding in teams is faster and needs someone else to hold the whole together. It shapes V14 and the portfolio scenario.

**Q5. What is deliberately left unrecorded?**
Your B5 records everything in market. Shopify and Atlassian record nearly everything and say that is how an organisation learns. A field experiment found that some privacy raised output by 10-15%.
*What changes:* "record everything" gives agents the most to work from and risks people holding back. A stated private space protects candour and leaves gaps in the record. It also sets what you agree with employee representatives.

## 6. Your answers (2026-11-22)

- **Q1. Freed time:** all of the above: bigger ambitions, relief and a stated mix. Fewer people is not a goal but a probable outcome, and fewer people in total brings coordination advantages of its own, even after the other benefits (organising better, using agents better) come first.
- **Q2. Horizon:** no dates. The truths describe the destination; timing stays out of them.
- **Q3. Customers:** both, as the situation dictates: whatever method gives the best insight for the task. Some agents may interview some customers. Data will answer questions interviews used to, and there will be more in-market tests. Speaking with human customers keeps lasting value for some questions, as direction and as evidence.
- **Q4. Who chooses:** it depends on the decision, and these are hypotheses. Some changes, such as fixing paper cuts, go out fully autonomously at small scale. Some need the sign-off of a single directly responsible owner. Shared context will shrink the area where people must decide at all.
- **Q5. What stays unrecorded:** record everything. Default to open, so agents and people have the most context.
