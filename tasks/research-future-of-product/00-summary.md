# The future of product: where this stands

An opinionated, evidence-based vision of where product development is heading, for all products (deterministic systems as much as AI products) and at the scale of a large regulated enterprise. It describes what becomes normal and what makes it possible. It is a vision, and nothing in it is a rule.

## Read in this order

1. [the-future-of-product-work.md](the-future-of-product-work.md): the whole thing in one file (the truths, the moves, the assets, the open tensions).
2. [04-truths.md](04-truths.md): the thesis and fifteen truths, with evidence links.
3. [07-product-system.md](07-product-system.md): the eleven moves of the product loop, the people, agents and artifacts, and what the scenarios have strained.
4. [08-assets.md](08-assets.md): a first draft of the agents, skills, artifacts and shared material that would support the loop.

Six scenarios test the vision (a rewards bet, a small-business payout idea carried by an engineer, a servicing journey and its steward, a fee change, an acquisition, and a newcomer learning judgment). They are kept out of the public repository.

## How it was built

- The project lead's own beliefs, broken into claims: [01-your-beliefs.md](01-your-beliefs.md).
- An evidence base from about 60 Lenny's Podcast episodes and newsletter posts (Nov 2025 to Nov 2026), this repository's teardowns of technology companies, and an open-web round on regulators, simulation, traceability and banks: [02-expert-view.md](02-expert-view.md), with source notes in `research/sources/` and theme evidence in [research/themes/research-future-of-product.md](../../research/themes/research-future-of-product.md).
- His beliefs set against that evidence, and his answers to the questions that were his: [03-convergence.md](03-convergence.md).
- [00-plan.md](00-plan.md) is the original plan, kept as history; the work has moved on from its later steps.

A first pass of the truths was rejected as too prescriptive and rigid. The current truths describe tendencies, say what would be observed, and keep the conditions separate from the vision. Final prose is written with a second model as editor; the research owns the claims.

## Working with Flight Deck

Flight Deck (a separate repository) is a workspace for product people that has to be useful today and move toward this vision. This research owns the what and why; Flight Deck owns the how and keeps its own notes on what serves each move. The two agreed three artifact names: statement of intent, solution design, decision record.

## Still open

The tensions between the scenarios and the truths are listed at the end of [07-product-system.md](07-product-system.md). The larger unknowns are in the truths' own open questions: where the limits for unreviewed release sit, how far simulated customers can be trusted, whether agents can settle integrations at scale, how product time is spent today, and how much portfolio-level ambition really rises.
