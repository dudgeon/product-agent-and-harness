Where Intercom’s agents sit in the product lifecycle, task by task

Same frame as the Stripe map: rows are tasks, not job titles. At Intercom engineers take on PM work, designers work in code, and nearly every employee has Claude Code. Each handoff is marked documented, inferred or unknown (9 documented, 2 inferred, 3 unknown). Almost every source is Intercom’s own, and every figure is self-reported.

Intent is captured downstream, at the pull request; upstream, research → intent → plan is left to people

  1. Downstream, the “why” is written down and checked. A hook forces a PR skill that asks why the change exists; review agents check the diff against that intent; a “what we built” doc feeds the support agent’s readiness gate at launch.
  2. No source describes research reaching intent or plan. The research team’s syntheses live in sessions, reports and a queryable interview skill; how they reach designers, PMs or engineers isn’t described.
  3. The plan isn’t an artifact. Engineers plan in 5–20 Claude Code turns with no formal spec; the plan stays in the session (one engineer’s account).
  4. Fin’s customer data isn’t shown feeding Intercom’s own roadmap.

Across people: like Stripe, Intercom shrinks the seam by collapsing roles. Where research, shaping and planning still sit with different people, the first durable statement of intent is the PR description, written after the plan.

What it means for our options

  • Re-capture intent at the PR, then verify it in review. Cheap and enforceable, but it comes after planning, so it can’t stop a well-built wrong thing.
  • One general harness plus a governed skills marketplace, not a set of named agents. The only named custom agents are the reviewer (Shrek) and Operator; Stripe, by contrast, names an agent per task.
  • Write-back after release is a first-class step. Support knowledge is a launch gate; harness gaps come back as skills.
  • The seam to build for is research → shaped intent, with the evidence attached. Intercom’s research team is heavily AI-assisted, but its handoff to the people who shape work isn’t on record.
