A product manager and a dependency agent

A notional session at a fictional insurer. Noor, a product manager on the mobile team, asks the agent to map a feature. The agent reads first, lists what it could not find, drafts the smallest questions, records every answer with owner, date and source, and re-plans when something moves. Six weeks later another team starts further along.

Illustrative: Lantern Mutual, the people, the counts and the dates are invented. The behaviours come from the research synthesis of 2026-10-28 (research-dependency-agent); the raw text is in 10-dialogue.md. Agents are hexagons; people are circles; the accent colour marks what a person contributed.
