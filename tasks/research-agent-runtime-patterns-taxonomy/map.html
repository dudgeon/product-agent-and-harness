Agent runtime pattern map

Companion to 01-synthesis.md (2026-10-24). Dimensions decide which pattern classes are available; user and product dimensions decide which pattern a job step is routed to; themes are what the research library already says about each pattern; PM jobs and Glass design choices show where each pattern has been observed. Hover a row or cell to trace links. A dot marks a link; it is a map of relationships, not a ranking. Nothing here recommends a winner.

Reading: P1 is the evidenced default for C1 and C2; P9 or P3 (and P2 when gates live in an own product) is the evidenced home for C3 and C4 once consistency and audit matter; P6 suits the read-only slice of C3; P8 matters most for C5 and hand-offs. The hybrid (general harness hands off to a bounded, gated job on entering a C3/C4 step) fits best on paper and has little direct evidence. No controlled comparison of a task agent against a skill in a general harness exists.
