The two mental models, and a third placement

Model A · finish-to-start relay

Boundary at the stage. The record in the feature repo is the through line; each agent reads it, works, stops. Closest to C5 to C8.

  • For: the record is the product; write once and re-read often is the low-loss condition; separation of duties; per-stage ownership and evals; harness-agnostic by construction.
  • Against: the seam loses reasoning unless the artifact carries it; wrong shape if mapping changes requirements often; something must start the next stage and the request leaves it unnamed; a repo file cannot notify, remind or receive an owner's answer.

Model B · through line

Boundary at the session. The person's harness stays in charge and calls sub-agents or primitives. Closest to C2 to C4.

  • For: one conversation holds the why; interleaving is free; lowest build; the vendors' 2026 default; the controlled studies favour one agent for sequential, interdependent reasoning.
  • Against: the session dies with the person and acts only as the person; what survives compaction is what was written to disk; subagents return summaries nobody checks; enterprise context must be served over MCP; single-user products without organisation controls; a 30-day transcript is not a record.

The record as the through line

Neither the harness nor a stage agent holds it; the intent record and its asks do, and any agent or owner attaches. The dominant shipped shape (C10 to C13), and the only one where an owner never in any session answers under their own identity.

  • Costs: who acts next and who is accountable when nobody does; a schema and a platform owner; not shown holding a product-definition flow for weeks.
  • Lean, with its reason: design the modules so the record is the through line and A versus B is a question about the person's surface. Would change if a record cannot carry reasoning well enough across sessions, or if the record platforms are unreachable from the harnesses product people use.
