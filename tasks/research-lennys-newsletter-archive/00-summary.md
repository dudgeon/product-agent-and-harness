# Findings: Lenny's Newsletter archive

Task: [research-lennys-newsletter-archive](../research-lennys-newsletter-archive.md). Written 2026-10-24.

**Scope.** Posts dated 2024-10-24 or later, per Geoff's cut-off. The publication has 1,281 posts; 717 earlier ones were not triaged. 564 posts were in the window and triaged ([01-triage.md](01-triage.md)): high 44, medium 121, low 278, none 121. All 165 high and medium posts were read: newsletter text in full; high-relevance podcasts in full transcript; medium-relevance podcasts as full show notes plus every transcript section on the project's topics (ranges in each note's "Could not verify"); seven already-filed posts were not re-read. 157 source notes were filed and one existing note was extended ([lenny-newsletter-tal-raviv-pm-agents](../../research/sources/lenny-newsletter-tal-raviv-pm-agents.md)). After reading, the final relevance counts are high 29, medium 95, low 33. By kind: 58 How I AI episodes, 45 Lenny's Podcast episodes, 25 newsletter and guest posts, 29 Community Wisdom digests.

**Access.** No paywall blocked anything: every post was read through Geoff's paid session. Podcast transcripts are Substack's own auto-transcription. 23 episodes had no transcript on Substack and YouTube captions could not be fetched, so those notes rest on the show notes and say so (list in section 8). Timestamps below are from the Substack transcripts.

**Read this first.** The project's deliverable is a menu of options by dimension with the key questions that would decide among them, to inform a resource request ([PROJECT.md](../../PROJECT.md), Decisions). This summary adds evidence to that menu. It does not recommend an option, and where it says a source "weakens" one, that is a statement about evidence, not a vote.

Evidence labels are the notes' frontmatter: **first-party** (the builder describing its own system), **vendor claim** (promotional, or a vendor about a customer), **independent** (no stake), **anecdote** (a practitioner thread, a guide, a second-hand account).

## 1. What this publication adds

Eight headline findings, in order of how much they add to what the library already held.

### 1.1 First-party operating models from the labs and large companies

Before this sweep the library's first-party operating-model evidence was Stripe, Ramp, Shopify and a few customer stories. Lenny's guests add the model vendors' own product teams and a dozen large companies describing, in their own words, how product work runs once coding is cheap.

- **Anthropic.** Growth team of about 40; five engineers with Claude Code produce like 15-20, so PM and design are "absolutely squeezed" (43:04); 60-80% of shipped work has no PRD; scheduled Cowork runs review 20-25 dashboards each morning and scan Slack weekly for cross-team misalignment ([amol-avasare](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-amol-avasare-anthropic-growth.md), first-party, April 2026). The Claude Code and Cowork manager says throughput rose 8x per engineer per quarter (Anthropic's figure) and the bottleneck became verification; her remedy is specs and "a statement of what good looks like" checked into the repo (15:00); six-month roadmaps gave way to monthly just-in-time planning ([fiona-fung](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-fiona-fung-after-coding-solved.md), first-party, June 2026). The head of Claude Code: Claude reviews 100% of pull requests with a human layer after it (16:52); Claude reads the feedback channel and telemetry and proposes fixes as PRs (17:54) ([boris-cherny](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-boris-cherny-after-coding.md), first-party, February 2026). The head of product: feature timelines from six months to a month, a week or a day; about 30-40 PMs; the PM writes about 10 evals when a feature needs defining; the stated cost is "sacrificing product consistency" (25:42) ([cat-wu](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-cat-wu-anthropic-product-team.md), first-party, April 2026). The CPO, mid-2025: about 90% of code AI-written, over half of PRs from Claude Code, merge queue re-architected, and "an upstream bottleneck, which is decision making and alignment" (14:25) ([mike-krieger](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-mike-krieger-anthropic-cpo.md), first-party, June 2025).
- **OpenAI.** API org: 95% of engineers use Codex daily, Codex reviews 100% of PRs, review time from 10-15 minutes to 2-3, engineers run 10-20 parallel threads, human attention on review "from 100% attention to 30%" ([sherwin-wu](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-sherwin-wu-openai-codex.md), first-party, February 2026). The Codex and ChatGPT Work product lead frames the job as "steering, not rowing", says staff share Codex threads as Slack screenshots, and wants persistent agents several people steer together ([tara-seshan](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-tara-seshan-persistent-coworkers.md), first-party, August 2026). The Codex lead says the limit is how fast humans validate and review agent output, and is sceptical of spec-driven development ([embiricos](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-embiricos-codex-human-bottleneck.md), vendor claim, December 2025). About 25 PMs in April 2025 and a "model maximalism" stance against scaffolding ([kevin-weil](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-kevin-weil-openai-cpo.md), first-party).
- **Gusto.** Five people (four engineers including the CTO, one designer, no PM) shipped a new product line in 10 weeks with no specs, Figma or Jira; scope was decided by opening review-ready PRs and keeping or closing them ("PR as the PRD"); median review time nine minutes because reviewers sat on an always-open Zoom; the CTO says it fits zero-to-one work and needed a co-founder's permission ([gusto-eddie-kim](../../research/sources/howiai-gusto-eddie-kim-cofounder.md), first-party, June 2026).
- **LinkedIn.** Heads of craft built trust, growth, research and analyst agents that critique a spec before build; replayed on the old Open to Work spec, the trust agent found holes the team had only caught later; giving agents all of Drive "failed miserably", curated gold examples worked; a Full Stack Builder title and ladder, AI fluency in reviews, and a claim that only the "cutting edge 5%" adopt unaided (38:24) ([tomer-cohen](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-tomer-cohen-full-stack-builder.md), first-party, December 2025; Cohen announced his departure in the episode).
- **Notion.** An `agent specs` folder in the repo; a dictated idea becomes a spec with code pointers and a verification section, Codex builds it, changes go to the spec and its history is the changelog; "Boxy" returns a PR with preview and self-verification screenshots about 10 minutes after a comment ([ryan-nystrom](../../research/sources/howiai-notion-ryan-nystrom-spec-driven.md), first-party, May 2026). The design team shares one prototype repo with a per-person folder, a shared CLAUDE.md and a check loop that lifts Figma-to-code from about 60% to about 80% right; 5-10 heavy users ([brian-lovin](../../research/sources/howiai-notion-brian-lovin-prototype-playground.md), first-party).
- **Meta, Netflix, Airtable, Google Labs, Whatnot.** Instagram's standard team goes from about 13 specialists to pods of four to six engineers plus one "product staff" ([adam-mosseri](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-adam-mosseri-product-staff.md), first-party, July 2026). Netflix moves to "paved paths" so many agents get consistent context and says it can no longer rely on tribal knowledge ([elizabeth-stone](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-elizabeth-stone-netflix-systems-thinkers.md), first-party). Airtable split into a "fast thinking" AI group shipping near-weekly and a "slow thinking" infrastructure group ([howie-liu](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-howie-liu-airtable-reorg.md), first-party). Google Labs project teams shrank from 5-7 people to 2-4 ([josh-woodward](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-josh-woodward-labs-team.md), first-party). Whatnot runs about 21-22 PMs on over $8B GMV, assigned to problems every six months ([tom-verrilli](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-tom-verrilli-whatnot-pm.md), first-party).
- **Warp, Sendbird, Webflow, Microsoft, Coinbase, Amplitude.** Warp's cloud "software factory" turns a Slack or Linear request into an issue, a PR and a computer-use QA video in public threads; dashboard shows 2,000+ PRs a month, 35 minutes from kickoff to PR and 3.5 hours from PR to first human review ([warp-zach-lloyd](../../research/sources/howiai-warp-zach-lloyd-software-factory.md), vendor claim, September 2026). Sendbird runs adoption as an internal product: "quests" with a risk rating, a compliant app template, a skills marketplace, a five-tier token dashboard kept out of reviews ([sendbird-john-kim](../../research/sources/howiai-sendbird-john-kim-adoption-playbook.md), first-party). Webflow's CPO pairs a prototype mandate with champions and a rewritten ladder ([webflow-rachel-wolan](../../research/sources/howiai-webflow-rachel-wolan-chief-of-staff.md), first-party). Microsoft: "every time you think you have one use case, you have really two", the feature and its governance (7:49) ([aparna-chennapragada](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-aparna-chennapragada-microsoft.md), first-party, May 2025); Dragon's acceptance rose from 30-60% to about 83% after expert annotation of 600,000 interactions ([asha-sharma](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-asha-sharma-loop-not-lane.md), vendor claim, August 2025). Coinbase (PR review time 150 to 15 hours across 1,000+ engineers) and Amplitude's internal assistant Moda are show-notes only and unverified ([coinbase](../../research/sources/howiai-coinbase-chintan-turakhia-ai-adoption.md); [amplitude-moda](../../research/sources/howiai-amplitude-wade-chambers-moda.md)).

### 1.2 The bottleneck moved, and the sources split on where

Every operating-model source agrees coding stopped being the constraint. They do not agree on what replaced it.

- **Upstream, to intent and judgment:** Krieger (14:25); Cat Wu, "the thing that becomes more valuable is deciding what to write" (18:16); Avasare; Simon Willison, an independent voice, says a spec that took "three weeks" now takes about "three hours" and the bottlenecks are "everywhere else" (21:27) ([simon-willison](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-simon-willison-state-of-ai.md), April 2026); a fully AI-enabled team where "the bottleneck becomes writing specs/stories fast enough" and an engineer shipped every feature in a call transcript to staging, half judged bloat ([engineering-outpaces-product](../../research/sources/lenny-community-wisdom-engineering-outpaces-product.md), anecdote); "Coding is no longer the bottleneck—clarity is" ([lazar-jovanovic](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-lazar-jovanovic-vibe-coder.md), vendor employee); "the judgment gap got exposed" ([ai-operating-models](../../research/sources/lenny-community-wisdom-ai-operating-models.md), anecdote); a large financial company where "the constraint becomes how fast someone can decide" ([discovery-big-org](../../research/sources/lenny-community-wisdom-discovery-big-org.md), anecdote).
- **Downstream, to review and verification:** the Codex lead ([embiricos](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-embiricos-codex-human-bottleneck.md)); Fung's "how do we know this works as intended"; Warp's 35 minutes to PR against 3.5 hours to review; Krieger's merge queue; a product-builder pilot where "PR reviews, design review, and QA are now the bottlenecks" ([product-engineers-pilot](../../research/sources/lenny-community-wisdom-product-engineers-pilot.md), anecdote); a healthcare product director: "The ceiling is verification ... I review more than I produce now" ([pms-ai-coding-tools](../../research/sources/lenny-community-wisdom-pms-ai-coding-tools.md), anecdote).
- **And the cost of steering is not zero.** "The cost of oversight is not zero" (45:20) ([molly-graham](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-molly-graham-delegating-to-ai.md)); Willison is "wiped out" by 11 a.m. running four agents; Lenny's 2026 survey of 5,332 tech workers (47% PMs) finds 82% say AI makes them better at the job but in their words that means more and faster, not higher quality; burnout rose from 44.7% to 55.7% in a year; one respondent: "I just follow Claude. I don't fully understand what I merge." ([tech-workers-2026-survey](../../research/sources/lenny-newsletter-tech-workers-2026-survey.md), independent, self-selected).

### 1.3 Evals as the spec, with its qualifications stated by its own advocates

- The clearest first-party statement: Anthropic's research PM team says "evals are the new PRDs", 30-40 prompts with golden answers per feature; vague "doesn't follow instructions" feedback turned out to be about 80% malformed JSON and now scores 99.9-100%; PRDs are still written, to align engineering, legal and safety ([dianne-penn](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-dianne-penn-evals-prds.md), first-party, July 2026).
- Practice at non-lab companies: Yelp's "golden conversations" written jointly by PM, design and engineering seed the evals and the prototype ([yelp-priya-badger](../../research/sources/howiai-yelp-priya-badger-golden-conversations.md), first-party); Gusto fixes agent bugs by writing a failing eval first; Microsoft's "prompt sets are the new PRDs"; a community thread keeps the PRD as alignment and adds a first-pass rubric: PRD, prototype, traces and evals as one loop ([prds-for-agents](../../research/sources/lenny-community-wisdom-prds-for-agents.md), anecdote).
- The qualifications: criteria emerge only after seeing outputs, so a complete spec up front is unrealistic and the PM is the single "benevolent dictator" of quality ([evals-hamel-shreya](../../research/sources/lenny-newsletter-evals-hamel-shreya.md); [hamel-shreya-evals](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-hamel-shreya-evals.md); [hamel-husain-error-analysis](../../research/sources/howiai-hamel-husain-error-analysis.md)); "error discovery" before metrics, with humans annotating the first traces ([advanced-evals-error-discovery](../../research/sources/lenny-newsletter-advanced-evals-error-discovery.md)); two enterprise practitioners say evals catch only known errors and call "PMs write evals, the new PRDs" a misreading ([reganti-badam](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-reganti-badam-ai-deployments.md), anecdote); their written framework logs every human take-back as eval data and climbs autonomy rungs only when calibration stops surprising ([cc-cd-ai-lifecycle](../../research/sources/lenny-newsletter-cc-cd-ai-lifecycle.md)).

### 1.4 The harness builders argue against scaffolding

The library's harness-over-model theme now has its strongest counter-evidence, from the people who build the harnesses.

- Cherny: give the model tools and a goal; scaffolding gains of "10%, 20%" get wiped out by the next model (1:03:43). OpenAI: "The models will eat your scaffolding", skills included ([sherwin-wu](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-sherwin-wu-openai-codex.md)). Weil's model maximalism (30:03). ChatGPT's head says "loops, graphs" are a passing phase and his agent teams shrink with each model ([sottiaux](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-sottiaux-chatgpt-dots.md), vendor claim, October 2026). One team dropped the BMAD spec-driven method as "no longer relevant" after Opus 4.6 and GPT-5.5 ([ai-operating-models](../../research/sources/lenny-community-wisdom-ai-operating-models.md)).
- Against that: Mozilla's bug-finding pipeline credits model and harness "sort of 50-50" and found bugs with non-frontier models ([mozilla-mythos](../../research/sources/howiai-mozilla-mythos-bug-harness.md), first-party); Gusto's production agent runs on Cloudflare Workers plus the Vercel AI SDK with "no other harness on top".
- Definitions the explainer can use: a harness as "a three-way group chat between an LLM, a human, and tools" ([ai-product-sense-part-1](../../research/sources/lenny-newsletter-ai-product-sense-part-1.md)); the anatomy of a persistent personal harness (gateway, agents with identity and workspace, cron plus heartbeat, self-installed skills) ([claire-vo-openclaw-guide](../../research/sources/lenny-newsletter-claire-vo-openclaw-guide.md)); a consultants' three-type taxonomy that describes Claude Code as a reasoning loop plus deterministic hooks plus isolated sub-agents ([three-agent-categories](../../research/sources/lenny-newsletter-three-agent-categories.md)). The one episode titled as a harness definition has no transcript ([claire-vo-agent-sdk-harness](../../research/sources/howiai-claire-vo-agent-sdk-harness.md)).

### 1.5 Context systems: curated beats open, and the PM is the "human API"

- LinkedIn: all of Drive hallucinated, gold examples worked. Netflix: paved paths over local stacks. OpenAI: agent failures are "usually a problem with context", so tribal knowledge goes into Markdown and skills. A Galileo field engineer answers from 15 repos plus Confluence and Slack over MCP, with a "customer quirks" page per air-gapped customer ([galileo-al-chen](../../research/sources/howiai-galileo-al-chen-codebase-context.md), anecdote). A healthcare director loads a 400+ line project file every session ([pms-ai-coding-tools](../../research/sources/lenny-community-wisdom-pms-ai-coding-tools.md)).
- The individual-PM pattern is everywhere and shared nowhere: Knowledge/, Tasks/, GOALS.md and AGENTS.md over a folder ([ai-product-sense-part-1](../../research/sources/lenny-newsletter-ai-product-sense-part-1.md)); Claude Code plus Obsidian ([teresa-torres](../../research/sources/howiai-teresa-torres-claude-code-pm.md)); one ChatGPT Project per initiative fed by manual exports ([pm-second-brain](../../research/sources/lenny-newsletter-pm-second-brain-chatgpt.md)); a Melio PM's plugin that runs 70-80% of his workday on local memory that cloud schedules cannot reach ([melio-daniel-blum](../../research/sources/howiai-melio-daniel-blum-pm-assistant.md), show notes plus public repo). Tal Raviv: "Today, I'm the human API between my copilot and everything" ([tal-raviv-ai-copilot](../../research/sources/lenny-newsletter-tal-raviv-ai-copilot.md)).
- Sharing attempts: a Git repo of skills and context with changes as PRs, dropped Obsidian for lack of control, non-technical staff overwhelmed by GitHub ([claude-context-sharing](../../research/sources/lenny-community-wisdom-claude-context-sharing.md)); a personal knowledge graph that turns to noise when shared unless a curated "golden" layer is kept separate ([knowledge-graph-mcp-cli](../../research/sources/lenny-community-wisdom-knowledge-graph-mcp-cli.md)); code as reality but not intent, so a human-owned intent doc agents may not edit ([architecture-docs-intent](../../research/sources/lenny-community-wisdom-architecture-docs-intent.md)).

### 1.6 Adoption is permission and mandate, not tooling

A new theme, [leadership-permission-mandate](../../research/THEMES.md#leadership-permission-mandate), filed from this sweep. Gusto's CTO: teams that skip specs "might get a slap on the wrist", and to scale he would forbid the old way (41:12). LinkedIn's 5% unaided. Webflow: "you can't get into a meeting with me without a prototype". Cisco made adoption top-down and non-negotiable ([jeetu-patel](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-jeetu-patel-cisco-ai.md)). Notion's head of product is surprised "how much prodding" it takes (36:52) ([max-schoening](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-max-schoening-agency.md)). Six companies' tactics, including Ramp publishing power users per team and Shopify peer-rating AI use in reviews ([25-ai-adoption-tactics](../../research/sources/lenny-newsletter-25-ai-adoption-tactics.md), second-hand). The counter: OpenAI says top-down mandates without bottom-up adoption are probably negative ROI and recommends a tiger team; a community thread says a mandate with a choice of tools worked and a mandate from outside the team did not ([engineers-ai-adoption](../../research/sources/lenny-community-wisdom-engineers-ai-adoption.md)). Every's consulting finds the CEO's own daily use the best predictor ([dan-shipper-every-ai-native](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-dan-shipper-every-ai-native.md)).

### 1.7 Shared, persistent sessions: strong demand, no supply

- Demand: OpenAI staff share Codex threads as screenshots; a 100+ person non-engineering team told to go AI-first built its own shared context space and agent UI ([vibe-coding-team-sport](../../research/sources/lenny-community-wisdom-vibe-coding-team-sport.md)); "Most value today accrues to individual power users, not teams", and nobody shares how they got to an artifact ([fragmented-ai-usage](../../research/sources/lenny-community-wisdom-fragmented-ai-usage.md)); a project "shared brain" updated from transcripts, PRs and Slack that agents run on ([ai-operating-models](../../research/sources/lenny-community-wisdom-ai-operating-models.md)); a PM's one shared Claude Project per initiative, mid-2025 ([ai-team-velocity](../../research/sources/lenny-community-wisdom-ai-team-velocity.md)).
- Supply: Anthropic's newest personal agent is "clearly an agent for single player use" (14:06, October 2026) ([claire-vo-claude-duet-review](../../research/sources/howiai-claire-vo-claude-duet-review.md)); Microsoft's CPO calls today's experiences "very single player" (55:06); Cognition says Slack threads work as multiplayer sessions and stickiness comes from several people in the same sessions ([scott-wu-inside-devin](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-scott-wu-inside-devin.md), vendor claim); SpaceXAI's Grok Bot gives every bot its own persistent cloud computer and credentials ([roman-ugarte](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-roman-ugarte-grok-bot.md), vendor claim); ChatGPT Sites shows each viewer only their own data through their own connector grants ([openai-korevec](../../research/sources/howiai-openai-korevec-chatgpt-sites.md), vendor claim); Meta's Muse keeps one persistent main chat plus side chats and a three-layer status display down to raw calls for audit ([meta-muse](../../research/sources/howiai-meta-muse-alex-cornell.md)).

### 1.8 Prototype-as-spec is contested by the practitioners who use it

For: Airtable ("prototypes over decks"; PRD claims about agent behaviour "are just words"), Figma Slides shipped on annotated design files with no PRD ([figma-slides](../../research/sources/lenny-newsletter-figma-slides-ship-like-startup.md)), Microsoft's "demos before memos". Against or qualified: a prototype cannot carry the why, constraints, edge cases or non-functional requirements ([prototyping-vs-prds](../../research/sources/lenny-community-wisdom-prototyping-vs-prds.md)); the realistic hand-off is prototype plus a thin written delta ([prototypes-release-comms](../../research/sources/lenny-community-wisdom-prototypes-release-comms.md)); prototype code is "mostly useless" to engineering and delivery still takes 2-6 weeks ([colin-matthews-team-prototyping](../../research/sources/lenny-newsletter-colin-matthews-team-prototyping.md)); a Claude Design prototype handed to Claude Code came back at 60-70% fidelity because nobody gave the coding agent a way to check its work ([claude-design-handoff](../../research/sources/lenny-community-wisdom-claude-design-handoff.md)); quasi-PMs' prototypes are "clickable PRDs" engineering will not own ([non-pms-shipping](../../research/sources/lenny-community-wisdom-non-pms-shipping.md)); the Codex lead says polish no longer signals maturity ([ambrosino](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-ambrosino-codex-product-work.md)); Shape Up's shaping, down to fewer than 10 moving parts with an engineer opening the code first, is close to a definition of ready intent, and its most common failure is "not enough detail" ([ryan-singer](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-ryan-singer-shape-up.md)).

## 2. By dimension

Full rows, examples and key questions are in the contributions file: [options/contributions/research-lennys-newsletter-archive.md](../../options/contributions/research-lennys-newsletter-archive.md). Register: [DIMENSIONS.md](../../options/DIMENSIONS.md). Each heading links to the matching section of the contributions file.

**[intent-shaping-and-spec](../../options/contributions/research-lennys-newsletter-archive.md)** (the most touched dimension)
- New spec standards with real examples: eval set as the spec with the PRD kept for alignment (Penn, Yelp); a spec with a verification contract in the repo (Notion); a typed JSON intent model with `open_questions[]` carried as data, in a large financial company ([discovery-big-org](../../research/sources/lenny-community-wisdom-discovery-big-org.md)); goal-with-verification as the hand-off (OpenAI's six-part goal format, [claire-vo-codex-goal](../../research/sources/howiai-claire-vo-codex-goal.md)); a layered Markdown PRD set compiled to a task file (Jovanovic); a data schema or sample data as the most agent-ready part of a spec ([ravi-mehta](../../research/sources/howiai-ravi-mehta-data-driven-prototyping.md)); a shaped pitch within an appetite (Singer); PR as the PRD (Gusto).
- New shaping modes: persona critique agents owned by heads of craft (LinkedIn); an AI-interviewed PRD where the agent asks 17 questions ([how-i-built-lennyrpg](../../research/sources/lenny-newsletter-how-i-built-lennyrpg.md)); a requirements file plus a dependency-check agent before build ([pms-ai-coding-tools](../../research/sources/lenny-community-wisdom-pms-ai-coding-tools.md)); short synchronous shaping then async hand-off (Cognition's "tasks, not problems"; the operating-models huddle).
- Counter-evidence: spec-driven development doubted by the Codex lead; "PRDs are dead" rejected by Penn, Ambrosino and the PRDs-for-agents thread.

**[review-and-autonomy](../../options/contributions/research-lennys-newsletter-archive.md)**
- A staged autonomy ladder (route, suggest, resolve) with logged human edits (CC/CD; Reganti and Badam's insurance pre-authorization and bank underwriting examples); autonomy earned per connector action with allow, block or ask and draft-only defaults ([jj-englert-cowork-101](../../research/sources/howiai-jj-englert-cowork-101.md); Webflow's never-send Gmail).
- Guardrails as commit checks instead of gate meetings (discovery-big-org); an encoded reviewer pre-pass that takes drafts "0 to 80" ([hilary-gridley](../../research/sources/lenny-newsletter-hilary-gridley-supermanager.md)); cross-model review against plan artifacts ([tenex-flowy](../../research/sources/howiai-tenex-cj-hess-flowy.md); [zevi-arnovitz](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-zevi-arnovitz-cursor-pm.md)); separated agent roles (a QA agent that cannot edit code) for non-engineer changes ([cursor-for-pm-work](../../research/sources/lenny-community-wisdom-cursor-for-pm-work.md)); behavioural testing by simulated users replacing code review at StrongDM, relayed by Willison, which a regulated firm cannot copy.
- The human-review-every-time option is now paired with its cost: Graham, the 2026 survey, Sherwin Wu's 100% to 30% attention, and a Claude-assisted PR that leaked source after "two layers of human review" (Cat Wu, 12:15).

**[adoption-model](../../options/contributions/research-lennys-newsletter-archive.md)**
- Explicit permission or prohibition of the old way for pilot teams (Gusto); an early-adopter lab beside a careful rollout (Microsoft Frontier); a fast/slow org split (Airtable); an internal labs team as pilot ground (Google); builder days (Webflow); a quest marketplace with a paved road to production (Sendbird); a weekly 10-minute demo slot (a consultant's measured driver, method not given, [driving-ai-adoption](../../research/sources/lenny-community-wisdom-driving-ai-adoption.md)); a power user's harness distributed as an onboarding plugin (Melio); a dedicated AI operations lead (Every); teams decide what stays human, with a veto.

**[pm-agent-evaluation](../../options/contributions/research-lennys-newsletter-archive.md)**
- Error discovery before metrics and trace error analysis then binary, judge-validated evals (Husain and Shankar); rework rate as the KPI, not speed ([quarterly-planning](../../research/sources/lenny-community-wisdom-quarterly-planning.md)); human interactions per unit of work, cost per PR and replaying past tasks under new configurations (Warp); experiment volume times quality (Anthropic growth); minimum viable quality bars plus a cost envelope ([ai-product-sense-part-2](../../research/sources/lenny-newsletter-ai-product-sense-part-2.md)).
