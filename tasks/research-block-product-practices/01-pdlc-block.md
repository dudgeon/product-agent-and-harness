## Why this map is anchored on tasks, not job families

Block is reorganising so that job titles predict less and less who does a task:
- **One owner per arc.** Square's head of product describes a DRI who owns a product "from idea to kind of scaling it", including go-to-market, and says the best ideation happens "in a design and engineering fashion" ([Avé](../../research/sources/productschool-willem-ave-square-org-design-ai-agents.md), first-party, 2026-08-19). Block's operating essay keeps three roles: IC, DRI and player-coach. There is no PM among them ([From Hierarchy to Intelligence](../../research/sources/block-from-hierarchy-to-intelligence.md), first-party, 2026-03-31).
- **PMs and designers write code.** "All of our product managers are shipping PRs" ([Jennings](../../research/sources/a16z-owen-jennings-block-all-in-on-ai.md), first-party, 2026-04-01). Over 90% of Cash App designers ship production PRs ([Worboys](../../research/sources/dive-club-cam-worboys-ai-native-design-org.md), first-party, 2026-03-05). The one open Square PM role asks for AI prototypes and PRs ([job post](../../research/sources/block-careers-staff-pm-post-checkout.md), 2026-10-06).
- **Teams shrank.** Feature teams of about 14 became squads of 1-6 that rotate between products ([Jennings](../../research/sources/a16z-owen-jennings-block-all-in-on-ai.md)). Block went from over 10,000 people to just under 6,000 ([Dorsey](../../research/sources/x-jack-block-smaller-note-2026-02-26.md), 2026-02-26).

So each row is a **task**, and "who does it" lists the roles the sources show doing it.

## The short answer: where does context break between research, intent and plan, across people?

**No Block source describes research moving into a plan across people. Block's strategy is to stop needing that handoff, not to build it.**

1. **Research happens in a person's own goose session.** That session is local, desktop or CLI, and runs against MCP servers: the governed-metrics Data MCP, Query Expert, Snowflake, Looker, Google Drive ([data foundation](../../research/sources/block-eng-data-foundation-automated-analytics.md); [CTO](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-block-dhanji-goose.md)). Recipes and skills are shared, but they carry method, not context ([recipes](../../research/sources/goose-docs-recipes.md); [skills](../../research/sources/block-eng-3-principles-agent-skills.md)). No filed source shows a goose session, or its findings, being handed to another person.
2. **The one documented shared handoff is a ticket or a Slack thread into Builderbot.** Anyone tags @builderbot with "a short description", or it picks up a Linear or Jira ticket. Builderbot then researches, plans, codes, opens the PR and iterates while teammates steer in the thread ([Builderbot](../../research/sources/block-builderbot-rollout.md), vendor claim, 2026-06-17). It is told what to build, not why.
3. **Block bridges the gap by role collapse.** The DRI owns idea to scale. PMs and designers build the prototype and often the PR themselves, and prototypes replace decks and documents in meetings ([Dorsey on Sequoia](../../research/sources/sequoia-dorsey-every-company-mini-agi.md); [Worboys](../../research/sources/dive-club-cam-worboys-ai-native-design-org.md)). The essay's company and customer "world model" would make the backlog system-generated: "that failure signal is the future roadmap". But this is a published plan that "will likely break before [it works]", not a system shown in use ([essay](../../research/sources/block-from-hierarchy-to-intelligence.md)).

**What the task view adds.** Block is the furthest case of the pattern seen at Stripe and Shopify: it removes the people boundary rather than carrying context across it. Stripe does this with engineers who wear the "product hat". Shopify does it with one public thread. Block does it with one owner per arc, squads of 1-6, and PMs who ship code. For a target org that keeps a PM/engineering split and regulated review, Block shows what this choice costs. The cut fell hardest on development, but compliance was "basically not touched" ([Jennings](../../research/sources/a16z-owen-jennings-block-all-in-on-ai.md)). Staff say every change still needs a human approver, and that about 95% of AI changes needed edits ([Guardian](../../research/sources/guardian-block-workers-ai-cant-do-their-jobs.md), independent, 2026-03-08). The seam to fund is the same: research → intent → plan across people. Block offers no artifact for it.

## What it means for our options

- **A general harness for everyone, with stage agents built as shared services.** Every employee gets goose, one general agent with a curated MCP bundle. The heavy, shared jobs became services on top of it: Builderbot for code and review, Query Expert and the Data MCP for data. That supports pairing a per-person general harness with a few shared stage services. It favours neither a pure "one agent, many skills" design nor "one agent per task" ([agent-unit, in work-structure](../../options/DIMENSIONS.md#work-structure-how-structured-the-work-is-what-agent-shape-fits-and-the-unit-of-an-agent)).
- **Owning the harness and data, not the model.** "To us the models are interchangeable", while the harness, data and distribution are Block's ([Q2 2026 letter](../../research/sources/block-q2-2026-shareholder-letter.md)). Engineering still buys seven coding harnesses and generates their configs from one rule set. That is a third option between owning one harness and buying one ([loop-source](../../options/DIMENSIONS.md#loop-source-who-supplies-the-agent-loop); [harness-layering](../../options/DIMENSIONS.md#harness-layering-is-there-a-layer-above-the-vendor-harnesses)).
- **Role collapse removes the seam only where an org can collapse roles.** The bridge Block uses (one owner per arc, PMs shipping PRs, prototypes in place of specs) depends on two things: tiny squads, and a regulator-facing human gate kept at push. Where those conditions don't hold, the research → intent → plan handoff across people stays open, and Block offers no artifact for it ([intent-shaping-and-spec](../../options/DIMENSIONS.md#intent-shaping-and-spec-intent-shaping-mode-and-spec-standard); [session-persistence-sharing](../../options/DIMENSIONS.md#session-persistence-sharing-session-persistence-sharing-and-handoff)).
- **The Slack thread is the shared session.** Builderbot, like Shopify's River, works in a thread that several people steer. goose itself stays local and single-user ([agent-interface](../../options/DIMENSIONS.md#agent-interface-agent-interface-own-app-chat-or-embedded)).
