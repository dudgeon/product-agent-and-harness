# Spotify's product lifecycle, mapped by task: people, agents and handoffs

Date: 2026-10-24. Worker draft (task research-airbnb-spotify-product-practices). This uses the same frame as the [Stripe map](../competitive-teardown-stripe-knowledge-agents/02-pdlc-agent-ecosystem-map.md): rows are tasks, not job titles. Each handoff is marked:
- **documented**: a Spotify source says it happens;
- **inferred**: consistent with the sources but not stated;
- **unknown**: no source covers it.

It is built only from filed notes in `../../research/sources/`. Visual version: [02-pdlc-spotify.html](02-pdlc-spotify.html), generated by `02-pdlc-spotify.build.py` with the shared renderer `mapkit.py`.

**Evidence caveat.** Most sources are Spotify's own: Engineering and Portal blogs, earnings calls, and leaders on podcasts. Spotify now sells its internal tools (Portal, Confidence, Xirp, all branded "Spotify Technology" since Oct 2026), so its posts about them double as marketing. Anthropic's pages on Spotify are vendor claims.

Independent sources:
- InfoQ coverage of the QCon talk.
- TechCrunch and heise on the "no code since December" claim.
- An ex-PM's critique of the squad model (Jeremiah Lee, 2020).
- A 2016 talk by a Spotify chapter lead, *Don't Copy the Spotify Model*, filed as independent.

Adoption figures conflict across Spotify's own sources: 73%, 76% and 60% (see Could not verify).

## Why tasks, not job titles, at Spotify

- **Engineers supervise; PMs and designers build.**
  - Senior engineers "only generate code and supervise it" ([Q4 2025 call](../../research/sources/spotify-q4-2025-earnings-call.md), first-party, Feb 2026).
  - PMs propose small changes through Honk without cloning repos ([Honk part 1](../../research/sources/spotify-engineering-honk-part-1-1500-prs.md), Nov 2025).
  - PMs and designers build clickable prototypes in Cursor against MCP-wrapped internal services ([Invest Like the Best](../../research/sources/colossus-ilb-gustav-soderstrom-how-spotify-thinks.md), May 2025).
  - A principal PM built and published his own Claude Code plugin ([Portal token post](../../research/sources/spotify-engineering-portal-claude-code-token-usage.md), Sep 2026).
- **Everyone up to the co-CEOs prototypes** in the real mobile apps and shares the results through an internal app store ([office hours](../../research/sources/claude-customers-spotify-office-hours.md), vendor claim, early 2026).
- **Evaluation is split by role.** In Premium R&D's Scorecard:
  - Desirable and Usable belong to the researcher;
  - Viable belongs to the PM;
  - Feasible belongs to the engineering manager;
  - Unified belongs to the designer.

  The PM is one evaluator among five ([Scorecards](../../research/sources/spotify-design-scorecards-alignment.md), first-party, 2021).

**How the operating model moved.**
1. **2012: autonomous squads.** Squads of fewer than eight people, each a "mini-startup". The product owner owned "what" and the chapter lead owned "how" ([Kniberg and Ivarsson](../../research/sources/kniberg-ivarsson-scaling-agile-spotify.md), first-party).
2. **2014–16: Spotify Rhythm.** Bets were argued with DIBB (data, insights, beliefs, bets) and stack-ranked on open boards ([Rhythm](../../research/sources/kniberg-spotify-rhythm.md)).
3. **Critics, then the turn.** Critics said the alignment half of the model "was never written" ([Lee](../../research/sources/jeremiahlee-failed-squad-goals.md), independent, 2020).
   - In 2023 Spotify centralised product and engineering under Gustav Söderström because teams spent "far too much time syncing on slightly different strategies" ([Jan 2023 org changes](../../research/sources/spotify-newsroom-january-2023-org-changes.md)).
   - Spotify "moved away" from squads: autonomy at the leaves "produces heat", so it now sits at VP level, and teams are 2–3x larger ([Lenny's Podcast](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-gustav-soderstrom-scaling-spotify.md), May 2023).
4. **2026: co-CEOs.** Söderström and Alex Norström became co-CEOs, with Daniel Ek as executive chairman ([newsroom](../../research/sources/spotify-newsroom-ek-executive-chairman.md), Sep 2025).

## The short answer: where context breaks between research, intent and plan

**Spotify's context layer carries systems, not reasons.**

1. **Agents get rich context about systems.**
   - The Backstage/Portal catalog records owners, lineage, standards and TechDocs. It also holds skills and governance rules as owned `AiResource` entities.
   - Agents reach the catalog over MCP ([Backstage AI docs](../../research/sources/backstage-docs-ai.md); [Portal MCP](../../research/sources/spotify-portal-docs-mcp.md)).
   - Claude uses it to find owners, read docs and message teams ([Coding Is No Longer the Constraint](../../research/sources/spotify-engineering-coding-no-longer-constraint.md), Jun 2026).
   - Xirp sessions start with catalog context, and their transcripts flow back to Portal ([Xirp](../../research/sources/spotify-portal-blog-introducing-xirp.md), Aug 2026).
2. **The "why" behind a bet travels in people.**
   - About 14 VPs pitch bets "as if … a VC", and the co-CEOs stack-rank 30–50 of them every six months.
   - VPs must explain the details themselves in a weekly three-hour E-Team ([Invest Like the Best](../../research/sources/colossus-ilb-gustav-soderstrom-how-spotify-thinks.md), ~16:26 and ~23:20; read via a third-party transcript).
   - No source shows a written evidence brief in today's bets process.
3. **Spotify built evidence-carrying intent artifacts, then kept them optional and off the platform.**
   - **The DIBB two-page bet brief** (2016) carried sponsor, success metrics and related bets ([Rhythm](../../research/sources/kniberg-spotify-rhythm.md)).
   - **The Thoughtful Execution tree** (2020) runs goal → data and insights → opportunities → hypotheses → solutions → learnings ([Thoughtful Execution](../../research/sources/spotify-design-thoughtful-execution-framework.md)).
   - **The Understand It Brief and the Scorecard** (2021) came next ([Scorecards](../../research/sources/spotify-design-scorecards-alignment.md)).
   - All of these live in Google Docs, Figma or MURAL, and none is linked into Backstage, the code or the bets record. The Thoughtful Execution page now returns 404.
4. **The engineering intent artifact is strong; the product one is missing.**
   - A fleet change is a Git-versioned prompt with the end state, preconditions, examples and "done" defined as tests ([Honk part 2](../../research/sources/spotify-engineering-honk-part-2-context-engineering.md); [Agent SDK story](../../research/sources/claude-customers-spotify-agent-sdk.md)).
   - Linking PRs to work items, A/B tests and user value is "what we're trying to build right now" ([office hours](../../research/sources/claude-customers-spotify-office-hours.md)).

**Across people.** Since 2023 Spotify has put the research → intent → plan seam at the VP level. The VP is the carrier, and the six-month bets board is the commitment. The teams that research, shape and build sit below it. Leadership says plans are now the constraint: "When you have productivity on tap, what you need to have are very good plans" ([Q4 2025 call](../../research/sources/spotify-q4-2025-earnings-call.md)). But its tooling goes into systems context (Portal, Xirp) and verification, not into product rationale.

**Compared with the others.**
- **Like Atlassian**, Spotify has strong shared objects (the catalog), and its gap sits upstream of the first shaped artifact.
- **Unlike Atlassian**, its objects describe systems, not work: there is no idea, spec or goal object.
- **Like Airbnb**, a senior person carries the evidence into the plan. At Spotify that person is a pitching VP; at Airbnb it is the reviewing CEO.

## What it means for our options

- **The best-built context layer in the eight maps, and it stops at the code** ([context-and-memory](../../options/DIMENSIONS.md#context-and-memory-context-and-memory); [artifact-and-project-structure](../../options/DIMENSIONS.md#artifact-and-project-structure-how-pm-artifacts-and-projects-are-structured-stored-and-found)).
  - Catalog, lineage, standards and skills are owned entities that agents can query.
  - Product rationale is not one of them: why this bet, and on what evidence.
  - The obvious extension is a bet or brief as a catalog kind, with an owner and a lifecycle. No source shows anyone building it.
- **Spotify's own history is a menu of intent artifacts, and a warning** ([intent-shaping-and-spec](../../options/DIMENSIONS.md#intent-shaping-and-spec-intent-shaping-mode-and-spec-standard)).
  - The menu: DIBB brief, Thoughtful Execution tree, Understand It Brief, Scorecard.
  - The warning: all were optional "reminders" in an autonomy culture, and at least one has lapsed. A spec standard an agent enforces would meet the same resistance unless the agent drafts it from linked data.
- **Engineering intent is already agent-ready.** End state, preconditions, examples and done-as-tests, versioned in Git. This is strong evidence for the "spec with an executable verification contract" option.
- **Verification, not generation, is now the limit** ([review-and-autonomy](../../options/DIMENSIONS.md#review-and-autonomy-review-and-autonomy)).
  - Merged changes went from about 8,100 to about 17,000 (August 2025 vs August 2026).
  - Spotify: "The next constraint became our ability to verify it."
  - Autonomy is earned by deterministic checks; the LLM judge was dropped.
- **Many surfaces, one context layer** ([agent-interface](../../options/DIMENSIONS.md#agent-interface-agent-interface-own-app-chat-or-embedded)).
  - Slack is the front door for Honk.
  - AiKA is both an MCP client and an MCP server.
  - Xirp is a multi-session workspace across vendor agents.
  - For big APIs, a CLI is preferred over MCP.
- **Adoption came from standardisation done for humans first, plus a top-down mandate** ([adoption-model](../../options/DIMENSIONS.md#adoption-model-adoption-and-enablement-model)).
  - Leadership: "We're retooling the entire company."
  - Spotify claims 99%+ weekly AI use by engineers. Golden technologies and Backstage predate the agents and make them work.

Key questions (in the [contributions file](../../options/contributions/research-airbnb-spotify-product-practices.md)):
1. Should a bet or brief become an owned entity in the same catalog agents already read, so product rationale has an owner, a lifecycle and health signals like Vedder's clusters? — *answered by:* a prototype catalog kind; a Portal or Backstage maintainer; a pilot with one product area.
2. Where research, intent and plan are split across levels (team research, VP bet, executive ranking), what must the bet carry for an agent downstream to use it? — *answered by:* reviewing 10 past bets against the PRs that served them; interviews with VPs who pitch.

## Cross-ecosystem notes (for the comparison worker)

- **Archetype: "systems context layer".** Spotify is the clearest case of an org making itself legible to agents through a software catalog: owners, lineage, standards, TechDocs, skills and MCP servers as entities. The same layer is now sold as Portal, and Xirp adds vendor-neutral sessions.
- **The seam sits at the VP level**, not between people at the same level. Research and shaping happen in teams; the bet is pitched by a VP and ranked by the co-CEOs; agents work below that. Nothing links a bet to its prompts, PRs and experiments, and Spotify says it is building that link.
- **The strongest experiment record of the eight.** Confidence's ship rule labels every test ship, abort or iterate, with reasons, so "operate and learn" yields a structured, machine-readable decision record. The bridge from that record to the next bet is not described.
- **Verification capacity** is named first-party as the new constraint. That is new evidence for the theme `verification-capacity-gap`.
- **Agents present:** Vedder and AiKA (research), prototyping in Cursor and Claude Code (shape), Fleetshift plus lineage (plan, deterministic), Honk and Xirp (code), verifiers (review), auto-merge (release), Confidence (learn). No agent sets the goal, and none carries the bet.

## Could not verify

- Adoption figures conflict:
  - 99%+ weekly AI use and PR frequency up 76% (Spotify blog, Jun 2026);
  - 96% and 60% (Code with Claude session page);
  - 73% of PRs AI-authored (Anthropic office hours, probably Jan–Feb 2026).
- The reason the LLM judge was dropped differs by source: false positives (QCon), models catching up (office hours), or "too rigid" (InfoQ).
- The 2025 bets-process details (14 VPs, 44 bets, 2.5 days) come from a third-party machine transcript. The original is login-walled.
- No source from 2024 to 2026 describes PM role, ladder or ratio changes after the 2023 layoffs. It is also unconfirmed whether "tribes" or "missions" still exist.
- Whether the Thoughtful Execution tree, Understand It Brief or Scorecard are still in use: the 2020 page now returns 404.
- There is no Spotify source on AI in user research or customer interviews, and no source on prototype-to-ship conversion.
- AiKA's "47% faster support resolution" and "6,000 hours saved" figures are undated marketing.
