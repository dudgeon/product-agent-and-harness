**Evidence caveat.** Almost every source is Airbnb's own: the CEO in interviews and on earnings calls, the CTO, the Tech Blog, and a platform-team talk hosted by DX, a vendor. Independent sources are critiques of the operating model (Cutler and Perri, Paul Graham, Semafor, Fortune). None checks the AI numbers. One ex-insider account (Vlad Loktev, anecdote) is the only first-hand view from below the CEO.

## Why tasks, not job titles, at Airbnb

- **The classic PM role is gone, split four ways.** In 2020 Airbnb "got rid of the classic product management function" and "combined product management with product marketing" ([Config 2023](../../research/sources/figma-config-2023-brian-chesky.md), first-party, Jun 2023).
  - Senior PMs became product marketers ("product management minus program management plus some outbound marketing").
  - Most other PMs became program managers ([Decoder 2024](../../research/sources/verge-decoder-chesky-founder-mode-2024.md), first-party, Oct 2024).
  - A business counterpart to each product marketer (business model, pricing, countries) sits in the Chief Business Officer's organisation (same source).
- **The CEO does product tasks.** Chesky calls himself "chief editor" and reviews every project on a 1–12 week cadence ([Lenny's Podcast](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-brian-chesky-new-playbook.md), first-party, Nov 2023). A design lead says he is "always in the pixels" ([Teo Connor](../../research/sources/designweek-teo-connor-new-airbnb-app.md), first-party, May 2025).
- **Engineers, managers and non-engineers build.**
  - Agentic-tool users are about twice the ~2,000 active developers. A product lead built an internal browser in three days instead of writing a PRD ([DX talk](../../research/sources/dx-airbnb-beyond-the-cli-airchat.md), first-party, Jun 2026).
  - Design and engineering managers are back to coding ([Q1 2026 letter](../../research/sources/airbnb-earnings-q1-2026.md)).
  - The CTO expects the engineer–product line to blur ([Al-Dahle essay](../../research/sources/medium-al-dahle-future-of-software-engineering.md), Feb 2026).

So each row below is a task, and "who does it" lists the roles the sources show doing it.

## The short answer: where context breaks between research, intent and plan

**The prototype carries intent forward. The evidence and the decisions stay with people.**

1. **Intent starts as a story and a short brief.**
   - Product marketing works on the story or press release "months ahead" with comms; "the story will often dictate the product" ([Lenny's Podcast](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-brian-chesky-new-playbook.md) 14:47).
   - Since 2026 the old chain (requirements, then Figma, then engineering, then test) has become a short "concept brief" followed by many high-fidelity prototypes. An AirChat app called Pascal drafts the brief from market research, the roadmap and business objectives ([DX talk](../../research/sources/dx-airbnb-beyond-the-cli-airchat.md); [Latent Space](../../research/sources/latent-space-airbnb-al-dahle-inside-out-ai.md), Oct 2026).
   - No source says whether the brief cites its evidence.
2. **Prototypes replace specs, and Airbnb names the drift itself.**
   - After prototyping, the brief isn't updated and reviews happen out of order.
   - A proposed "AI project coordinator" to keep brief, prototypes and Jira in sync is "early days" ([DX talk](../../research/sources/dx-airbnb-beyond-the-cli-airchat.md)). This is the clearest first-party statement in the library of the intent → plan break.
3. **Decisions are made in reviews and kept unwritten on purpose.**
   - Every project is reviewed every 1, 2, 4, 8 or 12 weeks, and the head program manager scores it green, yellow or red ([Lenny's Podcast](../../research/sources/lenny-podcast-brian-chesky-new-playbook.md) 28:39).
   - There is no written list of decision principles. People learn them by spending 10–20 hours a week in the room, as "one shared consciousness" ([Decoder 2024](../../research/sources/verge-decoder-chesky-founder-mode-2024.md)).
   - In 2016 the Knowledge Repo post already named "the synthesis of the work that is done by individuals" as unsolved ([Knowledge Repo](../../research/sources/airbnb-tech-scaling-knowledge-knowledge-repo.md)).
4. **Where evidence does travel by artifact, it travels as evals.** For AI features:
   - Insight Miner's taxonomy and prevalence of support situations became datasets and evals before the 2025 support launch ([Insight Miner](../../research/sources/airbnb-tech-insight-miner-agent-harness.md), Sep 2026).
   - About 500 seed queries from user research ground the AI-search training data ([CASTLE](../../research/sources/airbnb-tech-castle-nl-search.md), Oct 2026).
   - A decision-maker fixes goals and gates up front, and PMs label golden sets ([eval-driven development](../../research/sources/airbnb-tech-eval-driven-development.md), Jul 2026).

**Across people.** Airbnb split the PM's tasks across more people than Stripe or Intercom did: a product marketer (story), program manager (plan), designer (prototype) and business lead (model). So research → intent → plan crosses people by design. The bridge is not an artifact but a person, the CEO, present in every review. Everest, an LLM-built org context graph across the codebase ([Latent Space](../../research/sources/latent-space-airbnb-al-dahle-inside-out-ai.md)), is the candidate machine carrier. Its inputs are not published, and it is described as code- and project-oriented.

**Compared with the others.**
- Stripe and Intercom shrink the seam by collapsing roles.
- Ramp and Shopify carry it in shared sessions or threads.
- Airbnb widened the role split, then put the CEO in the gap.

It is the most person-dependent bridge of the eight maps, and the least transferable.

## What it means for our options

- **The PM's tasks can be split four ways and still need one carrier** ([target-user](../../options/DIMENSIONS.md#target-user-which-users-and-personas-we-serve-first-including-for-shared-agents); [work-structure](../../options/DIMENSIONS.md#work-structure-how-structured-the-work-is-what-agent-shape-fits-and-the-unit-of-an-agent)).
  - At Airbnb a "PM agent" would serve a product marketer, a program manager, a designer and a business lead.
  - That is evidence for a task agent per row over a persona agent.
- **The intent bundle is story + short brief + prototypes + evals, not a PRD** ([intent-shaping-and-spec](../../options/DIMENSIONS.md#intent-shaping-and-spec-intent-shaping-mode-and-spec-standard)).
  - It supports the prototype-first and eval-as-spec options.
  - The named open need is keeping brief, prototype and tickets in sync after prototyping changes the plan.
- **The review is the decision point, and nothing records it** ([review-and-autonomy](../../options/DIMENSIONS.md#review-and-autonomy-review-and-autonomy); [artifact-and-project-structure](../../options/DIMENSIONS.md#artifact-and-project-structure-how-pm-artifacts-and-projects-are-structured-stored-and-found)).
  - An agent that prepares each review (Chesky's weekly "semi-assembly") and records what was decided and why would fill a real gap.
  - It would work against a culture that prefers unwritten principles.
- **AI-feature work already carries evidence by artifact: taxonomies become evals** ([context-and-memory](../../options/DIMENSIONS.md#context-and-memory-context-and-memory)).
  - This is the clearest pattern in the eight maps for carrying research into a plan.
  - It exists only where the product is itself an AI feature, with a measurable target.
- **An internal harness run as a branded product, with no mandate** ([adoption-model](../../options/DIMENSIONS.md#adoption-model-adoption-and-enablement-model); [agent-interface](../../options/DIMENSIONS.md#agent-interface-agent-interface-own-app-chat-or-embedded)).
  - AirChat spans IDE plugins, a CLI over vendor agents, SDK web apps, Slack and a remote web UI.
  - Its platform team says adoption was organic, through champions, with no quotas.
  - Yet the CEO tracks token use and calls AI "existential". Mandate and organic adoption coexist.

## Cross-ecosystem notes (for the comparison worker)

- **Archetype: "CEO as the bridge".** Airbnb is the only mapped company that answers the research → intent → plan seam with a person rather than an artifact or a shared place. Product work is reviewed by the CEO on a fixed cadence, and principles are deliberately unwritten.
- **Most role-split of the eight.** Stripe, Intercom and Anthropic collapse roles. Airbnb split the PM into four roles in 2020, which makes it the closest analogue to a large, functional, regulated org. It still kept the decision central.
- **First-party naming of the intent → plan drift.** "The doc isn't updated" after prototyping, and a proposed AI coordinator ([DX talk](../../research/sources/dx-airbnb-beyond-the-cli-airchat.md)). No other ecosystem states this gap so plainly.
- **Evals as the evidence carrier.** Insight Miner taxonomies, seed queries and PM-labelled golden sets carry research into AI features. Spotify's evaluation funnel is similar.
- **Agents present:** Insight Miner (research), Pascal (brief), AirChat (harness for coding and SDK apps), validation and critique loops (review), on-call agents and the support agent (operate). None sets the goal or plans the change.
- **Release moved from a twice-yearly calendar to continuous shipping (2026)**, with the May event kept as a showcase.

## Could not verify

- Pascal, Everest, the designer prototyping tool and the AI project coordinator have no public technical detail. Each is known from one talk or interview.
- Code-share figures are defined inconsistently: "coauthored with AI" (Q1 2026 letter), "59% primarily AI-authored" (DX survey) and "60% AI-authored" (CTO). The 60% in the Q2 2026 letter is concept-to-launch time, not code share.
- Whether experiment volume and guardrails survived after 2021, given Chesky's 2023 line that A/B testing can be "abdicating a responsibility".
- The share of roadmap items kept: 10% (Config, Jun 2023) or about 20% (Lenny's, Nov 2023).
- What program managers receive at handoff: no spec template, PRD format or roadmap tool is named.
- Chesky's YC "founder mode" talk (Sep 2024) was not recorded. His June 2023 clarification on X was seen only in secondary sources.
- No independent check of any AI adoption or support figure.
