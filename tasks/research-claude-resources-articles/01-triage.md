Localised copies (`/de/`, `/fr/`, etc.) are duplicates and were skipped. All pages are first-party Anthropic publications; customer stories are vendor claims.

**How to read it.** One line per item: date (articles carry a publish date; guides, webinars and customer stories don't show one, so "undated"), title, relevance (high / medium / low / none), a one-line reason, and the source note when filed. "filed" = already in the library from an earlier task; "(folded)" = a companion page (a guide's article version, a short launch post, a second story about the same customer) filed inside another note. Guides are listed once with their sub-page count; each guide's sub-pages were read as part of its note.

**Topics used to judge relevance:** AI-native SDLC and product development; PM, design and analyst work with agents; harnesses, skills, plugins, subagents, MCP; Claude Code, Cowork and Enterprise for teams; persistent and shared sessions; context and knowledge management; governance, security and admin in regulated settings; adoption inside companies; evals. Low = on-site but adds nothing new for those topics (model launches, cloud availability, vertical pages, function stories outside PM/design/analysis, features already covered by filed docs). None = consumer, events, or public-benefit stories.

**Totals:** 695 lines (269 articles, 20 guides covering 145 pages, 284 customer pages, 118 webinar pages, 4 index pages). High 29, medium 66 (filed as 95 notes with companions folded in), already filed 9, folded 15, low 514, none 62.
