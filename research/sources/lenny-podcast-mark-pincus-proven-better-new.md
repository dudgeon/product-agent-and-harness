## Relevance to the project
A consumer-product veteran argues ideas are mostly wrong and AI should be a "testing machine, failure machine" that tests many ideas cheaply. This is the clearest statement in the lane of a slate of ideas validated by many small loops, and he notes teams are not doing it. Consumer-games context; transfer to regulated products is limited to the pre-customer stages.

## Themes
- idea-slate-validation-loops: instincts right about 95%, ideas wrong about 75%; test many ideas around an instinct, fail for the right reason (04:20-10:21); test 100 ideas a day, "build it completely wrong" first (35:38-36:25).
- problem-selection-as-core-skill: host quotes Sam Altman that the bottleneck is knowing what to create (01:24).
- ambition-expands-scope: start embarrassingly small; over-ambition misses product-market fit (24:12-27:03).
- prototyping-agents-limits: sees AI used to build one idea in three months, not a hundred in a day (35:38).

## Key points
- Proven, Better, New: copy what is proven, add only improvements all users would endorse, test new ideas separately (04:53-10:21).
- Fake-door test on the game board for a FarmVille expansion tested message and variant and sold about $19 million of early-access keys (37:23-38:46). Would meet disclosure limits at a lender.
