## Relevance to the project
The one Cash App design posting found that names the designer's agent tools: "Cursor, V0, and Figma Make to vibe-code interfaces", with "deep comfort using Cursor for vibe-coding". It is first-party evidence of the shape-the-intent toolset Cash App expects of designers in late 2025 (Cursor and v0 rather than goose), complementing the head of design's account ([dive-club-cam-worboys-ai-native-design-org](dive-club-cam-worboys-ai-native-design-org.md)).

## Themes
- prototyping-agents-limits: "Explore and deliver innovative solutions using Cursor, V0, and Figma Make to vibe-code interfaces"; "use LLM architecture knowledge and AI tools to rapidly prototype" (responsibilities).
- non-engineer-builders: "AI-native fluency: deep comfort using Cursor for vibe-coding, V0 for rapid prototyping", "Figma Make for design automation" (qualifications).
- pm-persona-role-expansion: designer owns parts of the experience "from framing opportunities to prototyping AI-enhanced solutions"; partners with product, engineering, trust and safety, ML, data science and research.
