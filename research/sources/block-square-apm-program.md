## Relevance to the project
Square's entry-level PM programme is pitched at "the next generation of AI-native product leaders" and selects on what candidates have built: the work sample can be "something you built using AI tools", and every advancing candidate does "a take-home AI-focused product challenge". It shows Block hiring PMs for building-with-AI fluency, while the job itself is still described in classic terms (discovery, strategy, execution, launch).

## Themes
- pm-persona-role-expansion: "designed to develop the next generation of AI-native product leaders"; "Your potential and what you've built matter far more than your résumé" (intro).
- non-engineer-builders: application requires a work sample, e.g. "something you built using AI tools"; take-home "tailored to evaluating product sense and comfort with AI-driven ideas" (FAQ).

## Key points
- Cohort of 4-6; two six-month rotations (e.g. payments, commerce tools, risk and identity, seller experiences, fintech infrastructure, "emerging AI initiatives"); full-time PM from day one; US-only, remote-first. US pay $115,000-$202,800.
- Work spans "discovery, product strategy, execution, and launches"; panel of PMs and product leaders evaluates product sense, analytical thinking, collaboration.
- "AI-native" is a "strong plus", not a requirement; technical or design background "helpful".
- No named tools or agent workflows.
