## Relevance to the project
A Cash App growth role written as a product owner ("You won't hand requirements off to someone else") that owns the goal, roadmap and experiment backlog, diagnoses its own funnel "without waiting on an analyst", and lists "AI tools (Claude Code, Goose, or others)" and "AI-assisted analysis for self-serve opportunity sizing" as tools used and taught. It is the one Block posting that names Goose for a non-engineering, product-adjacent role, and it shows the read-the-data and set-the-goal steps collapsing into one person with agent help.

## Themes
- pm-persona-role-expansion: "This role blurs growth and product"; "you are the product owner for your growth levers"; ships directly with Engineering, ML and Design (The Role; You Will).
- metrics-review-agents: "Diagnose your own funnel. Size opportunities, find friction, read experiment results, and iterate without waiting on an analyst"; technologies "SQL and AI-assisted analysis for self-serve opportunity sizing" (You Will; Technologies We Use and Teach).
- non-engineer-builders: "AI tools (Claude Code, Goose, or others) for strategy, execution, and automation" (Technologies We Use and Teach).

## Key points
- Cash App: 59 million monthly actives; "your experiments read in days, not months" (What's Unique).
- Owns roadmap and experiment backlog; scores every idea by expected impact and effort, whether product change, in-app surface, incentive or message.
- "Every experiment you run feeds, and is shaped by, the AI and automation stack we're building"; push AI decisioning in growth.
- Related posting read for the experimentation platform: Senior Data Engineer, Product (https://block.xyz/careers/jobs/5442647008, 2026-10-08) says Product Data Engineering builds "the data infrastructure powering Block's experimentation platform" and will "build AI agents for false positive reduction and automated resolution" of data-quality issues. No public description of the experimentation platform itself, or of AI on it, was found.
