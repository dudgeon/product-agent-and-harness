## Relevance to the project
Shows how a PM-tool vendor packages its PM agent: bundled into Business and Enterprise plans and metered in credits per maker. Corroborates that Spark is out of a separate beta and standard for new workspaces, which bears on case 2's GA question.

## Themes

## Key points
- "Spark is currently available as standard for all new Business and Enterprise Productboard workspaces"; existing customers being moved over gradually (contact support or account rep).
- "Every plan includes Spark, our AI built for PMs"; Business "Full Spark access (insights, skills, automations, integrations)"; AI Themes is Enterprise-only.
- No list price for Business (request demo); Enterprise contact sales. No dates on the page.
