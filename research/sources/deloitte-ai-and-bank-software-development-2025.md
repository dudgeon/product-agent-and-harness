## Relevance to the project
Sizes the software workforce at large banks and gives a prediction and a few reported results for AI coding tools, including legacy rewrite. Useful for scale (15-25 percent of workforce in software tasks at some large banks) and for how soft the productivity evidence is.

## Themes
- product-team-shape-future: some large banks have 15 to 25 percent of total workforce on software-related tasks (page).
- agent-factory-sdlc: predicts 20-40 percent savings in bank software investment by 2028 from AI tools.

## Key points
- Published 24 April 2025. 84 percent of developers use at least one LLM coding assistant (cited survey); studies report 30-55 percent gains for assisted programmers.
- Citizens Bank saw a 20 percent productivity boost among engineers in a test case; Goldman Sachs equipped 12,000 developers; Citigroup applying assistants to 30,000 developers.
- Prediction: AI tools save 20-40 percent of software investment in banking by 2028; US$0.5-1.1 million per engineer.
- US banks employ about 100,000 people in software development; US banking software spend about US$107.8 billion in 2024 (Gartner).
- Legacy: models being trained to rewrite old COBOL; advice to communicate openly about job shifts and career paths.

## Could not verify
Consultancy forecast; the 30-55 percent figures are from general studies, not bank estates; the 20 percent Citizens figure is one test case with no method given.
