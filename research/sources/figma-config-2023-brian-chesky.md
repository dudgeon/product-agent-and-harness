## Relevance to the project
This is the talk where Chesky said Airbnb "got rid of the classic product management function", which set off the 2023 debate. Figma's edited transcript is the best primary record of what he said. It also has the most concrete description of Airbnb's intent-shaping sequence: insight, story (often starting with a keynote), historical references plus research, concept development, design language, launch campaign, then study usage. And it has the "Airbnb Blueprint", a storyboard of every screen, policy and ops touchpoint used to set fix priorities. Useful for the project's map of where research evidence enters intent and plan.

## Themes
- pm-persona-role-expansion: "The designers are equal to the product managers. Actually, we got rid of the classic product management function." Then: "We combined product management with product marketing." Designers are "architects" (section "Pulling Airbnb back from the brink"). Also "product managers are critical, but they shouldn't be doing the job of designer" ("Principles" section).
- one-roadmap-functional-org: business-unit to functional org; "If it's not on the roadmap, it can't ship and it must be on one roadmap"; "We can only do 10% of the things on the roadmap" (same section; at Lenny's he said 20%).
- narrative-first-intent: "we actually like to start a lot of product development, not with design, but with marketing"; product marketing plus designers set "What is this project? What are the goals?"; "if it's going to be a keynote, we'll start with a keynote" (alignment answer).
- leadership-review-cadence: "I reviewed the work every week, every two weeks, every four weeks... I'm gonna be the chief editor"; top 30-40 people as "one neural network, one brain".
- opportunity-to-code-chain: the Airbnb Rooms / host passport example walks insight (guests nervous, hosts invisible in listings) to historical reference (passport) to attribute research to design language to ops (headshots of 40,000 hosts) to campaign to post-launch study.
- svpg-four-risks: A/B tests only with a hypothesis: "If B is better than A, you have to know why"; "Metrics are not a strategy".
- artifact-standardization: on design systems: "you should design whatever you want and then you put it in the design language system. If you can only pull from the system, you're never going to be able to take a giant leap."

## Key points
- Timeline: late 2019 he felt the company had drifted; met Hiroki Asai and Jony Ive around the 2019-20 holidays; hired Asai, made Ive's firm (LoveFrom) a client; COVID cut 80% of business in eight weeks (Chesky's figure); rebuilt company from the ground up.
- Pre-2020 state in his words: 10 divisions with about 10 subdivisions each, "very much run by product managers", "a plethora of A/B experiments"; more people and projects but "the less our app changed".
- Release model: "ship 80% of the products twice a year"; continuous code shipping is "a budget, that's about 20%" for optimisations.
- Roadmap: about three years out, "fuzzy" beyond two; clear picture to next November; Chesky as "keeper of the roadmap", updated "every single week".
- Product marketing defined as "product management plus outbound marketing", "a smaller function... extremely influential".
- Research: "We always try to combine data and research. They're equally important"; research includes "historical references", not only users.
- Airbnb Blueprint (storyboard idea from Disney's Snow White): every screen on one wall (about 150), every user policy (nearly 70, some 100 pages), every ops touchpoint; then 20 million customer service calls, hundreds of thousands of social posts, workshops and first-hand use fed "a prioritized map" to fix the core product first. Rule: no permission for new things "until people love our core service".
- Org: a "workshop group of a few dozen people" covering the whole app; a studio for implementations; specialists in haptics, animation, typography, colour; UX writing under marketing (in Nov 2023 he described a separate writing function).
- Results claim: from breakeven to "nearly $4 billion in free cash flow" in 2022 (Chesky).

## Notable quotes
- "A/B testing is abdicating a responsibility to the users." (brink section)
- "If you ship a feature, and no one knows, did it really matter?" (alignment answer)
