## Relevance to the project

Peer-reviewed field evidence that making work broadly visible can reduce real learning, because observed people hide their experiments, while small "zones of privacy" raised performance. It is the strongest independent counterweight found to Shopify's claim that public-by-default agent sessions maximise organisational learning, and it bears on the trust and privacy model dimension (what default visibility a harness's sessions should have). Caveat: one Chinese electronics factory in 2008-09, manual assembly, not knowledge work or agents.

## Themes

- **org-learning-loop**: Counter-evidence: "broad visibility, intended to increase transparency, can breed hiding behavior and myths of learning and control" (The Paradox of Transparency, p. ~207). Learning happened inside privacy zones and was shared upward "only after they had data" (HBS Working Knowledge summary, 2013-11-25, https://www.library.hbs.edu/working-knowledge/hiding-from-managers-can-increase-your-productivity).
- **shared-persistent-sessions**: privacy supports "productive deviance, localized experimentation, distraction avoidance, and continuous improvement" (abstract), i.e. the trial-and-error phase people may not want watched.

## Key points

- Design: embedded participant-observers (Harvard undergraduates working on the lines) plus a field experiment: curtains around 4 of 32 lines; 28 controls; 55 pre-period and 142 post-period operating days (May 2009 onward).
- Result: curtained lines' defect-free units per hour rose "by as much as 10–15 percent after the first week" and kept the lead for five months (Results).
- Mechanism: observed workers performed a sanctioned version of the work and hid better local methods; managers saw "a show"; privacy at group level (not individual) let teams experiment and then share.
- The author's future-research question: not whether to have a curtain but "when and for how long particular kinds of groups should be given their time inside the curtain" — directly analogous to a private-draft-then-publish session model.
- Generalisability is the stated main limitation (single site, manufacturing, China).
