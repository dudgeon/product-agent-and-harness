## Relevance to the project
Community roadmap for software traceability research, including industrial adoption. Filed so a later reader knows the field's own stated goal: traceability that is pervasive and 'effectively disappeared without a trace', that is, a by-product of work.

## Themes
- intent-to-market-traceability: the research community's goal is capture that happens as a by-product of work, not as a separate chore (abstract).

## Key points
- Proceedings of a 2017 meeting ten years after the first traceability grand-challenges symposium.
- Organised around four axes: strategising, trace link creation and evolution, trace link usage, and real-world applications; breakout groups on datasets and industrial adoption.
