## Relevance to the project
A worker-grounded taxonomy of *desired* human involvement per task: the Human Agency Scale (H1 fully automated to H5 purely human) crossed with expert-rated AI capability, giving four zones (Green Light, Red Light, R&D opportunity, low priority). This is the best existing model for an activity-classification axis that is about the human's wish for control, not only task structure.

## Themes
- work-structure-spectrum: desired human involvement varies by task and occupation, with an inverted-U pattern (per search summary) read as support for human-agent collaboration.
- human-judgment-pm-jobs: workers favour automating repetitive low-value tasks (positive attitudes for 46.1% of tasks per secondary summary) and may shift value toward interpersonal skills.

## Key points
- WORKBank: 1,500 workers and 52 AI experts, 844 tasks, 104 occupations (O*NET), data Jan-May 2025; v3 2026-02-01.
- Limitation: O*NET tasks only; no new tasks agents create; self-reported preferences.
