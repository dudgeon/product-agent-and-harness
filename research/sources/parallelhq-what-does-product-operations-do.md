## Relevance to the project
Vendor blog describing product ops: standardising workflows, owning tool stack, single source of truth. Basis for persona P5; the map infers that ops would own agent integrations (inference, not sourced).

## Themes
- **pm-persona-role-expansion**: Product ops owns the 'how': processes, tools and data; standardises workflows and documentation; manages tool consolidation and standardisation (not 'owns the tool stack'); keeps a single source of truth for the roadmap; maintains metrics, feedback and usage data; enables onboarding and templates.
- **feedback-triage-agents**: Product ops maintains feedback and usage data sources; its single source of truth is for the roadmap (J10).

## Key points
- The 'how': processes, tools, data.
- Standardises workflows and documentation; manages tool standardisation; single source of truth for the roadmap; maintains product data sources (metrics, feedback, usage).
- Job-posting based, no survey data.
