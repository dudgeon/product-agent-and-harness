## Relevance to the project
Dovetail's enterprise sales page, which carries the "30hrs weekly time saved per user" stat and links to a Dovetail-commissioned Forrester Total Economic Impact study. Shows how vendor ROI stats get detached from their method. Evidence kind: vendor-claim (the Forrester TEI was commissioned by Dovetail, so it is also vendor-claim). Audit 2026-10-24: page fetched in full; the Forrester TEI at https://tei.forrester.com/go/dovetail/CustomerInsights/ and Dovetail's TEI summary at https://dovetail.com/resources/industry-reports/2025-forrester-TEI-of-Dovetail were also fetched.

## Key points
- Enterprise page stat tiles (no speaker): "30hrs" weekly time saved per user; "30%" team capacity increase; "66%" faster shipping; "more than double the return... payback in under six months". Links to "Download the Forrester Total Economic Impact Study".
- Forrester TEI of Dovetail (Forrester Consulting, April 2025): "Dovetail commissioned Forrester Consulting" to do the study. Method: interviews with four decision-makers, combined into a composite IT services organisation (10,000 employees, $1B revenue, 100 primary users). Results: 236% risk-adjusted ROI, $1.59M NPV, payback <6 months, 36,000 hours saved on project processes over three years, 30% capacity increase for primary users. Modelled, not measured on a single customer.
- The "30 hours/user/week" figure on the enterprise page does not appear in the TEI landing page or Dovetail's TEI summary (unverified; may be in the full PDF, which was not downloaded). 36,000 hours / 3 years / 100 users is about 2.3 hours per user per week, so 30 hours/week looks inconsistent with the TEI headline.
- Notion PM "100 hours down to 10" and GrandPad "about half" testimonials: not on this page; verified in the 2024-10-15 launch release instead.
