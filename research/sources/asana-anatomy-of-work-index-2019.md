## Relevance to the project
Vendor survey of 10,223 knowledge workers in six countries: respondents say about 60% of the day goes to coordination (status, searching, shifting priorities). It is the most quoted figure for the size of the coordination prize and also the weakest: self-reported, from a work-management vendor, no PM-specific or enterprise cut. Later editions say 58% and 63% for firms over 5,000 staff (secondary reports; not verified).

## Themes
- `pm-coordination-work-automated`: 60% 'work about work' self-report; use as an upper-bound claim, not a measurement.

## Key points
- Self-reported recall, not diaries or telemetry; the category definition is broad and commercially motivated.
- The 2022 edition (58%; 63% for 5,000+ staff) comes from search snippets; I did not open those editions.

## Could not verify
The 2022 and 2023 editions' method and large-firm cut.
