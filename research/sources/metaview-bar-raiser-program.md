## Relevance to the project
Amazon's Bar Raiser is the best-known organisational pattern for a critique role with structural independence: a trained interviewer from outside the hiring team, with standing in the debrief and (at Amazon) weight in the decision. This vendor write-up argues the independence matters more than the veto, and that the operative mechanism is forcing written answers to objections. For agent design that is the template for a reviewer agent or human reviewer whose authority comes from not owning the deadline, and whose output is a recorded objection the owner must answer, not a block.

## Themes
- red-team-stance: the bar raiser is "an independent second reader rather than a veto", pressing in the debrief on which competencies were actually covered (article).
- gate-judge-separation: the raiser has no stake in the requisition or its deadline, which is what lets them say a candidate is not ready; the hiring manager owns the outcome (article).
- decision-rationale-records: a hire may proceed over an objection only if the objection and the reason it was overruled are written down (article).
- decision-rights-for-agents: a veto-only role "teaches people to route around it"; requiring written answers changes the loop even without a block (article).

## Key points
- Setup for small teams: two or three raisers who keep their day jobs; calibrate by scoring the same past interviews; write a standard per role family before interviewing; one raiser per loop from outside the team; agree where objections are recorded.
- Stated limit: no data shows bar-raiser programmes improve who gets hired, because records end at the offer; the author recommends judging on process measures (written standard existed, every interviewer submitted a scorecard, objections answered on record).
- Amazon's own description of the role was not found on an official page; third-party sources disagree on whether the veto is absolute or rarely used.
- Parallel in the library: the Anthropic SDLC playbook's rule that the authoring agent cannot approve its own code, and goose's Adversary Mode reviewer.
