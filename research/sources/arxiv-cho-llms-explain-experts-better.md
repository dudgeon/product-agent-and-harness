## Relevance to the project
Preprint claiming that tacit knowledge externalised by an LLM from expert behaviour (not from interviews) improves decision quality, narrows the expert-novice gap and beats knowledge written by the experts themselves. If it holds, capture can come from observing work rather than asking people to write it down. I read only the abstract; no sample, effect sizes or domain were available.

## Themes
- `memory-architecture`: behaviour-derived externalisation is claimed to beat self-articulation (Polanyi's paradox).

## Key points
- Preprint, abstract only; unspecified task domain; not replicated. A related 2026 preprint (Expert Mind, arXiv 2603.14541) is a design paper for energy-sector expert preservation with no results.
