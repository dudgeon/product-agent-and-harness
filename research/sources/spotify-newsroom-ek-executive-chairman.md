## Relevance to the project
Confirms who decides at the top since 1 January 2026: co-CEOs Söderström (product and technology) and Norström (business), reporting to executive chairman Ek, who keeps capital allocation and long-term direction. Spotify says this only formalises how it has run since 2023. Useful for the "who decides what" line in the operating-model picture.

## Themes
- autonomy-alignment-placement (proposed): decision rights stay with two co-leaders split product/tech vs business; Ek: "This change simply matches titles to how we already operate."

## Key points
- Effective 2026-01-01: Ek becomes executive chairman (European-style), focused on capital allocation and "the long arc of the company".
- Söderström (co-president, CPO and CTO) and Norström (co-president, CBO) become co-CEOs, report to Ek, and join the board subject to shareholder approval.
- Release says the co-presidents have led most strategic development and operational execution since 2023.
- No detail on R&D, product process or AI.
