## Relevance to the project

First-party account of the main European limit on mining employee communications: works councils objected to Copilot being able to evaluate or rank individual employees, and Microsoft rolled out under a "tolerance phase" in Germany, France and the Netherlands until spring 2025. Any context agent that mines Slack, email or meetings, or that infers "who knows what", touches the same co-determination rights. Microsoft's own framing.

## Themes

- **tacit-knowledge-capture**: council concerns included Copilot ranking employees in a meeting and making inferences about individuals without supporting data; early tools answered sensitive questions about religion, race, gender and ethnicity (concerns section).
- **enterprise-admin-controls**: controlled deployment via a tolerance phase; council feedback went to product engineering; Copilot Studio agents reviewed at platform level, with individual high-impact agents possibly needing their own approval (process section).

## Key points

- Tolerance phase ended spring 2025; Copilot now approved for all Microsoft employees worldwide (200,000+).
- The Employee Self-Service Agent, which touches sensitive employee data, was approved quickly after consultation.
- Legal background (law-firm commentary seen in search, not filed): German works councils have co-determination rights under §87(1) no. 6 BetrVG over technical systems capable of monitoring behaviour or performance, regardless of intent.

## Notable quotes

- Schleicher (German works council chair): "The earliest versions of these generative AI tools lacked guardrails"
- Chemerys: "They hate surprises, so we strive to stay ahead of things and make sure they stay informed."
