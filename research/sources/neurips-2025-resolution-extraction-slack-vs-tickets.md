## Relevance to the project

A direct comparison of knowledge mined from chat (Slack, Teams) with knowledge from structured tickets for the same job (IT support resolutions). Chat is more relevant and concise but noisier and less readable, and only becomes usable with high-precision filtering: a concrete picture of the trade-off in passive mining. Workshop poster, abstract level.

## Themes

- **tacit-knowledge-capture**: "Slack-based resolutions are more relevant and concise but noisier and less readable"; "with high-precision filtering, conversational sources can be transformed into a meaningful source of resolutions" (abstract).
- **org-context-grounding**: conversational data is complementary to, not a replacement for, structured sources (abstract).

## Key points

- 1,000 samples scored on six KPIs: context relevance, completeness, conciseness, noise, perplexity, readability.
