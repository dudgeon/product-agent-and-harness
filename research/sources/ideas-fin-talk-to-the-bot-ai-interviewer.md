## Relevance to the project
Intercom's research team on where AI-moderated interviews fit in its "talk to customers" row: used in a churn study with lost accounts, for pilot studies and for continuous learning on well-defined topics; not for early discovery, long interviews, synthesis, or (mostly) current customers. AI summaries are a cross-check, not the analysis, because they lack company context. A clear statement of a human-led boundary.

## Themes
- research-synthesis-agents: AI-moderated churn interviews; response rate 4% when offered as clearly AI-run and always available, vs a typical 1.5%; the churn pilot "drove more traction than any previous churn-related research initiative".
- human-judgment-pm-jobs: "Where AI moderation doesn't work": early-stage discovery (no guide yet), long-form interviews, analysis and synthesis, speaking with current customers, where a 1:1 shows "we're investing real time and attention".
- ai-synthesis-hallucination-risk: AI summaries used "as a reference point" to cross-check a researcher's own view; team adds company context during analysis.

## Key points
- AI moderators struggle to "pick up on interesting threads" and are "limited by the guidance and context we provide"; long AI interviews feel "tiring and transactional".
- Vendor/tool not named.
