## Relevance to the project
Accessibility is the cleanest example of an enterprise obligation that an agent can read on day one and check against: four principles, 13 guidelines, testable success criteria at three levels, and published "Understanding" documents per criterion giving intent, examples, techniques and common failures. It is also the clearest case where the two candidate partitions disagree: by quality attribute it is a property of the experience; by obligation source it is law (public-sector and equality legislation in many jurisdictions) and policy (the organisation's chosen conformance level). The arc42 summary of the same standard gives the counts (86 success criteria, levels A, AA, AAA, ISO/IEC 40500:2025 approval on 21 October 2025).

## Themes
- requirements-partitions: content must be perceivable, operable, understandable and robust; "if any of these are not true, users with disabilities will not be able to use the Web"; 13 guidelines, each under one principle.
- rules-vs-judgment-encoding: each success criterion "will be either true or false" when content is tested and is "technology neutral"; the Understanding documents are "not required to meet WCAG" and the normative requirements are only in the WCAG 2.2 document itself.

## Key points
- Structure: principles, guidelines, success criteria; Understanding documents per criterion cover intent, benefits, examples, resources, sufficient techniques, common failures and advisory techniques.
- Conformance levels (from the arc42 summary, not this page): A, AA, AAA, cumulative; AA is the usual legal and policy target.
