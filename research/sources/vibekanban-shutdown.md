## Relevance to the project
A cautionary data point for the orchestrator-wrapper category: a popular agent-orchestration UI (kanban over parallel Claude Code/Codex worktrees) shut down its company because it found no business model, leaving a community-maintained local tool. Bears on dependency risk of adopting thin wrapper tools.

## Themes
- [[meta-harness]]: thousands of daily users but mostly free; hosted features (shared kanban issues, comments, orgs) removed after 30 days; local workspaces continue; project continues open source, community maintained.
- [[build-vs-buy]]: dependency risk of building on a small vendor's collaboration features.

## Key points
- Quote: they 'couldn't find a business model that we could get excited about'. Data export added before shutdown.
