## Relevance to the project
An Atlassian Platinum Partner, which sells Rovo adoption services, still reports that Rovo is inconsistent at the most PM-like task (backlog analysis with precise JQL) and feels "half baked" without configuration. Because the bias runs towards praising Rovo, the admitted limits carry some weight; the praise carries little. Bears on case 1 (Rovo) and on whether platform agents need curated context to do PM work.

## Themes
- agent-failure-modes: "When asked to run nuanced backlog analysis or apply precise JQL, results can be inconsistent"; users report wrong ticket selection or ignored filters.
- org-context-grounding: value depends heavily on configuration, data quality and prompt design; out-of-the-box sidebar use can feel "half baked".

## Key points
- Source of feedback: unnamed clients and community discussions with admins/power users; no sample size, timeframe or method.
- Reported strengths: JSM auto-routing, priority setting, ticket enrichment; drafting and faster search in Jira/Confluence.
- Reported weaknesses: nuanced backlog analysis and precise JQL are inconsistent; experience depends on setup.
- Bias: Atlassian Platinum Partner with a commercial interest in Rovo adoption (anecdote with a partner-bias note).
