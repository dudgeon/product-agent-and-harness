## Relevance to the project
A PM's account of building Fin Insights (CX Score, Optimize/Suggestions, Topics): expert-labelled reference datasets before shipping a scoring model, an inbox-style review workflow so suggestions are acted on rather than ignored, and explainable clusters. Answers part of the open question "does Intercom use its own conversation analytics to decide what to build?": this post does **not** say so. The only internal use is observing Intercom's own knowledge managers while designing Suggestions (user research, not roadmap input).

## Themes
- feedback-triage-agents: Topics clustering worked technically but users could not see why conversations grouped; fixes were examples, summaries, explanations, drill-down, trends, rename/merge controls (Lesson 3).
- evals-autonomy-trust: CX Score built with support leaders defining good/poor experience in plain-English rules, a large human-reviewed dataset labelled by experienced support professionals, a production evaluation pipeline and a neutral default when signal is weak; one score reason dropped for never meeting the bar (Lesson 1).
- human-review-gates: Suggestions shipped as an inbox with prioritisation and "safe review" (supporting conversations shown; accept, reject or edit) after early versions without a workflow became noise (Lesson 2).

## Key points
- "Fin Flywheel": Train, Test, Deploy, Analyze; Insights sits in Analyze.
- Discovery method for Suggestions: observed Intercom's own knowledge managers at work.
- Resolution rate stated as rising from under 25% to an average 76% over three years (self-reported).
