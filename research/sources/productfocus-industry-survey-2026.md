## Relevance to the project
Vendor survey of 677 respondents in 40 countries. Its 97% versus 64% gap between productivity gains and improved product outcomes is the sharpest data point for the outcome-measurement gap, and 85% validation shows human review remains standard. Figures confirmed against the PDF in the 2026-10-24 re-check.

## Themes
- **outcome-measurement-gap**: 97% report some productivity improvement from AI (32% significant, 10% vast) but only 64% report improved product outcomes.
- **human-judgment-pm-jobs**: '85% of product managers use their expertise to validate AI output'.
- **pm-ai-usage-surveys**: 677 respondents, 40 countries.

## Key points
- 677 respondents, 40 countries; 83% Europe, 8% US; self-selected; collected October 2025 to January 2026; junior PM to CPO plus product marketers and owners.
- Product Focus sells product-management training (landing page https://productfocus.com/product-management-resources/industry-survey).
- 97% productivity gains versus 64% improved outcomes.
- 85% validate AI output with their own expertise.
