- Custom Agents: AI "teammates" that run workflows on a schedule or trigger, using workspace content and connected services (Slack, email, calendars). - Named early customers: SumUp, Pigment, Pennylane, Lovable. Pigment: "70% of employees now using them across every team", majority weekly. SumUp: "saving several hours per week". Relays Notion EMEA GM Mick Hodgins.
