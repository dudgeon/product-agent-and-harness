## Relevance to the project
The earnings-day script in which Dorsey told investors why Block was cutting from over 10,000 people to just under 6,000 on the same day as a strong quarter, and the CFO gave the one productivity number offered: production code shipped per engineer up more than 40% since September 2025. It is the clearest public instance of a large company tying a near-halving of headcount to AI tools rather than financial distress, and it sets out the four-part operating model later expanded in [block-from-hierarchy-to-intelligence](block-from-hierarchy-to-intelligence.md). For the project it shows the leadership-mandate pattern at its most extreme and how thin the published outcome evidence is.

## Themes
- ai-flattened-org / leadership-permission-mandate: "Intelligence tools have changed what it means to build and run a company... A significantly smaller team, using the tools we're building, can do more and do it better." (Dorsey, p.2). He predicts most companies will make "similar structural changes" within a year.
- outcome-measurement-gap: the only quantified productivity claim is Ahuja's: ">40% increase in production code shipped per engineer since September" (p.5), plus "engineering work that would have taken weeks... done by a small team in a fraction of the time". Output volume, not product outcome.
- vendor-claims-vs-independent-evidence: Ahuja hedges that "the long-term impact will depend on thoughtful implementation at scale" and that some automation workstreams are "earlier in their maturity" (p.5).

## Key points
- Framing: the cut follows "one of our strongest years"; Q4 2025 gross profit $2.87B (+24% YoY); FY2025 gross profit $10.36B (+17%). Guidance raised to 18% gross profit growth ($12.2B) and $3.2B adjusted operating income for 2026, reflecting "a smaller cost structure" (pp.3, 5-6).
- How Block will operate (Dorsey, pp.2-3): (1) intelligence at the core of decisions, risk, product building and service, moving toward customers building "their own features directly on top of our capabilities"; (2) extreme focus on four builds: customer capabilities, interfaces, proactive intelligence, and "an intelligence model to fully orchestrate the company's operations"; (3) speed: "A company of our new size has no excuse for being slow."
- Dorsey says the employee message was "an email I posted to twitter" (p.2).
- Products named as the customer-facing side: Moneybot (Cash App), Square AI and the path to Managerbot (p.5).
- Owen Jennings is introduced as "Business Lead" and Nick Molnar as "Sales and Marketing Lead for Block" (p.1).

## Notable quotes
- "I'd rather get there honestly and on our own terms than be forced into it reactively." (Dorsey, p.2)
- "We will decide faster, ship faster, and learn faster." (Dorsey, p.3)
