## Relevance to the project
Vendor marketing pages for a survey of 250 product leaders. Its single use in the map is the claim that leadership escalations override prioritisation frameworks, which frames J5 as political as well as analytical.

## Themes
- **pm-ai-usage-surveys**: 'Over 60% of prioritization frameworks are overridden by leadership escalations' (ProductPlan marketing page; report gated).

## Key points
- 250 product leaders per ProductPlan; Product-Led Alliance says 'nearly 250 product professionals'. 11th annual edition, co-produced with Product-Led Alliance (https://www.productledalliance.com/state-of-product-management-report.md, cites report p.52).
- Leadership escalations override prioritization frameworks in over 60% of cases.
- Used in J5 as support for rating trade-offs low for automation.

The '37% of companies include product ops' figure was not found in any ProductPlan material (the only ProductPlan 37% found is a 2023 cross-functional-alignment figure); treat as misattributed and do not use.
