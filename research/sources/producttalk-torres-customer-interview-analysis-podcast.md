## Relevance to the project
Primary practitioner evidence on where AI helps and hurts in interview analysis, the best-evidenced PM-agent job. Torres's tests set the guardrail (review AI synthesis against source quotes) that shapes the J1 and J10 automation ratings.

## Themes
- **feedback-triage-agents**: Torres tested 15 interviews on ChatGPT and Claude: AI helps only if interviews are strong and context is supplied; pasting transcripts is not a shortcut; 'expert + AI' gives the gains. Review AI synthesis against source quotes.
- **human-judgment-pm-jobs**: Empathy from talking to customers stays human; customer empathy cannot be outsourced.
- **ai-synthesis-hallucination-risk**: Keep analysis and synthesis separate; do not outsource customer understanding; 'expert + AI' beats 'beginner + AI' beats nothing.

## Key points
- Torres tested 15 interviews on ChatGPT and Claude (show notes: "running 15 interviews through ChatGPT and Claude").
- AI helps only if interviews are strong and context is supplied; pasting transcripts is not a shortcut.
- 'Expert + AI' gives the gains; empathy from talking to customers stays human.
- The map applies the caution (review against source quotes) to J10 feedback triage.
