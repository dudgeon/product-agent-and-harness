## Relevance to the project
Observation of 14 developers plus a survey of 22: programming is shifting from generating to evaluating solutions, 48.8% of observed actions were biased and 56.4% of those involved the LLM. Small and qualitative, but it is the only study found that observes reviewing of LLM output at the action level.

## Themes
- `overreliance-and-deskilling`: developer actions around LLM output carry new bias types (taxonomy of 15 categories).

## Key points
- n=14 observed, not a measure of missed defects; no review-accuracy outcome.
