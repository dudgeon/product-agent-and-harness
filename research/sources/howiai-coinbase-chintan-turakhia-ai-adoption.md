## Relevance to the project
A first-party account of AI adoption across a 1,000+ engineer organisation at a regulated financial company (a crypto exchange), with adoption tactics the project's adoption-model dimension can borrow: leaders working hands-on in the tools, a timed "PR speed run" event, finding power users from usage data and spreading their behaviours, and custom Slack-bot agents wired into existing workflow. It also claims a 10x cut in PR review time (150 hours to 15) and a compressed feedback-to-feature loop demoed live, which bears on the film's "bottleneck moved" argument from the engineering side. Only the show notes were available, so everything below is the episode's own framing, not verified content.

## Themes
- org-learning-loop: "How to identify and replicate the behaviors of AI power users" and a chapter on "Using Cursor to analyze AI adoption patterns" (23:14): power users found through data analysis, then their practices spread (show notes).
- enablement-by-product / adoption: "Why engineering leaders must get hands-on with AI tools to drive adoption"; chapter "The importance of leadership conviction and hands-on demonstration" (08:00). The "speed run" got "100 engineers to push 70 PRs in 15 minutes" (10:30).
- feedback-triage-agents: "Demo: Real-time feedback-to-feature implementation" (19:20) and "Demo: Building a live feedback capture system using AI transcription" (36:00); the stated outcome is "dramatically compressing the cycle from user feedback to shipped features".
- remote-agent-invocation: "Using custom Slack bots to automate engineering workflows" (40:50); "How to build custom AI agents that integrate with your existing workflows" (show notes). Tools named: Cursor, Linear, Slack, ChatGPT, Claude, GitHub Copilot.
- outcome-measurement-gap: chapter "Measuring success" (17:57) and "The metrics that actually matter when measuring AI's impact on engineering velocity"; the headline metric quoted is PR review time "from 150 hours to just 15 hours" (show notes).
- agent-factory-sdlc: the trigger was a mandate to rewrite Coinbase's self-custody wallet into a consumer social app (Base App) "in just six to nine months", with AI as the force multiplier (02:38).
