## Relevance to the project
A portal vendor's survey with two findings that matter for a dependency agent: most teams wait a day or more for SRE or DevOps help, and engineers do not trust the metadata in their central system of record (only 3% call it completely trustworthy). The second is direct vendor-claim evidence for the stale-records problem: if catalogue and ownership data is untrusted, an agent reading it inherits that distrust. Treat all numbers as vendor claims with no published method on the page.

## Themes
- coordination-cost-and-waiting: 78% of engineering teams wait a day or more for SRE/DevOps assistance; 16% report a maximum wait of a week or more; 22% say issues are resolved within a day on average (30% with a portal). Evidence: vendor survey.
- stale-records-mislead-agents: 50% of teams lack trust in the data quality of their central system of record; 3% find their metadata repository completely trustworthy; 53% update software-asset metadata no more than once a week.
- platform-onboarding-friction: 94% are dissatisfied with self-service tools; 6% very satisfied.
- cognitive-load-and-platforms: 75% lose 6-15 hours weekly to tool sprawl; average 7.4 tools for everyday operational tasks.

## Key points
- Only 15% of engineers say standards are clearly defined across domains.
- 53% use Backstage, Port or both; Confluence, Jira, CMDBs and spreadsheets remain in wide use.
- Cost model: tool sprawl could cost a 50-engineer team nearly $1 million a year (modelled from BLS wage data).
