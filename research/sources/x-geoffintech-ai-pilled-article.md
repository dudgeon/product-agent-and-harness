## Relevance to the project

The adoption playbook behind Glass: a four-level AI proficiency ladder, leaderboards, no token caps, central-platform-plus-spokes org design, and the story that 90%+ adoption still left people stuck on basic chat, which is why Glass was built. Key for the strategy deck on organizational change and for how a harness fits an adoption program.

## Themes

- **non-engineer-builders**: Non-engineers = 12% of human-initiated PRs; 1,500+ apps from 800+ builders in six weeks; 700-person hackathon.
- **org-learning-loop**: Four proficiency levels L0-L3; Dojo with 350+ skills; leaderboards; 'build from the center, drive from the spokes'.
- **cost-control-routing**: 'Kill token limits': no caps early, then later a $1.5M week led to metering (see launch article).
- **build-vs-buy**: Glass built on Anthropic's Claude Agent SDK as 'our own version of Claude Code's Cowork'; team of four, under three months.

## Key points

- 'The models were good enough. The harness wasn't': terminal/npm/MCP too hard for most.
- Glass auto-configures via Okta SSO with 30+ tools.
- Claims: AI usage up 6,300% YoY; 99.5% active; 84% weekly coding-agent use; 700 DAU within a month of Glass launch.
- Eight lessons: start now; proficiency is a curve; creative destruction; center + spokes; give a stage; fast aha; competition; remove constraints.
