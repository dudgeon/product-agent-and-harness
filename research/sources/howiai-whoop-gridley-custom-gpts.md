## Relevance to the project
A product leader at Whoop builds "dozens" of custom GPTs for her team that "think and give feedback like her": she reverse-engineers her own decision criteria into evaluation rubrics, then gives the team GPTs that apply them (for example a slide-deck evaluator and a writing-feedback GPT). It is an early, simple example of capturing a leader's tacit judgment as a reusable review agent, which bears on tacit-knowledge capture, review agents for PM artifacts, and how one person's practice spreads to a team. Low relevance because we have only the show notes, and the approach (custom GPTs, 2025) is lightweight.

## Themes
- tacit-knowledge-capture: "reverse engineering your own decision criteria" and turning management expertise "into clear evaluation rubrics that AI can consistently apply" (show notes; 02:42, 12:57 "The value of articulating taste").
- prd-agents: demo of a slide-deck evaluator GPT (15:23) and AI writing feedback for the team (23:42).
