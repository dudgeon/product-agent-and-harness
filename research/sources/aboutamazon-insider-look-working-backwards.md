## Relevance to the project
Amazon's own excerpt on the PR/FAQ: a narrative document that is both the end-product pitch and the record of a multi-round review, read silently in the meeting with minutes circulated after. It is the best-documented case of a judgment-encoding artifact whose review ritual is rigid while its content is open, and most PR/FAQs are meant to be rejected.

## Themes
- leadership-review-cadence: writers often revise ten or more times and meet senior leaders five or more times (body).
- human-review-gates: one-hour review starts with silent reading, then general feedback, then line-by-line comments; "the most senior attendees tend to speak last" (body).
- process-artifacts-as-coordination: minutes with feedback are distributed after each review; the PR/FAQ stays a living document after approval (body).

## Key points
- Working Backwards described as "a systematic way to vet ideas and create new products", used for major initiatives since 2004.
- Format: press release of a few paragraphs plus FAQ of five pages or less (external and internal FAQ per the book, not confirmed on this page).
- "So what?" test: is the product meaningfully better (faster, easier, cheaper)?
- Most PR/FAQs are not approved, presented as intentional resource protection.
- Not on this page: six-page narrative format details, bar raiser.
