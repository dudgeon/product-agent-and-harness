## Relevance to the project
Mainly a hiring playbook. Of use here only for a short view that narrow specialists and new graduates are squeezed while taste, decision-making and problem solving rise, and for how a fast AI-tool company decides on hires.

## Themes
- product-team-shape-future: narrow specialists waning, engineer, product and design squeezing together, new graduates squeezed (07:56); a "10X person on a bad team" does not work (1:09:54).
- human-judgment-pm-jobs: taste, decision-making, problem solving "coming back up" (11:00).

## Key points
- Hiring-manager-led decisions, no committee, same-day debrief; paid work trials (53:25, 50:42). Would not transfer to a large regulated employer.
- "Tale of two cities": large offers beside layoffs, a "resettlement of talent and labor" (04:40).
