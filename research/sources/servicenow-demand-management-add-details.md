## Relevance to the project
ServiceNow Strategic Portfolio Management's Demand Management is the intake front door in many large regulated IT organisations: work requests enter as "demands" through a catalog form, are assessed for effort, cost, risk and benefit, and are approved by demand managers before becoming projects, epics or enhancements. It shows that in these organisations a dependency on another unit typically starts life as an intake record in a portfolio process, with a business case and stakeholders, not as a link between two backlog items.

## Themes
- process-governance-load: "Demand managers analyze business demands, approve demands, and create projects and enhancements"; demand tasks gather effort, cost, risk and benefit estimates "needed for a business case" (body).
- platform-onboarding-friction: demands are submitted through the service catalog form or the Demand Management app; selecting a portfolio auto-populates the stakeholder list; resource assignments inform approval only when the Resource Management Workspace is active.
- dependency-types-and-practices: demand managers work with business relationship managers to identify stakeholders and gather requirements and risks, link requirements to project tasks and track them; demands link to RIDAC records (risks, issues, decisions, actions, change requests).
