## Relevance to the project
The strongest evidence in the commercial PM-agent case file: 11 named practitioners, written by Teresa Torres herself (not a guest post), with candid failures. The article states Lovable had no input and neither the author nor Product Talk was compensated. ## Key points
- Teresa Torres / Product Talk (Aug 13, 2025), 11 named practitioners; article says no compensation and Lovable had no input.
- Results (all self-reported): EQS Group (Moritz Homann) retention prototype in about an hour vs a week traditionally; TimelyCare (Jared Stephens) interactive concept in six hours over a weekend; Kallidus (Phill Lord-David) existing UI recreated in five minutes and four prompts, enhanced skills feature shipped; Aarthi Shankaran 11 days idea to user validation, prototype in two hours; "up to 75%" setup-time reduction is attributed to the cloning concept, not a measured result.
- Limits: brand/visual inconsistency; breakage as complexity grows; Supabase login slowed from ~0.5s to 25s at TimelyCare; a student (Princess Edo-Osagie) abandoned the Supabase integration for her demo (not Lovable as a whole); Kallidus PM spent an hour and ten prompts on graph text alignment and the tool claimed fixes it had not made; TimelyCare prototype looked so real colleagues asked when it would ship (he switched to a 90-second video); about half the teams moved design work to Figma, Stitch, a design system or a designer for production quality. Cursor was only suggested (EQS), not reported as adopted.
- Case 18 rating MED. Jobs: prototypes for intent shaping, stakeholder alignment, concept tests.
