## Relevance to the project
The first major supervisor guideline to name generative AI and AI agents explicitly and to tie controls to a materiality assessment and an AI inventory. It is the clearest published model for what a bank must hold to put agents in a regulated workflow: inventory, materiality tiers, lifecycle controls and human oversight proportionate to risk.

## Themes
- agent-stewardship: board and senior-management oversight, AI inventory and materiality by impact, complexity and reliance
- evals-autonomy-trust: lifecycle controls (testing, monitoring, change management, human oversight) scaled to assessed risk

## Key points
- Consultation paper issued 2025-11-13, comments closed 2026-01-31; applies to all financial institutions, proportionately.
- Three areas: oversight; systems, policies and procedures (identify AI use, keep an accurate inventory, assess materiality by impact, complexity and reliance); lifecycle controls and capabilities and capacity (data, fairness, explainability, human oversight, third-party risk, testing, monitoring, change management).
- Explicitly covers generative AI and AI agents. Secondary summaries say final guidelines and a response to feedback are dated 2026-10-07, with expectations effective 2027-10-07 and a further 2027 consultation on agentic guidance; not verified against the PDF.
