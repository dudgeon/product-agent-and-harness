## Relevance to the project
The only survey numbers found on how fresh and trusted internal software-ownership metadata is: half of engineers do not strongly trust the data in their central system of record, and 53% of orgs update metadata repositories no more than weekly. If humans do not trust these records, a dependency agent reading them will inherit the error at speed. Survey by a portal vendor, sample size not stated.

## Themes
- stale-records-mislead-agents: "53% of organizations do not update their metadata repositories more than once per week"; only 6% of engineers update asset metadata manually daily.
- agent-legible-platform: 25% of orgs still use a CMDB and 17% spreadsheets as metadata store; 53% have implemented an internal developer portal, 23% specifically as metadata repository.

## Key points
- Trust differs by role: 56% of engineering managers vs 46% of developers trust legacy metadata repositories.
- From Port's "2025 State of Internal Developer Portals".
