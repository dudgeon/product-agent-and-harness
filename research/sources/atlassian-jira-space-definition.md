## Relevance to the project

Defines Atlassian's unit of 'project' in Jira, and shows the vocabulary has shifted: 'space' for project, 'work item' for issue. A meta-harness choosing its own 'project' primitive should note that even the largest vendor has no stable name and splits the concept in two (Jira space vs. Home 'project').

## Themes

- work-structure-spectrum: team-managed (each team controls its own config) vs company-managed (admin-standardised) is a built-in structured-vs-flexible dial.

## Key points

- 'A Jira space is a collection of related work items' for a product, team, service or project; items carry a space key prefix (e.g. GDT-1).
- Spaces come with preset work types, workflows, screens and fields, all customisable.
- Team-managed: simple, controlled per team, anyone can create. Company-managed: shared admin-set configuration, only admins create.
- Software spaces (scrum, kanban, bug tracking templates) vs business spaces (HR, marketing, finance).
- Epic/story/task hierarchy is not defined on this page.
