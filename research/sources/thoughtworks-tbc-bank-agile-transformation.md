## Relevance to the project
A bank that adopted tribes and squads in 2019 reports that the "high interdependence among our 140 squads" prolonged time to market for products that spanned business units. Its response was organisational, not tooling: stronger portfolio management, a stronger product-owner role paired with a tech lead, and re-aligning tribes and squads to product domains, piloted with one end-to-end product team. Evidence that the squad model in banks moves the dependency problem rather than removing it.

## Themes
- coordination-cost-and-waiting: "high interdependence among our 140 squads" led to "prolonged time-to-market for products spread across various business units" (interview).
- platform-as-a-product: re-alignment of tribes and squads to product domains and decoupled teams as the fix.

## Key points
- Three strategic bets: portfolio management, product-owner role, alignment to product domains.
- Pilot: a "vertical thin slice" on the Subscription product with a dedicated end-to-end team, which overachieved its objectives about six months later (only figure given besides 140 squads).
- Supported by an Agile Transformation Office.
