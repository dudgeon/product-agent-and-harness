## Relevance to the project
The largest survey so far of knowledge workers on whether they still think critically when using generative AI. Two findings matter for the harness: confidence in the AI predicts less critical thinking, self-confidence in the task predicts more; and critical thinking does not vanish but moves to verification, integration and "task stewardship". That is the empirical basis for designing PM skills that keep the human's self-confidence and verification role intact rather than replacing them.

## Themes
- overreliance-and-deskilling: higher confidence in GenAI is associated with less critical thinking; higher task self-confidence with more (abstract).
- verification-capacity-gap: the work of critical thinking shifts from doing to "information verification", response integration and task stewardship (abstract).
- human-thinking-skills: the authors frame design challenges around supporting those three shifted activities (abstract, inferred by the fetch model; the page itself lists no specific recommendations).

## Key points
- 319 knowledge workers, 936 first-hand examples of GenAI use in work tasks; peer-reviewed at CHI 2025.
- Both task-specific and user-specific factors predict whether critical thinking is enacted and how much effort it takes.
