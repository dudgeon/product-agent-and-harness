## Relevance to the project
A law-firm reading of the 2026 Regulation B final rule that matters for experimentation in cards and unsecured lending: it removes the effects test, but advises that proxy-variable risk, state law, FHA exposure for mortgage and private suits persist, so test governance should not be dismantled.

## Themes
- experiment-decision-rules: advice: keep model governance, documentation and explainability, especially for proxy variables; non-mortgage lenders may scale formal disparate impact testing but a full retreat is unlikely to be prudent

## Key points
- Final rule published 2026-04-22, effective 2026-07-21: ECOA does not provide for disparate impact liability; effects-test language removed.
- Disparate treatment still prohibited, including facially neutral proxies; private ECOA suits, Fair Housing Act and state laws (e.g. Unruh Act) keep exposure alive; litigation against the rule is likely.
- Discouragement narrowed to express statements; for-profit special purpose credit programs cannot use race, colour, national origin or sex as eligibility criteria.
