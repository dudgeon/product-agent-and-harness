## Relevance to the project
An Amazon senior TPM job description states the role's dependency work in Amazon's own words: plan for dependencies, secure resources, coordinate release schedules across "multiple disparate groups", build "mechanisms", anticipate bottlenecks and run escalation. It is a first-party definition of the TPM as the human dependency carrier, from a beacon-scale company rather than a regulated one; other Amazon postings found in search (not read in full) ask for experience with "matrixed dependencies" and an "integrated program plan covering the critical path, cross-team dependencies, decisions, resources, risks, and release readiness".

## Themes
- dependency-knowledge-carriers: "plan for dependencies, budget for and secure resources to ensure the meeting of agreed upon deliverables and timelines"; "anticipating bottlenecks, providing escalation management and communicating status broadly across all levels" (description).
- dependency-types-and-practices: "Create mechanisms to drive timely, accurate, and comprehensive project execution"; "define processes that improve teamwork, communication, collaboration, and commitment across multiple disparate groups"; required experience "building processes and coordinating release schedules" (description and qualifications).

## Key points
- Team: Ring, Blink and Amazon Key; Bellevue, WA. Posting date not shown.
- "Mechanisms" is Amazon's term for repeatable processes with owners and inspection, which is the shape a dependency agent's standardisation work would take.
