## Relevance to the project
An industry-body reading of the April 2026 MRM revision: the carve-out of GenAI and agentic AI removes the anchor banks used to justify validating these systems, but not other obligations. It helps estimate what a bank's second line can still rely on when agents draft or act.

## Themes
- regulatory-drivers-of-process: obligations that still bind: fair lending law, Reg B, FCRA adverse action, NYDFS Part 500, FFIEC third-party expectations, state laws; UK firms still have SS1/23

## Key points
- Reads the carve-out as a sign SR 11-7 was the wrong framework, not as a retreat; the RFI is an opening for industry input.
- Advises practitioners to stop citing SR 11-7 for GenAI validation and cite consumer-protection law, third-party risk and internal governance instead.
- Engineering points still stand: define the model boundary; prefer deterministic, transparent inference where possible.
