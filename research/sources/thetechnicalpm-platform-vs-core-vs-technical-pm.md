## Relevance to the project
Single Substack, with job postings as supporting evidence, defining platform and technical PM roles. Basis for persona P4.

## Themes
- **pm-persona-role-expansion**: Platform PM defines and prioritises internal tools, systems and APIs; metrics are developer adoption, reliability and cost; Technical PM writes detailed technical requirements for engineering ('owns developer experience' is not on the page).

## Key points
- Platform PM: internal tools, systems, APIs; liaises between core PMs and engineering.
- Metrics: developer adoption, reliability, cost.
- Technical PM: 'Write detailed technical requirements for engineering teams'. Developer experience is not attributed to the technical PM (closest: platform PM 'improving developer productivity').
