## Relevance to the project
The source for the often-quoted line that Airbnb's support agent is "built on 13 different models that have been tuned off of tens of thousands of conversations". Chesky explains why support came first (high stakes, hallucination intolerable) and the plan to make the agent take actions, then plan trips. He also says Airbnb is open to AI-agent integrations but will not be booked as a commodity by third-party agents.

## Themes
- cost-control-routing: one custom agent on 13 models (Chesky, Aug 2025).
- ai-synthesis-hallucination-risk: "the risk of hallucination is very, very high and you cannot have a high hallucination rate" in support.
- evals-autonomy-trust: next steps: know which reservation, cancel it, then search and book the next trip.
- build-vs-buy: "it's not enough to just have like the best model. You have to be able to tune the model and build a custom interface".

## Key points
- Letter (Aug 2025): AI support agent at 100% of US users; share of hosts and guests needing a human agent down 15%; more countries and languages later in 2025.
- Call: AI comes into travel search "next year" (2026); Airbnb is transitioning "from a pre-generative AI app to an AI native app".
- CFO: the rebuilt tech stack and infrastructure investment improved the developer environment, "increasingly aided by AI".
- On third-party agents: booking requires a verified account and messaging, so "I don't think that we're going to be the kind of thing where you just have an agent or operator book your Airbnb for you".
