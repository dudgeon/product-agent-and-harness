## Relevance to the project
Matched survey of 81 triads (retiree, supervisor, successor) at a technology firm, before and after departure. Retirees' knowledge-continuity effort made the knowledge more useful to successors, but only where supervisors were seen as transformational and the innovation culture was strong. It suggests that capturing tacit knowledge is a leadership and culture problem, not a tooling one, which matters for T1's cost.

## Themes
- `context-trust-mechanisms`: handover value depended on the supervisor and culture, not on the capture method.

## Key points
- One firm, self-reported usefulness, small n; outcome is successor-perceived usefulness, not firm performance. Read from the abstract page.
