## Relevance to the project
This is Torres's own write-up comparing her interview synthesis with AI synthesis, the companion to the All Things Product podcast. It supplies the quote-accuracy failure rate (ChatGPT got about 30% of direct quotes wrong or missing), which backs the map's rule that a human checks every AI synthesis against source quotes (J1, J10).

## Themes
- **research-synthesis-agents**: She synthesises each interview first and then uses AI as a "fresh perspective"; "Sometimes the AI catches things that I missed."
- **ai-synthesis-hallucination-risk**: ChatGPT got "About 30% of the direct quotes" wrong or missing.
- **human-judgment-pm-jobs**: "The sweet spot seems to be expert human synthesis aided by AI synthesis."

## Key points
- Her method: human synthesis first, AI second as a cross-check.
- AI sometimes surfaces things the human missed.
- Quote fidelity is the main failure: about 30% of direct quotes were wrong or missing in ChatGPT's output.
- She concludes that expert synthesis aided by AI is the sweet spot, not AI alone.
- The article gives no "% of insights missed" figure. The "AI summaries miss 20 to 40%" figure once cited in the map does not come from here.
