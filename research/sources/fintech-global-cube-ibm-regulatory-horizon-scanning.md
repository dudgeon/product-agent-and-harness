## Relevance to the project
Trade-press report of CUBE (a regulatory-intelligence vendor whose RegPlatform claims to extract obligations from regulatory text with NLP) supplying regulatory horizon scanning inside IBM watsonx.governance, so AI governance teams can see regulatory changes, assess impact on AI systems and keep an auditable record of their review. It shows the 2026 shape of the regulatory-intelligence data products a bank already licenses and that a requirements agent could read through: feeds of regulatory change linked to governance objects, sold into GRC and AI-governance platforms (CUBE also partners with Microsoft and ServiceNow).

## Themes
- obligation-libraries-as-data: CUBE claims more than 10 million regulatory datapoints processed a year and more than 250 regulatory and legal experts; the integration surfaces updates from regulators, legislators, standards bodies and industry associations.
- vendor-claims-vs-independent-evidence: all capability and scale figures are the vendor's, relayed by trade press; third-party profiles quote conflicting coverage figures (from 180 to 750 jurisdictions).

## Key points
- The article does not explain how a regulatory change is mapped to a specific AI use case.
- Search results (not fetched) report CUBE's 2025 acquisitions of 4CRisk (AI policy-to-obligation-to-control mapping) and Acin (operational-risk controls data), giving traceability "from regulation to control testing" in A-Team Insight's words.
