## Key points
- Notion Research in partnership with MeasuringU; page says "Notion studied 218 people" in six markets (US, UK, France, Japan, Australia, South Korea), four context-heavy workflows, conducted May 2026.
- Results: 93% of tasks completed successfully with Custom Agents vs 59% without an agent; 38% less time per task; participants also reported work felt more manageable and they felt more confident (self-report).
- Landing page does not name the workflows, report significance tests, randomization, or within/between-subjects design. Full report gated behind a form (work email, name, company, job title, size, country).
- Not PM-specific: tasks are generic knowledge work.
- Also published in other locales, e.g. https://www.notion.com/zh-cn/resources/roi-of-notion-agents (same content).
- Rating MED-LOW.
