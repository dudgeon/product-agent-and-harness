## Relevance to the project
The product lead for OpenAI's Codex and ChatGPT Work describes how she works and where the product is going: "steering, not rowing", agents as persistent coworkers, and multiplayer work with shared agents, which is close to the "Helm" concept's shape. Two claims bear directly on open questions: knowledge work can't be verified by output the way code can, so a PM agent has to show its process, inputs and citations to be trusted (pm-agent-evaluation, review-and-autonomy); and at OpenAI the shareable artifact has moved from docs to "mocks, not docs" while she still writes privately to think (intent-shaping-and-spec). Also confirms that Work mode is the Codex harness with a different UI, and that the goal is for the system to pick the harness and model. First-party on OpenAI's practice and product direction; vendor interest is obvious.

## Themes
- pm-job-stable-methods-change: the PM core is unchanged: find the "eigenquestion", form the sharpest hypothesis, test fast, feed back; the "trappings" (running execution, docs, presentations) are falling away, and EMs, engineers, data scientists and designers now all work this loop (9:19).
- human-judgment-pm-jobs: steering is "making opinionated calls" about direction, which stays human; humans remain the "entity of accountability", "especially in industries and places that are highly regulated" (11:23, 46:19). Writing-as-thinking is never automated; writing-as-reporting is (53:13).
- shared-persistent-sessions: next shift is agents "that are persistent, that feel like teammates", syncing at cadences and reviewing each other's in-progress work; today work with agents is one-on-one, and OpenAI staff were "sending ... screenshots of their Codex threads to each other on Slack" to share how they got a number (16:02-17:37); "steering with other people over a group of agents" (12:54).
- evals-autonomy-trust / outcome-measurement-gap: coding is output-verifiable by tests; knowledge work is not ("I can't simply look at the deck in the end ... and actually believe that"), so ChatGPT Work is being shaped to show in-progress work, citations and inputs; she asks whether the thread, "so suited to coding", is even the right surface for knowledge work (1:05:08).
- spec-becomes-prototype-and-evals: "mocks not docs, or prototypes not docs"; a prototype or A/B result beats a doc because "a long doc is not a signal that you thought through something" any more; she still writes hundreds of docs, for herself (55:04-56:37). Shares docs at 70% so others can shape them (57:29).
- org-context-grounding: cloud agents need prosaic data access and infra; an isolated agent is like a hire "locked into a room" without Docs, Slack or the database (18:47).
- status-update-agents: she happily automates status reports, launch plans and summaries (writing-as-reporting) (53:13, 58:28).
- agent-as-service-many-surfaces: Work mode "under the covers, this is Codex", with coding UI removed; the north star is no toggle: the system picks harness and model from the task (29:50-32:23). Sites (hosted, with a database, private/team/public) replace many docs and sheets as her presentational artifact; available on web, mobile, Codex and Work (49:06-51:25).
- pm-persona-role-expansion: role boundaries dissolve but someone is still the DRI for whether users want it and whether it is high quality (42:48); open question whether craft depth erodes (44:23).

## Key points
- Three eras: chat; agents (so far mostly coding); and next, a "persistent coworker" who gets things done with you, maybe with other people too (0:00, 34:30).
- Build "two to three months" ahead of model capability; staying close to research is how to judge that (0:19, 28:17).
- Stripe vs OpenAI: Stripe's established market rewarded long, rigorous strategy docs; OpenAI's market changes too fast for a 12-month roadmap, so be "prolific and empirical" (6:49-8:23).
- PM job now includes "elevating others' ambitions": asking for the 10x or faster version (24:08). OpenAI memes: "Is this maximally accelerated?" and "Are you mainlining it yet?" (using your own product all day) (25:24).
- The corporate finance team uses Work mode for complex financial models, turning one person's expertise into something the team can execute (30:11). No numbers given.
- Speed risk (from Lenny's takeaways): without a sharp hypothesis teams "blow off course way quicker".
- Shipping early with conviction beats polish in this era, unlike at her previous companies (36:04).
