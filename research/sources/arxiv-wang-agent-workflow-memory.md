## Relevance to the project
The foundational academic method for "traces reveal where to standardise" (catalogue item 7): agents induce reusable routines ("workflows") from their own past trajectories and feed them back as memory, improving relative success by 24.6% on Mind2Web and 51.1% on WebArena. It is evidence that mining repeat work from traces can raise quality, in benchmark web tasks, not organisations. Pair with the 2026 negative result on mining SKILL.md files from GUI trajectories (arxiv-hao-skillmd-trajectory-mining).

## Themes
- skill-improvement-loops: workflows induced from past trajectories and selectively provided to guide later actions; offline (from training examples) and online (on the fly from test queries) (abstract)
- memory-architecture: induced workflows are stored as agent memory, with fewer steps needed to solve WebArena tasks (abstract)
- org-learning-loop: online AWM beat baselines by 8.9 to 14.0 absolute points as train-test gaps widened across tasks, websites and domains, suggesting reuse generalises beyond the exact task (abstract)

## Key points
- Benchmarks only; no organisational deployment.
- A 2026 survey (arXiv 2605.07358) groups this line of work as "procedural induction and packaging" with AWM as the representative case (search summary).
