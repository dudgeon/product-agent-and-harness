## Relevance to the project
The largest and most independent survey in the set (1,750 respondents across roles, newsletter-run, self-selected readers). It gives the clearest per-task data on how PMs use AI today (PRDs, prototypes, comms) and where demand is unmet (user research, prototyping), which directly informs which PM jobs to build agents for.

## Themes
- **pm-ai-usage-surveys**: Top PM AI uses: writing PRDs 21.5%, mockups/prototypes 19.8%, communication 18.5%; user research 4.7%, roadmap ideas 1.1%. Largest demand gap is user research (+27.2 points); prototyping desired use rises 19.8% to 44.4%.
- **pm-ai-usage-surveys**: 63% of PMs and 83% of founders report saving 4+ hours/week; over 70% of PMs and founders (combined) report quality gains; designers, the most sceptical group, 47.5% save 4+ hours; engineers' top use is writing code (51% current). More than half of all respondents save at least half a day per week on their most important tasks.
- **human-judgment-pm-jobs**: The write-up says AI helps PMs produce but lags in helping them think; strategic and discovery work near the bottom of use.
- **pm-persona-role-expansion**: Founders report highest satisfaction, with productivity and decision support as top use (32.9%); designers use AI most for research synthesis (22.3%) and content (17.4%).

## Key points
- 1,750 respondents across roles; newsletter-run, self-selected readers.
- PM uses: PRDs 21.5%, mockups/prototypes 19.8%, communication 18.5%, user research 4.7%, roadmap ideas 1.1%.
- Largest demand gap: user research (+27.2 points between wanted and current use). Prototyping desired use 19.8% to 44.4%.
- 63% of PMs save 4+ hours/week; over 70% of PMs and founders together report quality gains. Founders 83% save 4+ hours. Designers 47.5% save 4+ hours. Engineers: writing code is the top use (51% current).
- Prototyping's desired-use rise is described as a +24.6pp swing.
- Headline: more than half of respondents say AI saves them at least half a day per week on their most important tasks.
- Write-up characterisation: AI helps PMs produce but lags in helping them think; strategy and discovery near the bottom.
- Founders: highest satisfaction, productivity and decision support top use at 32.9%. Designers: research synthesis 22.3%, content 17.4%.
