## Relevance to the project
The CTO's view of the engineer's job as AI writes the code: the line between product and engineering thinking thins and "may disappear entirely"; the valued engineer can say "This is the wrong spec." This bears on target user and role design for a product harness: Airbnb's technical leader expects engineers to own intent, not just implement it.

## Themes
- pm-persona-role-expansion: "the rise of the engineer-product thinker": if AI turns intent into code, the engineer's main job is defining intent precisely.
- human-judgment-pm-jobs: "Code was always the medium. Judgment was always the craft."; taste as a technical skill: spotting what is subtly wrong or fragile in AI output.
- pm-job-stable-methods-change: deciding what should be built, and why, stays scarce even as writing code becomes cheap.

## Key points
- Three shifts: engineer-product thinker; systems thinking over code fluency; taste as a technical skill.
- Advice: fluency with AI tools is baseline; invest in communication, product intuition, decisions under ambiguity.
- Pairs with his later rule that every engineer must be able to explain an AI-generated PR (latent-space-airbnb-al-dahle-inside-out-ai).
