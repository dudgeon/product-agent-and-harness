## Relevance to the project
An on-site account of the May 2025 relaunch event (Services, rebuilt Experiences, new app), with the CFO's explanation of how Experiences was paused and re-planned, and the reaction. It shows the "release as keynote" model in action and that the business case (billion-dollar revenue lines in three to five years) was set by the CEO and CFO. Useful for how Airbnb launches are staged and how a multi-year pause fed a re-plan.

## Themes
- narrative-first-intent: the launch was a Chesky keynote with a new tagline ("Now you can Airbnb more than an Airbnb"), celebrity-hosted "Originals", and a surprise experience for press; Fortune calls it his "founder mode" persona on display.
- one-roadmap-functional-org: CFO Ellie Mertz: Experiences was launched, scaling, then "The pandemic hit, we put it on the back burner, and haven't really done anything with it until this point"; the "multi-year pause" allowed a reimagining (pricing, vetting, redesigned app). Consistent with the functional model's limit: only as many things as the CEO can focus on.

## Key points
- Ambition (Mertz): build the new businesses into "billion dollar revenue streams over an order of magnitude, in a three-to-five-year period"; 2025 "is about launching". Airbnb revenue 2024: $11.1 billion (company figure).
- Critics questioned whether people will use Airbnb for everyday services at home and questioned marketplace pricing; Mark Pincus called the keynote "Steve Jobs-esque".
- Author's doubt: whether "singular, intimate experiences" can be curated at scale.
