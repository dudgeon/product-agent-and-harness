## Relevance to the project
A current UK local-government template shows what a RAID log (risks, assumptions, issues, dependencies) actually contains in public-sector programme governance: a dependency is "external reliance", every item needs an owner, next action and date, items are scored probability times impact into a RAG status, triaged weekly, reviewed monthly by the programme board, and closed or re-baselined at gateways. It is a concrete schema for the artifact a dependency agent would populate and keep fresh, and its "hygiene" checks are the rules such an agent could enforce.

## Themes
- dependency-types-and-practices: taxonomy (§4): "Risk (uncertain future event), Assumption (believed condition), Issue (happening now), Dependency (external reliance)"; cross-authority engagement, MoUs, evidence commissioning and data sharing are each named as sources of dependencies (§2).
- process-artifacts-as-coordination: "The RAID Log is the single place to record and manage Risks, Assumptions, Issues, and Dependencies" (§1); monthly pack includes "Top 10 RAID items", new and closed counts, a "dependency heatmap" and a one-page narrative of decisions needed (§6).
- process-governance-load: roles (§3.1) are a RAID owner who updates before gateways and monthly reporting, theme leads who own entries, and an SRO/programme board who approves escalations; cadence (§3.2) is weekly triage, monthly board review, gateway reviews.

## Key points
- Fields (§7): type, ID, title, description, theme, category, probability 1-5, impact 1-5, score, RAG, response (avoid/reduce/transfer/accept), mitigations, owner, due date, status, trend, trigger/threshold, linked process, linked change ID, source/references, last updated.
- RAG thresholds (§4): Green 1-6, Amber 7-14, Red 15-25. Impact scale ties to slip and budget: e.g. 3 = 1-3 months slip or 2-5% budget; 5 = more than 6 months or more than 10% budget (§4.1).
- Lifecycle (§5): New, Assessed, Mitigation agreed, In review, Closed with rationale; "unresolved Reds require an escalation path at the next gateway".
- Hygiene checks (§8): every item has owner, next action and target date; mitigations are "verbs + dates + owners, not statements of intent"; closed items include rationale and evidence link.
