## Relevance to the project
Simulation (synthetic restaurant and service data) of consumer-side Assistant agents and business-side Service agents searching, negotiating and transacting. Frontier models approach optimal welfare only under ideal search, performance degrades sharply at scale, and all show severe first-proposal bias (speed worth 10-30 times quality). It is the nearest rigorous evidence on agents bargaining with other parties' agents, and it is not production evidence.

## Themes
- `remote-agent-invocation`: agent-to-agent bargaining is studied in simulation only; first-proposal bias means the first responder wins.

## Key points
- Simulation with synthetic data; manipulation results not seen in the abstract I read.
