## Relevance to the project
Chesky's own account, on launch day, of how the 2025 app relaunch was made: research on guests, multi-year evaluation of experience quality in 100+ cities, recruiting tastemakers and hosts, and Chesky sitting with the product teams "every single day". It is a rare description of the research-to-intent step for a big Airbnb bet, and it confirms that the CEO was the daily design reviewer.

## Themes
- leadership-review-cadence: "I basically sat with these teams who were developing these products every single day, poring over these problems"; "Every day there was a new design problem that I had to solve".
- research-synthesis-agents (human version): "We did a lot of research on our guests and we wanted to provide them with all the same services [of] a hotel"; "We've spent years evaluating what makes a good-quality, unique and authentic experience in each city. We did this in over 100 cities across 30 countries."
- opportunity-to-code-chain: insight (hotels win on services; people's scarcest asset is time) to research to supply recruiting (tastemakers, then "tens of thousands" of hosts, handpicked and vetted for licences) to a rebuilt app with three tabs, host tools, and itineraries.

## Key points
- Learnings from the 2016 Experiences launch: needed to be "more affordable, more unique to Airbnb and... merchandised correctly".
- Launch scope: Services in ten categories; Experiences in 650+ cities; "Airbnb Originals" (formerly Icons) with celebrities.
- Host side rebuilt too: new "today" tab, day/hour calendar, tools for services and experiences.
- Profile with "over 200 million verified identities" (Chesky's figure); app "becoming more of a social network in the real world".
- Design direction: away from flat design to animated, dimensional, colourful UI.
- Expected "100,000 applications" to host services and experiences after launch (Chesky's prediction).
