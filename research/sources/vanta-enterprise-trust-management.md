## Relevance to the project
Vanta's enterprise page is a representative vendor claim for automated compliance evidence: pull data from "400+ tools", run continuous controls monitoring, automate audit prep, vendor security reviews and questionnaires, across SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, HITRUST, DORA, FedRAMP and others. It shows how far commercial tooling claims to have automated the evidence side of governance gates, which bounds what a dependency agent would need to build versus read. All figures are vendor-reported testimonials.

## Themes
- governance-as-code: "Automate audit prep and evidence collection"; continuous controls monitoring via automated tests; "Automatically pull data from 400+ tools".
- platform-onboarding-friction: "Manage vendor onboarding and security reviews in one place"; a healthcare customer (Vibrent Health) says vendor security assessments took "50 hours per vendor", reduced to a few hours a week (testimonial).
- process-governance-load: an auditor partner (A-LIGN) says customers "reduce their audit completion times by 50%"; Snowflake and Samsara "Automated 93% of questionnaires"; Ramp "2,000 hrs. saved annually" (testimonials).

## Key points
- Claims "16,000+ customers, from startup to enterprise".
- Frameworks listed include SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA, HITRUST, NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001, CMMC, NIS2, DORA, CPS 234, EU AI Act, FedRAMP.
- Named customers skew to technology firms; the page does not name large banks or insurers.

## Could not verify
- None of the outcome figures are independently verified; test counts and integration counts vary across Vanta pages (300+ to 400+ integrations).
