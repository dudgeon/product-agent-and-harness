## Relevance to the project
A monday.com PM's practical account of the "project as PM memory" pattern: one ChatGPT Project per initiative, with custom "personality" instructions, fed hundreds of exported files (Slack channels saved as PDF, docs pages, decks, Reddit CSVs) and the PM's own finished artifacts fed back in so each new thread starts current. It is evidence for what PMs do today in the absence of a harness: manual export-to-PDF as the connector layer, and a per-initiative container as the unit of context. Bears directly on context-and-memory and artifact-and-project-structure, and on the film's "persistent sessions" idea (here persistent but single-user and manually fed). Anecdote from one PM; the outcome claim is his own.

## Themes
- compounding-context-repo: finished PRDs, overview docs and decks are downloaded and fed back into the Project so "each new thread you open will be up to date with you on your work. It becomes a living thing" (Step 2). The screenshot of his Project shows its contents "after hundreds of threads".
- org-context-grounding: context lives in "Slack channels, Notion pages, Monday boards, decks, Google Docs"; the realisation that "everything is essentially text" (Step 2).
- pm-tool-connectivity: the connector layer is manual: export or copy-paste a Slack channel into a doc, save as PDF, upload; Command+P the public docs site to PDF (Step 2). No live integrations used.
- product-artifact-primitives: the per-initiative Project (ChatGPT Projects, Claude Projects, Gemini Gems) with instructions plus files is the container (Step 1; Appendix).
- research-synthesis-agents: fed interviews, market research and feedback threads, the Project "surfaced the key pain point": users "weren't blocked by capability but by confidence. They needed scaffolding, not features" (Context is the new interface).
- status-update-agents: "tailored communications": the same initiative rewritten for marketing blurbs, three QBR sentences for leadership, an enablement deck, a user-testing script, removing the "reload cost" (Step 3).
- human-judgment-pm-jobs: "You can't outsource judgment or creativity ... You still make every decision" (Context is important).

## Key points
- **Setup** (Step 1): ask ChatGPT to write the Project's instructions for a thought partner that is "sharp, smart, fun, and not always agreeing", pushes back and is "a pro at product management".
- **Inputs** (Step 2): for his first initiative (monday.com's first AI agent/co-pilot), a few colleagues' decks, documentation pages as PDFs, and CSVs of Reddit threads about monday.com, AI and competitors.
- **Uses** (Step 3): waitlist sign-up form questions designed to verify the pain point; Lovable prompts generated from the Project's context plus annotated Figma screenshots, giving a working prototype in about ten minutes to show designer and engineer before building; audience-tailored comms.
- **"Context is the new interface"**: working memory limits a PM to five or six ideas; context held outside the head lets you "reason better".
- Same pattern works in Claude Projects and Gemini Gems (Appendix).
