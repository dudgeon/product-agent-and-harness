## Relevance to the project
Asana's Launch Planner teammate is the closest shipped vendor description of a cross-department dependency agent: it "calculates the critical path across the Work Graph", forecasts how a slip in one department affects another outside the same project view, and "If a dependency is threatened, it automatically alerts both owners and proposes a re-aligned schedule." The page gives no detail on how dependencies are found (declared vs inferred), how alerts are delivered, or how proposals are approved, and no Launch Planner outcome figures.

## Themes
- dependency-detection-automation: "maps the entire multi-departmental ecosystem"; addresses launches that "miss deadlines or fail due to invisible dependencies".
- agent-originated-requests: alerts go to both owners of a threatened dependency, with a proposed schedule.
- legible-org-for-agents: the only named input is Asana's Work Graph; anything outside it is invisible to the teammate.
- vendor-claims-vs-independent-evidence: only general beta results across 200+ organisations (e.g. "2x faster") are quoted, not tied to Launch Planner.

## Key points
- 21 teammates at launch, including Launch Planner, Status Reporter, Decision Tracker, Compliance Specialist, Spec Reviewer, Sprint Coach.
- Add-on to Starter, Advanced, Enterprise and Enterprise+; contact sales; no price listed. See also claude-customers-asana-ai-teammates.
