## Relevance to the project
A practitioner essay on how platform teams decay into "a service desk in disguise" when they are measured on tickets closed: engineers move Jira tickets instead of building reusable self-service, consumers see the platform as bureaucracy and build workarounds, which feeds shadow IT. It proposes a ticket-to-outcome ratio as a health signal. Useful mainly as a named description of the TicketOps failure mode and its link to shadow IT.

## Themes
- platform-as-a-product: causes include stakeholders treating platforms as support services, and metrics that reward closed tickets over outcomes.
- platform-onboarding-friction: teams "build workarounds", fuelling shadow IT, when the platform feels like bureaucracy.

## Key points
- Remedies: a clear charter; outcome metrics (developer satisfaction, deployment time); split work into keep-the-lights-on, enablement and strategic bets; build self-service "knobs" instead of fulfilling one-off requests; protect strategic time (a ticket-free sprint each quarter); retrain stakeholders.
- Ticket-to-outcome ratio: high means service desk, low means leverage.
- Figures in the article (e.g. onboarding tickets down 40%) are illustrative examples, not a study.
