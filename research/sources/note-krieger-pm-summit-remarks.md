## Relevance to the project
Independent attendee write-up: Anthropic's Mike Krieger first asked whether Claude made a PM unnecessary, then told the PM a week later "You were 100% right" after the PM handled customers from individuals to large enterprises, customer-success explanations and involving safety staff. Stripe's Katie Dill: LLMs are good at the most likely answers, so teams need their own perspective and codified standards. "Every bet needs one decision owner" and "make editing a job" are NOT in this write-up; they come from the Product Management Society write-up.
