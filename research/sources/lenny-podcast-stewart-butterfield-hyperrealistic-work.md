## Relevance to the project
A pre-agent diagnosis of why organisations fill with coordination work: headcount grows faster than "known valuable work", and people generate meetings and deck previews that look like work. It bears on the belief that agents can take the coordination that fills most of product management, because it says the coordination is partly manufactured and could be regenerated by agents unless leaders curate the work.

## Themes
- pm-coordination-work-automated: "hyper-realistic work-like activities" are coordination that looks identical to work (01:03:14-01:06:34); the fix is leader clarity on priorities, not scolding (01:07:48).
- product-team-shape-future: every manager wants to hire a junior to do the level below; no budget process concludes "hire fewer" (00:56:57).
- taste-and-homogenisation: taste can be trained like a chef's (00:15:49); essential basics (sign-up, forgot password) rarely get love but form emotional connection (00:14:34).

## Key points
- Known valuable work: early in a company everyone has 10 things they know are valuable; as hiring grows, supply shrinks and people create their own (01:03:14-01:05:27).
- Parkinson's law applied to organisations: staff count tracks administrators, not the work (00:54:25).
- Utility S-curve and divine discontent: standards rise, so quality bars move; invest where the curve is steep (00:07:08, 00:10:13).
- No AI claims in this episode; recorded before the 2026 agent products.

## Notable quotes
