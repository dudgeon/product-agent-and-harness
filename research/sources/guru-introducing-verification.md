## Relevance to the project
The clearest product example of expiry and re-verification for knowledge cards: every card has a named verifier and a verification interval; when the interval lapses the card shows as needing verification and the verifier is notified; a trust indicator shows when it was last verified. It bears on the expiry-and-re-verification mechanism and on cost to the owner. It also shows a hybrid: verifiers who edit or share their cards are treated as trusted without explicit verification, i.e. use by the owner counts as re-verification. No outcome data is given.

## Themes
- context-trust-mechanisms: verifier (default the creator) plus interval (default one month in this post); Guru notifies verifiers "whenever their cards become stale"; each card has a trust indicator showing when it was last verified or why it needs verification (sections on verifier, interval and trust indicator).
- stale-records-mislead-agents: three actions on a stale card: verify unchanged, edit (which re-verifies), or archive or reassign (section on verifying a card).

## Key points
- Group verifiers: if a group is the verifier, the task sits in every member's queue until one verifies (section on groups). Shared ownership spreads the cost but blurs accountability.
- Owner activity counts as verification: "As verifiers edit or share their cards, they are considered Trusted and will not require explicit verification." Lower owner cost, but sharing a card is weak evidence it is still correct.
- Changing a verifier puts the card back to unverified until the new verifier confirms (section on changing verifiers). Ownership transfer resets trust.
- Claimed cost: verifying "only takes 1-2 minutes" (vendor's own figure, unmeasured).
- The interval is a judgement per card: Guru's own guidance elsewhere says lengthen the interval for evergreen content and shorten it when much changed (Guru verification checklist card, seen only in search results). Community posts seen in search results mention a three-month default and repeated reminders after long custom dates; not verified.
