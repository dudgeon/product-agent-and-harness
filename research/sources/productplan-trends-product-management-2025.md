## Relevance to the project
The only survey evidence found on PM tool sprawl, and it measures consolidation intent, not number of tools or where artifacts live. Vendor survey, small sample.

## Themes
- pm-tool-connectivity: more respondents than 2024 prefer to consolidate; top reasons standardising tools across teams, budget, data silos/poor visibility; strategy, roadmapping, prioritisation most often wanted in one tool.
- vendor-claims-vs-independent-evidence: vendor-run survey of nearly 400 product professionals; survey dates and exact percentages for consolidation not given. 92% 'somewhere on AI journey', 46% adopted for at least one use case.
