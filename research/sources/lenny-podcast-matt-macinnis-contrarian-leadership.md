## Relevance to the project
Leadership-heavy episode with little on AI in product work. Of use for the large-organisation side: a 1,300-person R&D org run by a "product quality list" ship gate that grows a line every time a defect escapes, and the explicit trade-off that process lowers variance but suppresses upside. He says AI is "just a new way to flip bits" and that owning the data is the moat.

## Themes
- checklist-implementation-fidelity: a product quality list is the factory-inspection gate; a missed feature flag led to a new line in the list (30:01-32:37).
- governance-as-code: "processes exist for the sole purpose of lowering beta [variance]... The downside is that it suppresses alpha"; apply with judgment (27:55).
- product-team-shape-future: every product person from junior to executive gets the same hard case study with a level-graded rubric (37:03); the product function had become "locally optimized but globally incoherent" (19:51).
- launch-readiness-review: each new product is shipped first to the CEO as the internal admin of the company's own product (30:50).

## Key points
- Bundling thesis: point solutions lack the cross-domain data AI needs; those that rent the model and the data have poor economics (1:18:18-1:20:25).
- Uses AI for essay phrasing; "80% of what it outputs is trash" (1:24:37); has little experience with Claude (1:26:05).
