## Relevance to the project
An early, simple example of the feedback-synthesis job for a product team: a community manager puts feedback into a Claude Project, gets a structured report, refines it and sends it to PMs weekly, plus a competitor-sentiment dashboard. It shows the baseline "chat plus Project" form of the job (human in the loop, links back to sources) against which agentic versions can be compared. Evidence is thin: a short Anthropic customer page from the Claude 3.5 Sonnet era with one headline number ("95% feedback accuracy") whose method is not given.

## Themes
- feedback-triage-agents: weekly community feedback reports for the product team generated from multiple sources (section "Choosing Claude").
- research-synthesis-agents: output keeps a high-level view "while still allowing us to link directly to specific feedback sources".
- human-review-gates: "Claude gets it 95% of the way there, and then our team adds final refinements before sending the report to the product managers."

## Key points
- Workflow: choose a Project, upload a PDF, receive a structured report "in seconds"; human refines before sending to PMs.
- Second workflow: dashboard tracking and comparing user sentiment across competitors.
- Claimed outcomes (Sourcegraph's, unquantified): faster analysis, regional performance issues identified and addressed, better retention via faster resolution.
- Product: Claude for Work / Claude Enterprise; Sourcegraph also builds on Claude for its Cody assistant (so a vendor-partner relationship).
- Limits: no date, no volumes, no description of sources or how accuracy was measured; a single quoted person.
