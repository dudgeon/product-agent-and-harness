## Relevance to the project
Block's investor-facing statement of its internal agent strategy: one "general AI agent" (goose) integrated with "about 150 services" for every function, plus g2, a tile-based interface built on goose where non-technical staff create persistent, autonomous workflows. It is the clearest first-hand evidence that Block runs one general harness plus a persistent-automation layer, and that the same "agentic substrate" powers customer agents (Moneybot, Managerbot). Numbers are investor claims without method.

## Themes
- single-agent-many-skills: "we have developed our own general AI agent, which is tailored to our needs and integrates with every software and data layer" (p.1); goose integrated with "about 150 services" via MCP (p.2).
- non-engineer-builders: HR, customer operations and legal use the general agent (p.1); g2 lets "any non technical person" build a custom application in minutes; each tile "contains a persistent application that works on the user behalf, continuously and asynchronously" (p.2-3).
- build-vs-buy: shared agentic substrate reused for Square AI, Moneybot (MVP in six weeks) and Managerbot (eight weeks) (p.2).
- harness-portability: goose is "foundation model agnostic" (p.2).
- agent-factory-sdlc: tag a bug report and goose prepares a diagnosis and code change "with almost no human in the loop" (p.2).

## Key points
- goose uses cited: analysing internal sales data, building dashboards, managing ticketing work, creating documents, editing video assets (p.2).
- g2 rolled out in Q3 2025 (transcribed as "g2" and "Jitu"); described as a "text to persistent app AI playground".
- Metrics claimed: 25% reduction in manual work hours with AI tools used by 75%+ of employees; AI handles 65% of Cash App support cases; 90%+ of code submissions partly or fully AI-authored; +30% median weekly code changes per engineer through September (p.3).
- Engineering goes "beyond single tools like goose or g2"; AI proficiency added to job ladder and interview rubrics (p.3).
