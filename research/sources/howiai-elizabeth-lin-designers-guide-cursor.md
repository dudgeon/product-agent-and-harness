## Relevance to the project
A design educator demonstrates Cursor for designer prototyping: exploring visual styles from one prompt, interactive prototypes with sound, restyling an "ugly" dashboard, prototypes fed by Notion databases, and Cursor Rules to avoid repeated setup. It is supporting evidence that designers prototype directly in coding agents, and that reusable rules files carry taste and setup between sessions. Downgraded from medium to low: no transcript was available, and the show notes say nothing about PM workflows, handoff, or team practice.
