## Relevance to the project
A tour of AI tools across a discovery sprint (market research, riskiest assumptions, AI-moderated user research, landing pages, prototypes, sharing findings), useful mainly as a map of which discovery jobs PMs were already handing to point tools by late 2025 and a few second-hand company cases: OpenAI's internal assistant that summarises research sessions and pushes insights to stakeholders in Slack, Uber's CPO using NotebookLM to consolidate findings, Amazon using Strella. It frames discovery ("what to build and why") as the skill AI does not erode, which agrees with the project's human-judgment theme. It is a tool roundup with no measured outcomes, and its vendor claims (honesty with AI moderators, 8 weeks to days) are repeated uncritically. Medium: breadth, not depth.

## Themes
- research-synthesis-agents: AI-moderated interview tools (Strella, Askable, Listen) run sessions at scale and produce synthesis; OpenAI's in-house assistant summarises research sessions and sends them to relevant stakeholders via Slack (sections 2 and 5). All second-hand.
- ai-synthesis-hallucination-risk: warns that deep-research tools still fabricate, citing Deloitte's partial refund to the Australian government over invented sources (section 1). Compare [Torres's error analysis](producttalk-ai-hallucination-glossary.md).
- synthetic-users-limits: Askable is described as building a queryable model from real interview data to answer hypothetical market questions, a synthetic-respondent pattern presented without caveat; see [NN/g](nngroup-synthetic-users.md).
- vendor-claims-vs-independent-evidence: repeats a Strella co-founder's claims that research "used to take eight weeks" and that respondents are more honest with an AI moderator.
- prototyping-agents-limits: landing pages (Lovable, Claude Code) with generated imagery and video testimonials for proposition testing; prototypes via Lovable, Google AI Studio's annotate mode, Alloy (a browser extension that builds on a screenshot of your product and pulls specs from Jira/Linear/Notion); a "product playground" of grey-box flows on design tokens.
- status-update-agents: findings shared as Slack summaries, branded decks via skills, NotebookLM audio summaries for execs.

## Key points
- Market analysis: compares deep-research features; recommends meta-prompting (have the model write the research prompt); applying a business book's principles as a lens; AI browsers (Atlas, Comet) either reading open tabs or acting as an agent to fill a Google Sheet; recurring scheduled research (e.g. a weekly changelog sweep of competitors).
- Riskiest assumptions: generate by category (desirability, viability, feasibility, plus usability and ethics), then rank on importance vs knowledge; mentions Shopify's name for it ("Assumption Slam"), second-hand.
- A database of 35+ AI user-research tools is offered.
