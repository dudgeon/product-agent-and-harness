## Relevance to the project
First call after the CTO hire. Chesky gives the adoption figure "More than 80% of engineers are now using AI tools" (Feb 2026), says nearly 30% of English North American support tickets are handled by the AI agent, and explains the hire of Ahmad Al-Dahle (ex-Meta Llama lead) as making Airbnb "much more of an AI-native company". He describes AI work as "rapid iteration, not big launches": AI search was live to a very small share of traffic.
