## Relevance to the project
A US insurer's own slides on removing dependency waits from delivery: Nationwide described teams "waiting for work due to 'discrete' planning processes" and delivery "slowed down by manual and high ceremony processes", then moved to self-managed internal cloud provisioning, after which the share of work requiring a request to another team went from 100% to 0%, alongside "months to hours" speed to market for Java applications. First-party conference slides with no method; the most direct regulated-industry claim that self-service removes request queues.

## Themes
- platform-onboarding-friction: "Work requests required" went from 100% to 0% after moving to self-managed internal cloud provisioning, with patching and configuration initiated by users rather than operations (results slide).
- dependency-value-evidence: speed to market "months to hours" for Java applications; large app releases down 80%; presentation-tier deploys up to 150 a month (results slide).
- process-governance-load: problem framed as "Water-SCRUM-Fall": "Teams waiting for work due to 'discrete' planning processes" (slide 5); moved from readiness-based to time-based releases.

## Key points
- Other changes: separating the presentation tier from the business tier; feature toggles for dark and canary launches.
- Other claimed results: 86% reduction in high defects; 82% increase in user system availability; 80% of agile teams in the top two industry quartiles on QSM benchmarks.
