## Relevance to the project
Reproduces Dorsey's leaked staff email for the March 2025 cut of 931 people (about 8%), which explicitly said the cuts were not about "replacing folks with AI". Eleven months later Block cut over 4,000 and attributed it to AI. The pair is a useful caution on reading AI attributions in layoff messaging, and it shows the flattening (manager layers capped, managers moved to IC) predates the AI framing. Evidence type is first-party because the substance is Dorsey's email as published by press.

## Themes
- ai-flattened-org: 80 managers cut to flatten the org to a maximum depth of "innercore+4", and 193 managers moved to individual-contributor roles; aim "flattening our org so we can move faster and with less abstraction".
- vendor-claims-vs-independent-evidence: "none of the above points are trying to hit a specific financial target, replacing folks with AI, or changing our headcount cap." Compare the February 2026 framing in [block-q4-2025-earnings-prepared-remarks](block-q4-2025-earnings-prepared-remarks.md).

## Key points
- Three buckets: strategy (391 people; off-strategy teams and fixing undefined "discipline ratios"), performance (460 rated "below" or trending there), hierarchy (80 managers).
- 748 open roles closed except offers in progress, critical operations, start/accelerate and key leadership roles.
- Dorsey: "we're behind in our actions", hence acting at once rather than over time.
- Previous large cut: about 1,000 roles in January 2024 (per coverage).

## Could not verify
- "Discipline ratios" is not defined; it may refer to function ratios such as PM-to-engineer, but the email does not say.
