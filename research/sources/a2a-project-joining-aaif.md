## Relevance to the project
The A2A project's own announcement of its move into AAIF, alongside MCP. Confirms that the two protocols now share governance but are kept as separate layers; it says nothing about convergence. Useful as the dated first-party anchor for the governance change.

## Themes
- `remote-agent-invocation`: A2A accepted as a Growth Stage project at AAIF; MCP described as the vertical layer (agent to tools and data) and A2A as the horizontal layer (agent to agent).

## Key points
- Lists native platform support (Google Cloud, Bedrock AgentCore Runtime, Azure AI Foundry), enterprise SaaS (ServiceNow, Salesforce, Atlassian, SAP) and frameworks (LangGraph, CrewAI, Pydantic AI, AG2, IBM BeeAI).
- Claims production use "across supply chains, financial services, and mobile platforms"; gives no named enterprise deployments, volumes or SDK statistics.
- SDKs listed for Python, Go, Java, JS, .NET and Rust.
