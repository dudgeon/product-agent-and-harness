## Relevance to the project
A 77-page map of the field that supplies vocabulary for dimension 7. It splits improvement by what evolves (models, memory, tools, architecture), when (intra-test-time vs inter-test-time) and how (scalar rewards vs textual feedback; single vs multi-agent), and names safety, scalability and co-evolution as open problems. Useful mainly to label where curated-correction skills (inter-test-time, memory, textual feedback) and run-time self-refinement (intra-test-time) sit; abstract only.
