## Relevance to the project
One of the few bank-reported figures on AI for legacy estates: an in-house tool turns old code into plain-English specifications that developers then rewrite from. It matches the idea that specs are recovered from code before new work, and it keeps people in the loop.

## Themes
- agent-factory-sdlc: legacy code converted to plain-English specs, then rewritten by developers; 9 million lines reviewed, about 280,000 developer hours saved in five months (bank's own figures).

## Key points
- Tool built in-house on OpenAI models and trained on the firm's own codebase including firm-specific languages.
- Reported by the firm's head of technology and operations: 9 million lines reviewed and about 280,000 hours saved in the five months after the January 2025 launch, across about 15,000 developers.
- Full automatic translation still limited; human oversight kept for efficient new code.
- Bank said the tool would not take jobs.
