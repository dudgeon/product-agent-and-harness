## Relevance to the project
A small study (N=38) comparing two versions of a co-creative sketching system that differ only in whether the AI can communicate back to the human. Communication improved collaborative experience and engagement and made the AI seem more reliable and intelligent. The relevance is narrow: in alternating co-creation the agent's commentary, not just its contribution, carries the collaboration. For PM skills it supports the agent explaining what it changed and why in a shared document, and is a reminder that "critique" is itself a contribution.

## Themes
- human-thinking-skills: AI-to-human communication made the co-creative AI seem "more reliable, personal, and intelligent" and improved engagement (abstract).
- work-structure-spectrum: an instance of Kantosalo and Toivonen's alternating mode, where the AI contributes sketches as design inspiration during a design task (abstract).

## Key points
- Two prototypes; between-design comparison; measures of perception, collaborative experience and engagement.
- Limits: small sample, drawing task, perception measures rather than output quality.
