## Relevance to the project
First-party account from one Anthropic PM of how the job shifted from alignment work toward product craft, with concrete agent uses (idea pressure-testing, adoption analytics, sentiment monitoring). No quantitative results and no failure discussion; Managed Agents was in beta.
