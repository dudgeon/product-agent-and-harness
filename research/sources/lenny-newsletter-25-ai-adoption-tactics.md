## Relevance to the project
A compact catalogue of adoption practices at six companies (Shopify, Ramp, Zapier, Duolingo, Intercom, Whoop), gathered by Peter Yang in interviews with their leaders. Its value to the project is breadth for the adoption-model dimension and one early, often-repeated PM-agent example: Ramp's AI user personas loaded with research context that review any spec for gaps. The evidence is second-hand (Yang reporting what leaders told him), with a few hard numbers that are the companies' own. Dated August 2025, so it predates the agent-harness wave and reads as a baseline.

## Themes
- enablement-by-product: five-step frame: explain the how, track and reward, cut red tape, turn enthusiasts into teachers, prioritize high-impact tasks (intro); Ramp's cross-functional "AI SWAT team" builds each group's top automation and runs PM vibe-coding offsites (section 4).
- org-learning-loop: Shopify "learning villages" and build-in-the-open; Duolingo two-hour "FriAIdays"; Zapier weekly live demos (60+ attendees) and quarterly hackathons; Whoop counts upleveling others in promotions (section 4).
- synthetic-users-limits / spec-edge-case-review: Ramp "built AI personas loaded with all their user research context. PMs can now give any product spec to these personas and get instant feedback" (section 5). No accuracy or limits discussed.
- outcome-measurement-gap: Ramp publishes power users (5+ actions a week) per team for Cursor, Claude Code, ChatGPT; Intercom uses merged PRs as a proxy, "about 20% year-over-year"; Shopify peer-rates reflexive AI use 1-5 in reviews (section 2). All are input or proxy metrics.
- evals-autonomy-trust: Yang's four questions for any AI product demo: customer problem, better than non-AI, ground-truth dataset and evals, preparation for model failure (section 5).
- non-engineer-builders: a Whoop PM built a prompt evaluation tool across dozens of persona types without engineering; Duolingo vibe-coded a chess lesson "in hours instead of weeks" (section 5).

## Key points
- Framing claim: "The biggest barrier to AI adoption isn't technology; it's organizational change"; cites a Gallup poll that 8% of US workers use AI daily (intro).
- Explain the how: Shopify memo named concrete tactics (AI prototyping in the GSD process); Zapier "code red" in March 2023 with a week off to practise; Intercom CTO goal "2x productivity with AI", spending a week a month embedded in teams (section 1).
- Cut red tape: Duolingo $300 per employee AI budget; Zapier lead PM owns fast-tracking tool approvals with procurement, legal and engineering; Shopify offers many tools (Claude, Perplexity, Cohere, Gemini, Cursor, Copilot, Claude Code) and a shared prompt/agent library (section 3).
- High-impact tasks: Zapier Zendesk triage that flags sales-ready customers; Zapier sales reps save 10 hours/week on lead research; Shopify performance-review prep tool; Duolingo 100 courses in 12 years vs 150 in 12 months (sections 2, 5).

## Notable quotes
- "Instead of focusing on shiny demos, look for rigorous data and evaluations." (section 5)
