## Relevance to the project
Two months after presenting Cortana as its company-wide assistant, Uber rolled out a bought general agent, OpenAI's ChatGPT Work, from a 1,000-person pilot to 30,000+ employees, connected to Gmail, Drive, Calendar and Slack, while engineers keep Codex. This sets up a build-and-buy overlap at the non-engineer "front door" that no source reconciles with Cortana. Bears on build-vs-buy for a company assistant and on which surface owns non-engineering work.

## Themes
- build-vs-buy: company-wide bought agent for office tasks alongside the in-house Cortana; OpenAI's page (as relayed by search snippets) adds an internal AI skills library, approved integrations and training.
- agent-as-service-many-surfaces: ChatGPT Work reaches Slack, Gmail, Drive and Calendar.
- non-engineer-builders: target functions are operations, finance, marketing and product (meeting briefs, slides, data analysis).

## Key points
- Default model GPT-6.1 Sol (about a fifth of the top model's price); Codex continues for engineering.
- CTO Praveen Neppalli Naga quoted on giving everyone tools to innovate; no outcomes reported yet.
- Context given: Uber spent its 2026 Claude Code budget by April; laid off 10% of customer service in July, the first layoff it tied to AI.
