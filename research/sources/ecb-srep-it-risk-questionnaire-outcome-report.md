## Relevance to the project
A supervisor's own view of how change and release management is implemented across 100+ significant euro-area banks. It gives the regulator's list of expected change controls and shows where the gaps were, which bears on what an agent-assisted change process must preserve.

## Themes
- governance-as-code: change-control expectations (release management team, segregation of duties, management approval of every change, emergency procedures) are the regulator's checklist for any redesigned change process.

## Key points
- Over 100 significant institutions answered the 2019 questionnaire (reference year 2018).
- 69 percent reported medium to highly complex IT systems; those institutions had more disruption losses, more critical projects and more end-of-life dependence.
- Most banks rated change and release management controls as effective, but a significant number lacked a release management team, segregation of duties across the change process, approval and scheduling of every change by a management level or committee, authorisation of security control changes, or emergency change procedures.
- Life-cycle gaps included project-independent quality assurance and cross-project dependency management.
- Critical IT audit findings open over a year fell 30 percent year on year.
