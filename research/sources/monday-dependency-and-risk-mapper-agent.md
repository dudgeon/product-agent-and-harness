## Relevance to the project
A packaged monday.com agent that reads a board's items, dependencies, owners, dates and status, traces the critical path and blocked chains each morning, flags at-risk chains "with the reason and a next step", and sends an end-of-day summary. It is representative of the work-management tier: the agent operates over dependencies people already declared on one board, with an approval mode before it acts.

## Themes
- dependency-detection-automation: "Reads items, dependencies, owners, dates, and status from the board"; "Trace the critical path and find blocked chains"; no statement that it infers undeclared links.
- status-update-agents: daily at-risk flags and end-of-day summaries; "Re-map on your cadence".
- human-review-gates: can "wait for your approval before taking any action" or run automatically once trusted (FAQ).

## Key points
- Connectors listed: Slack and monday notetaker; claims "Full org context" without detail.
- monday also markets AI Risk Insights and Portfolio Risk Insights that scan timelines, dependencies and workloads (search summaries, not read).
