## Relevance to the project
Planview, a Gartner-named strategic portfolio management leader with many regulated-enterprise customers, launched "AI-powered dependency intelligence": a graph over 60+ enterprise connectors that maps dependencies from OKRs to tasks and people, with agents that "analyze millions of work connections" and alert "when dependencies shift". It is the most ambitious portfolio-level claim found, and the thinnest on evidence: no customer quotes, no figures, no description of whether dependencies are inferred or declared, or of review and approval.

## Themes
- dependency-detection-automation: "surfacing critical path risks and bottlenecks before they impact delivery"; the release implies inference ("analyze work patterns to uncover blockers") without saying how.
- agent-originated-requests: agents "automatically escalate critical risks" and are "running at all times, alerting you when a bottleneck or dependency needs resolving" (CPO quote).
- vendor-claims-vs-independent-evidence: "the first and only company to deliver enterprise-grade AI for dependency intelligence at this scale"; benefits ("reduce coordination overhead") unquantified.
- legible-org-for-agents: unified data fabric, semantic layer, 60+ connectors, real-time sync; Snowflake, AWS Bedrock and Anthropic named as partners.

## Key points
- Part of Planview Anvi (formerly Planview Copilot), introduced October 2025.
- Leaders can "implement recommendations directly through the interface"; no approval or audit detail.
