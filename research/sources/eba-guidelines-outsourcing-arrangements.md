## Relevance to the project
The EBA outsourcing guidelines (applying from 30 September 2019) set the pre-contract gate and the register that EU banks and payment institutions run for any outsourced function, the predecessor and non-ICT companion to the DORA register. Before any outsourcing the firm must assess criticality, supervisory conditions, risks, due diligence and conflicts of interest; it must keep a register of all arrangements, hand it to supervisors "in a processable electronic form", and tell supervisors in advance about planned outsourcing of critical or important functions. For product teams this is a gate with regulator-visible timing; for a dependency agent the register is a structured source.

## Themes
- regulatory-drivers-of-process: para 61: "Before entering into any outsourcing arrangement, institutions and payment institutions should: a. assess if the outsourcing arrangement concerns a critical or important function...; b. assess if the supervisory conditions for outsourcing ... are met; c. identify and assess all of the relevant risks...; d. undertake appropriate due diligence on the prospective service provider...; e. identify and assess conflicts of interest" (Section 12, p. 39).
- dependency-knowledge-carriers: para 52: "maintain an updated register of information on all outsourcing arrangements"; para 54-55 list minimum fields, adding for critical functions items such as alternative providers, whether the function is time-critical, and "the estimated annual budget cost" (Section 11, pp. 36-38).
- legible-org-for-agents: para 56: provide the full register or sections of it "in a processable electronic form (e.g. a commonly used database format, comma separated values)" (para 56, p. 38).
- process-governance-load: para 58: inform competent authorities "in a timely manner" or engage in supervisory dialogue about planned outsourcing of critical or important functions (p. 38); para 60: document the assessments and ongoing monitoring results.

## Key points
- Applies to credit institutions, investment firms, payment and e-money institutions; covers arrangements entered, reviewed or amended on or after 30 September 2019.
- Register may be kept centrally within a group (para 53).
- The impact assessment considered limiting the register to critical functions and rejected it in favour of documenting all arrangements so supervisors can see concentration risk (Section 5, options B1/B2).
