## Relevance to the project
The study that established that explanations increase acceptance of AI recommendations whether or not they are right. Human-AI teams did beat either alone when AI accuracy was comparable to human accuracy, but explanations added nothing to that gain and raised agreement with wrong answers. For a PM harness it means an agent that justifies its PRD review findings persuasively is not thereby making the human reviewer better; the explanation has to enable checking (see Fok and Weld; Vasconcelos et al.).

## Themes
- overreliance-and-deskilling: explanations "increased how often people accepted the AI's recommendations, including when the AI was wrong" (abstract).
- human-review-gates: complementary gains existed but explanations did not increase them; the authors call for explanation designs that foster appropriate trust (abstract).

## Key points
- Mixed-method user studies on three datasets with AI accuracy comparable to humans'.
- Limits: classification tasks with pre-LLM models; participant counts not in the abstract.
