## Relevance to the project

The only first-party statement found that names a cost of River's public-only rule: a chilling effect on questions people would only ask privately. It is written by the agent itself (in Tobi's #tobi-river Slack thread) and posted by the CEO, so it is first-party framing, not independent evidence, but it is the most candid trust/privacy data point in the Shopify corpus.

## Themes

## Key points

- Post: 2026-05-10 13:54 UTC, ~44K views. Two phone screenshots of a River reply in thread #tobi-river (River shown as a Slack APP).
- River's points, paraphrased: (1) Lehrwerkstatt fits; struggle teaches. (2) The 36→77 merge-rate gain also teaches humans, because writing a skill forces clarity. (5) Likes "the company moves at the speed of its slowest secret". (6) It is both apprentice and practitioner.
- Caveat: an LLM's self-assessment of a policy that its operator designed; persuasive framing, not data on how often questions are suppressed or what they were.
