## Relevance to the project
Read directly. NN/g found synthetic users overly optimistic (claimed to finish all courses), listing needs without priorities, nearly uncritically approving a drone-delivery concept, and unable to report real experience. Verdict: supplement real research, never present as real-user findings; low-UX-maturity orgs are most at risk. Caveat: text interviews only, 2024-era tools.

## Themes
- synthetic-users-limits: optimism bias, no priorities, uncritical concept approval; supplement but never replace real users.

## Key points
- Tested text interviews with 2024 tools.
- Risk highest in low UX-maturity organisations.
