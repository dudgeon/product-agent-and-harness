## Relevance to the project
Mostly CEO coaching (hiring, scaling). The product-relevant content is about speed and unevenness: planning cycles have shrunk from a year to about three months, optionality has a cost, and inside a typical software company AI works well in development, support and legal but little elsewhere. Opinion from a coach of fast-growth founders.

## Themes
- bets-and-planning-cadence: "The planning cycles used to be a year, I think the planning cycles now are three months long"; "a massive tax in optionality" (35:21-36:01).
- ai-flattened-org: AI is working in software development, customer support, legal; go-to-market little changed; enterprise sales roles and process "exactly the same" (27:53, 32:15).

## Key points
- Prediction: a persistent, context-aware assistant for every knowledge worker in "3, 4, 5 years", joining meetings as a participant (30:38).
- Risk: AI lets teams do far more, which can erode focus on the beachhead market (34:39-35:21).
