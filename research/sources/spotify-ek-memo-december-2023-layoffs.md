## Relevance to the project
Ek's memo announcing the third and largest 2023 cut (about 17%, roughly 1,500 people per CNBC/NBC). It names the efficiency problem ("more productive but less efficient") and the shift to being "relentlessly resourceful" with fewer, more focused "bold bets". It is the backdrop for Spotify's later framing of AI as a way to ship more with flat headcount (see the Q3 2026 earnings call).

## Themes
- leadership-permission-mandate: "being lean is not just an option but a necessity"; too many people in "supporting work" rather than work with "real impact" (memo body).
- bets-and-planning-cadence (proposed): future profits to be invested more selectively in "bold bets" with "a more focused approach".

## Key points
- Cut ~17% of staff (Ek); ~1,500 jobs (CNBC source cited by NBC). Earlier 2023 cuts: ~6% (~600) in January and ~2% (~200) in June (NBC).
- Rationale: cheap capital in 2020-2021 funded growth in teams, content, marketing and new verticals; output in 2022-2023 "was linked to having more resources", so "more productive but less efficient".
- Calls it "a strategic reorientation", not a retreat; "Lean doesn't mean small ambitions".
- No product or R&D specifics; promised more detail at an internal "Unplugged" session.
