## Relevance to the project
Spoken walk-through of the 2026 tech worker sentiment survey (about 6,000 respondents), companion to the written report already filed (lenny-newsletter-tech-workers-2026-survey). The human side of the change: burnout up, expectations absorb the speed gains, judgment erodes, and the junior ladder is in doubt. A counterweight to claims that freed time goes to thinking.

## Themes
- ambition-expands-scope: top fear is being asked to do more for the same pay (00:10, 53:51-55:25).
- overreliance-and-deskilling: 97.2% say AI makes them better, yet "more, faster, not better" and "my brain is rotting" (45:22-52:26).
- pm-persona-role-expansion: PM is a generalist role; "era of the generalist"; swim lanes dissolving (23:32, 39:58).
- product-team-shape-future: no role recommends itself, juniors not recommended; "rungs of the ladder" removed (14:06, 31:45-38:12); keep entry-level progressing (1:29:24).

## Key points
- Archetypes: 41% energized, 35% conflicted, 12% resentful, remainder disoriented; 50% amplified (08:36-17:07).
- Burnout 44.7% to 54.7%; optimism 54.8% to 48.7% (19:40-20:51).
- AI identity effect about three times as large as manager quality (12:15-12:51); managers still matter most as the buffer (1:15:20).
