## Relevance to the project
Vendor blog hosting practitioner quotes from Aakash Gupta. Supplies a list of likely-automatable PM tasks and the claim that prototyping is the biggest change to the PM job in years, relevant to the PM-as-builder trend.

## Themes
- **non-engineer-builders**: Gupta calls AI prototyping the biggest change to the PM job in years and says PMs should deliver prototypes alongside PRDs and strategy docs.
- **human-judgment-pm-jobs**: Likely automatable per Gupta's examples: email triage and drafting, Slack summarization, competitor launch tracking, feedback clustering, first-draft replies, research-note summaries, prototyping. Human-held: governance (spend and access guardrails, under 'What leaders must provide: access and guardrails') and human review of drafts 'where judgment still matters'; he also urges writing evals to keep quality from drifting (not framed as human-only). 'Deciding what to build' is NOT in this article. The article does not sort tasks explicitly; this is the map author's reading.
- **status-update-agents**: Gupta claims an agent can draft 70 to 80% of emails (his claim, unverified).
- **pm-persona-role-expansion**: Prototyping as a PM job in the founder-PM persona (P3).

## Key points
