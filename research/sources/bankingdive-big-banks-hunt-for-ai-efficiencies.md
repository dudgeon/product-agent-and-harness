## Relevance to the project
Round-up of what five large banks' chief executives said on Q4 2025 earnings calls about AI efficiency. Its value is a negative result: none gave developer productivity numbers or role or headcount changes, so public bank evidence on operating-model change is thin and mostly process-level.

## Themes
- product-team-shape-future: banks frame AI as remaking operating processes (Goldman picked six; Citi is assessing 50+ large processes), not as changing product roles or ratios.

## Key points
- BNY: AI will let it remake processes and systems; Gemini Enterprise inside its Eliza platform and a deeper OpenAI partnership.
- Bank of America: several hundred million dollars invested, 20 projects, more value expected the next year.
- Citi: assessing AI across more than 50 of its largest and most complex processes; transformation largely done, now simplifying and cutting expense.
- Morgan Stanley: confidence rising in efficiency and effectiveness.
- Goldman Sachs: six operating processes chosen for AI redesign since autumn 2025; updates promised through 2026; freed capacity could fund growth.
- Article reports no developer productivity numbers and no headcount or role changes for any bank.
