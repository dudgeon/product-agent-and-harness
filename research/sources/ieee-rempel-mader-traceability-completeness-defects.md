## Relevance to the project
One of the few quantitative studies linking how completely requirements are traced to defect rate. It supports T12's premise that traceability pays for itself, but it comes from open-source projects, not regulated estates, and gives no cost side.

## Themes
- intent-to-market-traceability: more complete traceability lowers expected defect rate for three of four studied requirements activities (abstract).

## Key points
- Studied 24 medium to large open-source projects; defined completeness measures for four requirements-to-implementation activities; analysed with multi-level Poisson regression (abstract).
- Found traceability completeness significantly affects defect rate for three of the four activities; authors say this is the first empirical evidence of the effect.
- Conclusion: traceability is valuable for any software project, even when no standard or regulation requires it.

## Could not verify
Only the abstract was retrieved (via the OpenAlex record). No effect sizes seen. The study measures benefit, not the cost of keeping links.
