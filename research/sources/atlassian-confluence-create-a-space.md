## Relevance to the project

Gives the current Confluence space "purposes" (the earlier pass could not find space types): Collaboration, Knowledge base, or Custom, chosen with a permissions scheme at creation. Confirms the space is the unit at which a PRD's audience is first set, and that a space has one owner (user or group) plus many admins.

## Themes

- project-taxonomy: "A space is a container where you and your team create, store, and collaborate on work related to a shared project or initiative."
- work-structure-spectrum: "Select the purpose that best fits your space (Collaboration, Knowledge base, or Custom)"; Collaboration spaces ship "templates to help you craft the perfect project plan, document decisions, and keep effective meeting notes"; Knowledge bases add "the ability to edit pages privately before publishing them".

## Key points

- Creating a space needs the "Create Space global permission"; you choose name, icon, purpose and "permissions scheme" (some schemes plan-dependent).
- "there can only be one space owner at a time" (user or group); owners keep all permissions even after being removed as owner.
- Space key: unique, alphanumeric, up to 255 chars, part of the URL; apps depend on it.
