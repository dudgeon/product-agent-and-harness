## Relevance to the project

Four short replies in which Tobi answers practical objections to the public-channel design: whether searchable archives would be enough, whether a cheap Claude-Code-posts-to-a-channel version would work, and how invocation works in Slack. They sharpen what Shopify thinks the value is: watching how people *begin* work with the agent and pulling the agent into existing human threads, not the transcript as an archive. Found via X search "from:tobi river" (live), 2026-10-24; texts via fxtwitter API.

## Themes

- **org-learning-loop**: "It's not about the transcript of the work. It's about seeing how other people begin the work with the ai and how they approach it" (reply to @peterarmstrong, 2026-05-09 19:40 UTC, https://x.com/tobi/status/2053198353970688378).
- **shared-persistent-sessions**: River is "brought in during a conversation into a thread": people discussing a bug say "@river would be good to get your opinion", River investigates against real code, adds facts and "usually offers to go fix it" (reply to @mahtabsoin, 2026-05-09 15:41 UTC, https://x.com/tobi/status/2053138177477456032).

## Key points

- **@mahtabsoin asked** (paraphrase): wouldn't publicly searchable channels grepped by an agent be enough, since beyond a few people nobody will scroll others' channels (noise, like bloated email lists)? **Tobi:** "it's more like osmosis" — the agent joins human threads already in progress. (He does not directly answer the noise point.)
- **@peterarmstrong asked:** would a "discount version" (Claude Code posting interactions to channels or a wiki) work for a small team? **Tobi:** "wouldn't be the same" — the value is seeing how others start and approach the work, and River being pulled into conversations.
- **@brendancandon asked** how Tobi organises topics in #tobi_river and whether it replaces his direct Claude conversations. **Tobi:** "River responds in thread, so you just top level post @River and go from there" (2026-05-09 22:11 UTC, https://x.com/tobi/status/2053236371611918574). The second question (does it replace Claude) was not answered.
- **@keeran** (Firefly joke, "Is she...?"). **Tobi:** "She isn't. Our mono repo is called 'world'. River works on our mono repo. Rivers shape the world." (2026-05-11 00:18 UTC, https://x.com/tobi/status/2053630840458944849). Naming rationale.
