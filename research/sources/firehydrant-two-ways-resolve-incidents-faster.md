## Relevance to the project
The only quantified claim found that linking incidents to catalogued services shortens recovery: in FireHydrant's benchmark of 50,000 incidents, incidents with services attached had 36% lower mean time to resolve. It is a vendor's association on its own customers' data (FireHydrant sells a service catalog and incident tool), with no controls for incident type or team maturity. Use only as a vendor-labelled indication that knowing what is affected and who owns it pays at incident time.

## Themes
- dependency-value-evidence: incidents with services attached showed a "36% decrease in MTTR" across severities versus incidents without (Incident Benchmark Report summary).
- stale-records-mislead-agents: the benefit assumes the service record and its owner are correct; the post does not test accuracy.
- vendor-claims-vs-independent-evidence: one vendor platform; period, customer count and statistical method not stated; the author calls MTTR "a less than perfect metric".

## Key points
- Assigning incident roles was associated with 42% lower MTTR; MTTR rose 18% with each responder beyond six.
- Services created on the platform grew 1,640% over 2022.
