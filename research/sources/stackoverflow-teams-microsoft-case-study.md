## Relevance to the project

A vendor case study with a contrast useful for cold-start modelling: Microsoft's earlier internal Q&A system "only had about 900 users", while its internal Stack Overflow instance reached over 70,000 users and 80,000 questions about two and a half years after the search began in 2016. The stated reasons are low-friction content creation, structure, and developers' existing familiarity with public Stack Overflow, i.e. the new tool borrowed habits users already had. For the dependency agent, meeting feature teams in a form they already use (their editor, chat, the agent they already run) may matter more than the context layer's own interface. All figures are the vendor's and unaudited.

## Themes

- **two-sided-cold-start**: before: about 900 users on the old internal Q&A, answers scattered through email, distribution lists and chat, and about 70% of questions unanswered; after: over 70,000 users asking 80,000 questions (case study body). Vendor claim.
- **org-learning-loop**: support teams had answered the same question repeatedly ("three times a month"); afterwards "It feels like repeat questions do not happen anymore" (case study body). Vendor claim, a single interviewee's impression.

## Key points

- Selection criteria in 2016: easy onboarding, well-structured content, low-friction creation (case study body).
- "When we launched our internal Stack Overflow instance at Microsoft, it took off like wildfire." (case study body)
- The page does not describe seeding, champions, integrations, executive sponsorship or whether use was required. More than 50,000 developers work at Microsoft, so 70,000 users exceeds the developer population, which suggests non-developers also used it (inference, not stated).

## Could not verify

- No publication date; the vendor now calls the product Stack Internal. A separate vendor-hosted Dropbox summary says rollout used training, moderators and "seeding of content" (700 to nearly 1,200 users in under a year), but it is a third-party case-study aggregator page and was not filed.
