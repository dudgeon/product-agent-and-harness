## Relevance to the project

The best causal field evidence that tacit know-how mined from work exhaust can be redistributed by an AI assistant: across 5,179 support agents, novices gained 34% while top performers gained almost nothing. It supports the value side of a context-creation agent (spreading what the best people do) and also flags a contribution problem: the people whose knowledge is mined are the ones who benefit least.

## Themes

- **tacit-knowledge-capture**: "the AI model disseminates the best practices of more able workers and helps newer workers move down the experience curve" (abstract; described as suggestive evidence).
- **org-learning-loop**: issues resolved per hour +14% on average, +34% for novice and low-skilled workers, "minimal impact on experienced and highly skilled workers"; also better customer sentiment and retention (abstract).

## Key points

- Staggered rollout of a conversational assistant at one customer-support firm; difference-in-differences design.
- The assistant's suggestions derive from the firm's historical conversations, so the source of the "best practices" is the exhaust of high performers (paper body; not re-read here).
- Published QJE 2025; working paper April 2023, revised November 2023.
