## Relevance to the project
Vendor content marketing (MindStudio / "Remy" agent) that uses River as a hook. **It restates the public-only rule loosely** and adds generic advice. Its one notable claim, that River works in "No DMs. No private groups", is broader than Tobi's own "declines direct messages". Treat it as unverified. This is a different source from the existing `mindstudio-ai-agents-for-product-managers` note.

## Themes
- `org-learning-loop`: "Default to Public, Opt Into Private". Customer data, HR matters and competitive strategy stay private.
- `vendor-claims-vs-independent-evidence`: it mis-describes River as a "coding and knowledge assistant" that helps with "architecture decisions". There are no figures and no sources cited, and it ends by pitching MindStudio's Slack integration.

## Key points
- Suggested channel taxonomy (#ai-research-help, #ai-code-review, …). Review usage every 30–60 days. "Higher stakes require more visibility, not less."
