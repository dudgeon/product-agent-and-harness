## Relevance to the project
Zapier's CEO's executive assistant built agent workflows for meeting prep (participant research plus CRM data), values-aligned meeting feedback, an **executive document review system** that helps teams check documents against the CEO's expectations before formal reviews, and a NotebookLM "strategy companion" that makes company strategy queryable by all employees. Two of these map onto PM-harness ideas: pre-review of documents against a senior reviewer's known standards (a reviewer persona, close to spec review) and making strategy legible to people and agents. We only have show notes, so the mechanics are unverified and relevance is set low until the transcript is read.
