## Relevance to the project
An open, source-backed catalogue entry built only from Shopify's own posts and Tobi's interview. **It restates Shopify's claims with no new facts.** It is useful as a peer-comparison frame: it sits beside Stripe Minions, Ramp Inspect, Spotify Honk and others, and it has separate infrastructure entries for "Shopify · Aquifer" and "Shopify · Roast". It also explicitly marks fields as "Not reported".

## Themes
- `vendor-claims-vs-independent-evidence`: every metric is tagged "Reported by Shopify", "Confidence: Medium", "denominator not reported". The merge-rate metric is undefined.
- `human-review-gates`: "Work-product review (Level 3 of 5)" is assigned only for River-authored PRs (from Tobi's "reviewed by us"). "Oversight of other River tasks is not specified."
- `identity-delegation`: Model, Sandbox and **Credentials: "Not reported"**. Validation and failure handling: "Not reported".

## Key points
- Sources: Under the River (verified 2026-08-31), Knowledge Project (2026-09-15), Learning on the Shop floor.
- Metrics listed: 59,918 sessions / 5,170 channels / 3,536 coauthored merged PRs (30 days, from `river_sessions`). 5,938 employees / 4,450 channels (30 days to 2026-05-09). 1,870 PRs opened in the week to 2026-05-09. Merge rate 36%→77% over two months.
- Mechanisms: "Work in public" (declines DMs, asks for a public channel) and "Carry knowledge forward" (channel context, memory).
- The repo keeps preserved copies of each source.
