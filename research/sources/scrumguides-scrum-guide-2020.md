## Relevance to the project
The Definition of Done is the agile world's shared, binary quality bar: work that does not meet it is not part of the Increment and cannot even be shown at review. It is the closest human-team precedent for a deterministic quality gate held by a multi-person team over many sprints, and it notably has no Definition of Ready.

## Themes
- process-artifacts-as-coordination: DoD "creates transparency by providing everyone a shared understanding of what work was completed" (Increment section).
- human-review-gates: an item not meeting DoD "cannot be released or even presented at the Sprint Review"; it returns to the Product Backlog (Increment section).

## Key points
- If the organisation has a DoD standard, teams follow it as a minimum; otherwise the Scrum Team must create one; Developers are "required to conform".
- The DoD is the commitment attached to the Increment (Product Goal for the backlog, Sprint Goal for the sprint backlog).
- "Definition of Ready" does not appear; the guide only says items finishable within a sprint are "deemed ready for selection".
- Evidence: a definitional document; no outcome data.
