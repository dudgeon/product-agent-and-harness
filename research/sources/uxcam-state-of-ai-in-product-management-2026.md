## Relevance to the project
Vendor-run survey of 157 product professionals in 34 countries on AI adoption. Useful as a directional signal that adoption is near-universal while strategy and measurement lag, which bears on where a PM agent and harness should add measurement. Small sample and vendor-run, so treat as directional.

## Themes
- **pm-ai-usage-surveys**: 99% use AI and 70% spend 5+ hours a week with it (search/page figures as reported).
- **outcome-measurement-gap**: 70% of leaders grew AI budgets but only 6% set AI OKRs.

## Key points
- Survey of 157 product professionals across 34 countries; tagged SURVEY-VENDOR.
- 99% use AI; 70% spend 5+ hours a week with it.
- 70% of leaders grew AI budgets but only 6% set AI OKRs.
- Small sample; the file's section 3 says samples are self-selected and not comparable across surveys.
