## Relevance to the project
Former Uber employees, via Business Insider, describe Genie (not Cortana) as the internal Slack chatbot that answers questions from internal documents in many channels and now takes many questions that used to go to managers or colleagues. In September 2026 the outside view of Uber's Slack assistant is still "Genie", which matters for the naming question: Cortana and Genie appear to coexist, or reporters use the older name. Bears on 'ask the org' agents and the Cortana lineage.

## Themes
- status-update-agents: Genie answers employee questions from internal documents across multiple Slack channels.
- human-relay-removed: one employee says Genie handles many questions previously asked of managers or colleagues.
- overreliance-and-deskilling: internal guidance "not to let ChatGPT do your job for you"; customer-service staff pasted Glean answers straight into chats.
- outcome-measurement-gap: management said gains matching the cost of AI tools had not been confirmed; Claude credits cut or ended in H1.

## Key points
- One former employee: tasks took about one fifth of the time after several weeks with AI tools (source of the "80%" headline).
- Tools named: Genie (Slack), Glean (customer service search), Gemini and ChatGPT (client prep), Claude (credits cut).
- Uber ran office hours and briefings on AI; agentic pods were used in finance and HR.
