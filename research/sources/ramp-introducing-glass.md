## Relevance to the project

Ramp's own 1:48 launch video for Glass; the shortest authoritative statement of the product pitch and its three design purposes. Useful as the baseline positioning to compare other company-wide agent harnesses against.

## Themes

- **cost-control-routing**: Origin story: giving everyone all tools blew the AI budget in weeks; employees default to frontier models (0:06-0:26); finance gets 'full visibility and full control' (1:04).
- **single-agent-many-skills**: 'One single agent' pre-built with all the company's connectivity: analytics, design, prose, 'multiple employees in one' (0:28-0:45).
- **org-learning-loop**: Collaborative sessions: share a Glass session with a technical colleague who steps in (0:48-1:01).

## Key points

- Three stated purposes: one agent with built-in connectivity; collaborative (shareable sessions); makes every token go further (finance visibility/control) (0:28-1:10).
- Positioned for the whole company, not just engineering; anonymous testimonials (financial-model rows, improved conversion) with no numbers (1:14-1:29).
- Says it was built over the last year and is now 'ready to open it up to the broader world' (1:30-1:42).
