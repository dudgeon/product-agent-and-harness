## Relevance to the project
Cagan's own source for the "product management theater" warning, which the jobs map had attributed to vendor coverage (airfocus never uses the phrase). It says people who are really project managers, but call themselves product managers, are pretending, and that generative AI will make this worse. That bears on which PM work is exposed to agents (project and admin coordination) and on the IC PM and GPM personas.

## Themes
- **pm-persona-role-expansion**: Describes "a person that is essentially a project manager" who declares they are a product manager as "just pretending" (body).
- **human-judgment-pm-jobs**: "Generative AI will only compound this": project-style PM work is the exposed part (body).

## Key points
- Product management theater: people with the PM title doing project-management work. Cagan says they are pretending to be product managers.
- He expects generative AI to compound the problem.
- Part of a series: "Product Leadership Theater" (2024-02-13) and "Transformation Theater" (2024-02-27).
