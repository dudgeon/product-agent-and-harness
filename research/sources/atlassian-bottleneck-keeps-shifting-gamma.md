## Relevance to the project
Interview with Gamma hosted on a vendor blog; single-company evidence of the PM role merging with engineering and design. Names 'discernment' and context documentation as the PM's key skills, relevant to the founder-PM persona and the human judgment line.

## Themes
- **pm-persona-role-expansion**: Noronha gives Gamma's ~1 PM to 4 engineers (the article's narration sets it against a 1:10 'rule of thumb'); 'Half of Gamma's PMs and Designers are now committing code' (narration); Noronha: 'I'm increasingly viewing my job as context wrangler'.
- **human-judgment-pm-jobs**: The author's narration: 'the new superpower for Product Managers is discernment'; the constraint now comes 'from deciding what to build'; Noronha wants what is in his head written down so his team and 'all of their agents' can use it. The article closes by asking whether the bottleneck is shipping code, deciding what to build or gaining alignment on priorities ('persistent challenge' is a paraphrase).
- **non-engineer-builders**: Half of PMs and designers commit code (single company).
- **outcome-measurement-gap**: Gamma's A/B tests showed evals missed a customer preference, so human validation stays.
- **org-context-grounding**: Documenting context for humans and agents is part of the PM's new bottleneck.

## Key points
- PM:engineer ratio about 1:4 versus historic 1:10; half of PMs and designers commit code; lead is a 'context wrangler'.
- PMs use AI for bug triage and then choose whether to ticket or escalate (J6).
- A/B tests showed evals missed a customer preference (J9).
- Bottleneck moves to deciding what to build, alignment, and documenting context.

## Could not verify
- Single company; no rigorous industry data on how often the PM role merges into engineering or design.
- Re-check 2026-10-24: page read. Bug-triage and A/B-versus-evals claims confirmed (evals scored 100% yet missed users' preference for minor copy improvements).
