## Relevance to the project
Writer (AI HQ) is an enterprise agent workspace for business users that ships its own model and its own "rebuilt orchestration harness", with Playbooks, 200+ Skills, triggers, supervision and spend reporting. It is a P5-shaped product sold as SaaS to large enterprises (AstraZeneca, Vodafone), and it claims the harness rebuild alone gave 44% faster, 41% cheaper tasks: a vendor's own harness-over-model claim.

## Themes
- harness-over-model: "the rebuilt harness completed tasks 44% faster at 41% lower cost on average" (vendor claim).
- agent-control-plane: centralised reporting on adoption, spend and Playbook/Skill performance; agent supervision suite (introduced at AWS re:Invent) with Bedrock integration.
- non-engineer-builders: Playbooks, Routines, Connectors (Nov 2025); event-based triggers from Gmail, Slack, Gong (Apr 2026).
- skill-standards: Agent Skills and Playbooks added March 2026; library of 200+ enterprise Skills.
- cost-control-routing: admins can enable Anthropic, OpenAI or custom models via Azure, Bedrock or NVIDIA NIM.

## Key points
- Palmyra X6 (released 2026-08-13), post-trained on GLM 5.2; $2/$8 per million input/output tokens; agents unattended up to eight hours.
- Brand governance (voice, terminology, style) added 2026-05-28.
- Customers: AstraZeneca, Currys, Vodafone; Perficient as partner. No seat pricing.
