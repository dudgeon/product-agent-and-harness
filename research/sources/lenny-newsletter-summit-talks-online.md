## Relevance to the project
An index of the eleven main-stage talks from the September 2026 Lenny & Friends Summit (about 1,000 product managers, produced by Stripe's events team) plus Lenny's five takeaways. It is mostly pointers: several talks are filed separately (Geoff Charles on Ramp's AI software factory, Tamar Yehoshua on expanding roles; see lenny-podcast-geoff-charles-product-when-coding-solved and lenny-podcast-tamar-yehoshua-roles-expanding), and others look worth filing (Karri Saarinen's "Context is now the product", Claire Vo's "The last roadmap", Katie Dill's "Raise the ceiling: how to scale intent, quality, and artistry with AI", the Anthropic and OpenAI product panels). The takeaways themselves are useful for the film's framing: "nobody has it figured out" and "it's never been easier to build something nobody wants", which line up with the claim that the bottleneck moved to intent and that the ground moves fast.

## Themes
- pm-job-stable-methods-change: "Nobody has it figured out": speakers took both sides on software factories, the end of roadmaps and PMs shipping to production; Yehoshua: "The answer that we have come to is: it depends"; Ami Vora (Anthropic): "We don't know the answer. We don't think anyone knows the answer" (A few reflections, trend 2).
- product-coherence-over-automation / human-judgment-pm-jobs: "It's never been easier to build something nobody wants"; choosing what to build, knowing what good looks like and caring enough to fix it matter more; Saarinen: "The output is not the product"; Robby Stein (Google Search): the "true value of PM is around judging ... taste" (trend 3).
- pm-persona-role-expansion: roles "expanding, not disappearing"; Geoff Charles (Ramp): "PMs will become GMs, and they will own the actual business outcome"; Elena Verna (Lovable) does pricing pages, prototypes, production deploys, research and analysis herself (trend 4).

## Key points
- Talks listed: Katie Dill (Stripe design), Dan Shipper (Every), Claire Vo ("The last roadmap"), Geoff Charles (Ramp, "The limiting factor: how to design an AI software factory for speed"), Tamar Yehoshua (Atlassian), Robby Stein (Google Search), Elena Verna (Lovable), Marty Cagan (SVPG), Karri Saarinen (Linear, "Context is now the product"), Anthropic panel (Ami Vora, Mike Krieger), OpenAI panel (Tara Seshan, Nan Yu).
- Trend 1 is about loneliness: the more time people spend with agents, "the lonelier the work becomes" and the more they want in-person community (Lenny's observation from attendee messages).
- Trend 5, ambition: Nan Yu (OpenAI Codex): "The biggest constraint is honestly people's understanding and ability to absorb what you're giving them"; Vo: next year is "the ambition game".
