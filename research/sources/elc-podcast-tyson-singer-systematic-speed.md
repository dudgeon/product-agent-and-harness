## Relevance to the project
Spotify's platform head argues the industry over-focuses on "Build it" (coding agents) and that Spotify is applying AI to "Think it" (prototyping and strategy grounded in business context, with AiKA) and "Maintain it" (Honk). This lifecycle framing (Think it, Build it, Ship it, Tweak it) is the closest Spotify statement to our "intent before code" thesis. Only show notes were available.

## Themes
- agent-factory-sdlc: lifecycle "Think it, Build it, Ship it, Tweak it"; "localized speed" to "systematic speed" (7:27, 14:57).
- intent-factory-before-discovery: investing in "Think it" via AI prototyping that draws on deep business context (16:49); AiKA and context engineering for humans and bots (18:47).
- risk-sloped-review: declining code quality and the human PR review bottleneck (25:50); probabilistic vs deterministic code review (29:43).
- prototyping-agents-limits: bottlenecks outside R&D, such as legal and licensing (32:12).

## Key points
- Change is mainly about people and identity (35:57); move from I-shaped to T-shaped talent.
