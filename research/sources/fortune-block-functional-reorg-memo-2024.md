## Relevance to the project
Independent, dated record of the internal memo (subject line "fn block") that moved Block from business units (Square, Cash App, TIDAL, TBD, Proto, foundational) to a functional organisation with a newly appointed leader per function. It is the structural step Block's CTO later credits with enabling central AI investment ([sequoia-training-data-dhanji-goose](sequoia-training-data-dhanji-goose.md)), and the baseline that the 2026 AI-justified flattening builds on. The memo itself did not mention AI.

## Themes
- ai-flattened-org (precursor): reorganise "around function" and end business-unit reporting, framed as a return to "how we started as a company"; targets three problems: collaboration, craft and flexibility.

## Key points
- Previous structure: business units each with their own engineering, product, design, sales and marketing; new structure groups people by function under new functional leads.
- Context: about a year of change, including about 10% of staff laid off, ending of performance reviews, a new CTO, and a headcount cap of 12,000; announced one week before Q2 2024 earnings (1 August).
- A person familiar said Dorsey did not mention further layoffs. Block did not comment.
- Dorsey warned the change may feel "big and disruptive or uncomfortable" (headline).
