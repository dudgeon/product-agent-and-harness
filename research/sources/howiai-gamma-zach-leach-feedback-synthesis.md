## Relevance to the project
A small design team at Gamma uses ChatGPT deep research to analyze hundreds of multilingual feedback items about its AI image-editing feature (about 60% of users are international) and turn them into what the product team does well and poorly. It is a simple, early (mid-2025) example of research synthesis as an AI job inside a product team, with the prompt published. Only show notes were available, so it adds little beyond confirming the pattern; the rest of the episode (Midjourney imagery, background removal, job descriptions) is off-topic.

## Themes
- research-synthesis-agents: ChatGPT deep research used to "transform hundreds of multilingual feedback items into actionable design insights" (show notes; 5:25-12:40 chapters), compared with how feedback was analyzed before AI (9:10) and with "basic scripting" (10:10).
- feedback-triage-agents: the published prompt asks the model to "find where we are doing poorly and where we are doing well" and "What do people love? What do people hate? Where can we improve?" for the product team (show notes, "ChatGPT feedback prompt").

## Key points
- Context: Gamma serves users in more than 60 countries; "60% international user base" (show notes).
- Feedback was about Gamma's AI image editing feature, whose build is discussed at 2:42.
- Other workflows (not relevant): Midjourney style references for on-brand art, Replicate for background removal, AI templates for job descriptions; Claude Projects referenced.
