## Relevance to the project
Describes a supervisory review of ICT change management at more than 30 euro-area banks, framed as testing effectiveness in practice. It is the clearest sign that change management, not only model risk, is where supervisors will look at faster, agent-assisted delivery. It is a practitioner account of the ECB exercise, not an ECB publication.

## Themes
- human-review-gates: the review tests whether change frameworks work in practice, not whether they exist (article).

## Key points
- Targeted review of more than 30 banks under the ECB's 2026-2028 priority on robust ICT capabilities; off-site, with a structured questionnaire, supporting evidence, follow-up from joint supervisory teams, a feedback letter and anonymised benchmarking.
- Eight areas tested: regulatory application; accountability and decision-making; governance frameworks; risk assessment; planning and execution; exceptions and emergencies; rollout and rollback; metrics and reporting.
- No ECB findings reported yet; the weaknesses listed (unclear roles, thin documentation) are KPMG's own experience.
