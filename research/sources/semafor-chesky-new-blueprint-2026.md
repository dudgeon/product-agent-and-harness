## Relevance to the project
The most recent interview found (two days before retrieval). It confirms that, as of October 2026, Chesky still runs Airbnb as "editor-in-chief", now spends two to three hours a day on hiring, and is co-hiring manager for Airbnb's top 200 roles. It also shows his talk-to-real-users stance ("normal people we don't pay"). For the project: the decision structure into which any AI tooling lands is still CEO-centred; the change since 2023 is in how far down his hiring reach goes, not in delegation of product calls.

## Themes
- leadership-review-cadence: restates the post-pandemic model: scrapped divisions, removed management layers, Chesky as "editor-in-chief" reviewing every product in detail before release and working with people several levels down; he calls it micromanaging and admits "huge pushback" from people who felt disempowered and feared slowdown.
- evals-autonomy-trust: the golf-instructor line again: "you've got to watch their swing, and eventually you can let go."
- human-judgment-pm-jobs: "We should talk to normal people we don't pay, people who are willing to tell us to F off and tell us why they don't like us."

## Key points
- Hiring: two to three hours a day; first hire for a new company would be a recruiter; co-hiring manager for executives' direct reports, "Airbnb's top 200 roles", so that tier feels "a direct connection to me". "They have to be my people, not just their people."
- Next product direction: verified identities (about 290 million users and 5.5 million hosts, his figures) as "a real-world social graph" for "matchmaking" (roommates, long-term rentals); details promised for 2027. Possible new "enemy": loneliness.
- AI stance: society feels AI "is happening to them, not for them"; wants "design principles and humanity" in AI; argues neither Silicon Valley nor Washington can regulate it alone.
- Housing: $250 million last-dollar financing fund for housing projects (Airbnb claim: unlocks $5 billion).
- Same week: Airbnb restricted chores hosts can require of guests; a host petition passed 7,000 signatures.
