## Relevance to the project
The standard peer-reviewed review of complacency and automation bias. It says both occur in expert and naive users, are not removed by practice, and arise especially when attention is divided across tasks, which is exactly the reviewer of many agents' output. Complements the already filed Parasuraman-Riley and Parasuraman-Sheridan-Wickens notes.

## Themes
- `overreliance-and-deskilling`: complacency appears in experts and novices, is not eliminated by practice, and is worse under multi-task load; automation bias yields both omission and commission errors with imperfect aids; training and instruction do not prevent it.

## Key points
- Read from the PubMed abstract and secondary summaries only, not the full text.
- Draws mostly on aviation and process-control lab studies.
