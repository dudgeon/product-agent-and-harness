## Relevance to the project
Independent report of the Q1 2026 earnings, three months after the cut, with Owen Jennings crediting "smaller teams" and "flatter teams" for faster launches and analysts judging execution intact. It shows how the market read the AI-native reorg: as working, on the evidence of financials rather than product measures.

## Themes
- ai-flattened-org: Jennings, asked how Block is speeding up launches, credited AI tools and the reorg; smaller, flatter teams mean more autonomy and "less red tape".
- vendor-claims-vs-independent-evidence: analysts (Seaport's Jeff Cantwell, KBW, Oppenheimer's Rayna Kumar) say the cut "does not appear to be weighing" on execution and is "gaining early traction"; their evidence is financial results, not product outcomes.

## Key points
- Q1 2026: gross profit up 27% to $2.9B; net loss $309M driven by $852M of restructuring and other charges; about $495M restructuring spend in Q1, largely complete by end of June.
- Managerbot used by about one million businesses; broader U.S. rollout planned.
