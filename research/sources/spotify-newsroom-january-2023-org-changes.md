## Relevance to the project
Ek's staff memo that cut about 6% of staff and moved most engineering and product under Gustav Söderström, with business areas under Alex Norström, both made co-presidents. It is the start of the centralised "two co-presidents" operating model that Spotify later said it formalised with the 2026 co-CEO change. The stated reason, too much time "syncing on slightly different strategies", is a direct admission that the autonomy model cost decision speed.

## Themes
- autonomy-alignment-placement (proposed): consolidation of most product and engineering under one leader; Ek says teams spend "far too much time syncing on slightly different strategies, which slows us down" (rationale section).
- leadership-permission-mandate: CEO frames efficiency, not only speed, as the new mandate; 2022 opex grew twice as fast as revenue (author's claim).

## Key points
- About 6% headcount reduction (Ek, Jan 2023); ~5 months average severance.
- Gustav Söderström (CPO) takes most engineering and product; Alex Norström (CBO) takes business areas incl. content, advertising, licensing after Dawn Ostroff's departure. Both become co-presidents running the company day to day.
- Ek: "I was too ambitious in investing ahead of our revenue growth"; takes "full accountability".
- No detail on how product and engineering would work day to day; Söderström and Norström were to explain "in the coming days".
