## Relevance to the project
Dorsey's own note to staff announcing the cut from over 10,000 people to just under 6,000, read in full in Geoff's browser. It states the causal claim plainly (intelligence tools plus "smaller and flatter teams") and closes with the product direction that matters for PM work: customers will "build their own features directly, composed of our capabilities and served through our interfaces". That is the customer-facing half of the operating model in [From Hierarchy to Intelligence](block-from-hierarchy-to-intelligence.md), where the backlog comes from what the system cannot yet compose.

## Themes
- ai-flattened-org: the reason given is not distress ("our business is strong") but that intelligence tools "paired with smaller and flatter teams" change "what it means to build and run a company" (paragraph 3).
- leadership-permission-mandate: one large cut instead of gradual ones, framed as honesty; Dorsey says he "may have gotten some of them wrong" and built in flexibility (paragraphs 4-5). Coverage later reported some rehiring ([Cointelegraph](cointelegraph-block-rehires-laid-off-staff.md)).
- agent-led-intent-shaping: the future is customers composing features from Block's capabilities through its interfaces (closing paragraph), i.e. some intent shaping moves to the customer and the system.

## Key points
- Severance terms: 20 weeks' salary plus a week per year of tenure, equity vested to end of May, six months of health care, devices, and $5,000.
- Slack and email left open for goodbyes; a live thank-you session the same day.
- "we're going to build this company with intelligence at the core of everything we do" (closing paragraph). A further note was promised for the next day; that appears to be the [Hierarchy to Intelligence](block-from-hierarchy-to-intelligence.md) essay or a related post (not confirmed).
