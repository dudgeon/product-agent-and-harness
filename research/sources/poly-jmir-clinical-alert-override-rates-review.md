## Relevance to the project
Systematic review of 23 studies on clinical decision-support alerts. It shows what happens to a human gate that fires too often: average override rates of 46.2% to 96.2%, and many overrides were appropriate. It is the evidence base for alert fatigue (gap 10) and warns against designing agent review as a high-volume prompt stream.

## Themes
- `human-review-gates`: override rates of 46-96% across studies; drug-duplication, drug-allergy and formulary alerts were often rightly overridden, so alert design (not only user vigilance) is the lever.
