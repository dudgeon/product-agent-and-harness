## Relevance to the project
A consultancy's estimate of what aviation software certification adds to cost, from the one industry with the longest requirements-to-test traceability duty. It is the nearest thing found to a cost figure for T12, and it is a vendor claim, not a measurement.

## Themes
- intent-to-market-traceability: DO-178C raises initial software engineering cost by 25-40 percent; commercial traceability tools 'greatly reduce' compliance cost (page text).

## Key points
- Level D costs about 15 percent above a medium-quality commercial software baseline; Level C needs about 35 percent more effort than Level D; overall DO-178C adds 25-40 percent to initial software engineering (page text).
- The author argues the step from Level B to C is far smaller than theory (50-70 percent) predicts and Level A over B is 'quite minor' in software.
- Based on the firm's own 250+ projects and 200+ person-years of experience; no method or data published on the page.

## Could not verify
Undated page; figures come from the vendor's own experience, not an independent study. Share of effort spent on traceability alone is not given. A search snippet elsewhere quotes a similar 20-40 percent figure for DO-178B with no stated method.
