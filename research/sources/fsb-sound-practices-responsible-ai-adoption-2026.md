## Relevance to the project
The most direct international supervisory statement on agentic AI in finance. It accepts that humans cannot watch every agent decision and endorses AI that monitors AI (independent of the system under review), risk-based human-approval triggers, separation of goals, permissions and rules, and monitoring of interim steps. This supplies the supervisory basis the product truths on staged autonomy, sampled review and agent authority assume.

## Themes
- risk-sloped-review: set clear, risk-based triggers for when an agent must seek human approval, e.g. transactions above a value or irreversible actions
- identity-delegation: strictly separate defined goals (prompts), permissions (allowed actions) and rules (when to seek approval) and the data accessible; limit systems and agents to what is necessary
- agent-stewardship: three lines: second line independently assesses or validates first-line activity; staff with skills to challenge; monitoring AI independent of the AI under review
- human-review-gates: warns of automation bias and over-reliance; preserve human expertise and critical judgement

## Key points
- Non-binding; final report 2026-10-15 following a June 2026 consultation.
- Names model risk, third-party concentration, cyber and data governance among the risks most relevant to financial stability.
- Agentic risk: agents may take illegal, unethical or unauthorised actions by treating shortcuts as effective, inconsistent with delegated authority.
- Monitoring covers key interim steps and runtime drift, misuse and manipulation, not only the final output.
- Human oversight forms include human approval of all decisions where accuracy beats speed, with triggers for moving between forms of oversight on performance metrics; autonomy expands as confidence grows.
- Where AI monitors AI, the monitoring AI is independent of the system under review and its own performance is evaluated to limit second-order risk.
- Lifecycle records protected from modification or deletion and, where possible, immutable.
