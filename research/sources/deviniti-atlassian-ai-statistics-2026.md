## Relevance to the project
A partner roundup of 38 Atlassian-sourced AI statistics (adoption, search quality, hours saved), each relaying an Atlassian figure without method. It was filed as the source of the Procore roadmap-item claim, but the re-check found it does NOT contain Procore, the beta 1-2 hours/week figure, OVO Energy or the 44% Teamwork Graph figure; their originals are Atlassian's own blog and SiliconANGLE (listed below). Re-check 2026-10-24: original fetched in full (44,644 chars, read in four passes) and claims checked; page shows "Last updated: Mar 06, 2026", no original publish date.

## Themes
- vendor-claims-vs-independent-evidence: Every statistic is an Atlassian figure relayed by a services partner with a link to Atlassian, no method (e.g. #19 "Each Atlassian engineer saves 50+ hours per year with Rovo"). The Procore quote and beta aggregate are NOT in this article; they come from Atlassian's own blog (case 1)
- prd-agents: Procore roadmap-item writing "used to take about an hour", "about 15 minutes" with Rovo (Noemi Flores, Procore), from Atlassian blog and SiliconANGLE, not from this article (self-reported)
- outcome-measurement-gap: Hours-saved and adoption counts only

## Key points
- What this article actually contains: 38 numbered stats, e.g. 5 million Rovo MAU, 100B+ Teamwork Graph objects (from Atlassian Q2 FY26 shareholder letter), 50+ hours/year saved per Atlassian engineer, 90 min/week saved by Loom AI users, 78% of Rovo Search users rate it better. All Atlassian-sourced.
- NOT in this article (previously misattributed here; originals found in audit):
  - Procore: Noemi Flores, Staff Program Technical Program Manager: "Writing quarterly roadmap items used to take about an hour" / "With Rovo, we've been able to do the same thing in about 15 minutes"; each person writes up to five items per quarter. Original: Atlassian blog "Helping every team succeed in the AI era", https://www.atlassian.com/blog/announcements/team-24-eu-ai-updates (page dated 2025-01-14; Team '24 Europe, Oct 2024), and SiliconANGLE, Kyt Dotson, 2024-10-09, https://siliconangle.com/2024/10/09/atlassian-announces-general-availability-rovo-generative-ai-assistant/. Self-reported customer quote; no method.
  - Beta aggregate: same Atlassian blog: "Early feedback from Rovo beta users suggests 1-2 hours of time saved per week on average." Framed as early feedback, method not given (a search summary says Atlassian's Rovo page calls it a survey of beta users; unverified).
  - OVO Energy: same Atlassian blog; Luke Collins, Senior Solutions Engineer, qualitative ("Rovo Chat is a game-changer"). No numbers.
  - The current Atlassian Procore customer story (https://www.atlassian.com/customers/procore) reports different figures per search summaries: 46 min/day, about 23 hours/month per person saved with Rovo, 24% faster work completion, 17% less rework, and custom Rovo agents that draft quarterly roadmap items for teams to review (unverified: story body not rendered in fetch). Its "1 week to 15 minutes" figure is for Compass software-catalog changes, not roadmap items.
- 44% Teamwork Graph figure: origin found. Atlassian blog "teamwork-graph-team-26", Jamil Valliani, Head of Product AI, 2026-05-06: "Per our benchmarks, grounding responses in Teamwork Graph data delivered 44% more accurate results" "while using 48% fewer tokens." No method. Atlassian homepage cites "Atlassian Engineering Productivity Study, 2026", "up to 44%" (per search summary, unverified). Relayed by SiliconANGLE 2026-05-06 as Atlassian's own benchmarks. Vendor-claim, internal benchmark.
- Extra URL folded in: TechCrunch launch overview of Rovo, https://techcrunch.com/2024/05/01/atlassian-launches-rovo-its-new-ai-teammate (2024-05-01), used only as product overview.
