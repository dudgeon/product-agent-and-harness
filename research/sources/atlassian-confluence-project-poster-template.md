## Relevance to the project

The Atlassian Team Playbook's "project poster": a living Confluence page for a project's problem space, validated assumptions, and proposed solution, with a "reference materials" section where links to other artifacts go. It is the closest Atlassian comes to a project-level intent document, and it is deliberately upstream of a PRD (problem framing, known facts vs open questions, "confirm or disprove the solution").

## Themes

- artifact-standardization: sections: basics (owner, team, status, "how this project fits into your larger strategy"), "Define your problem space", "Validate your assumptions" (what you know vs questions still to answer), "Get to work" (solution, why a customer wants it, scale and scope), "Include reference materials" via /link.
- project-taxonomy: "a project poster is ... a living document. Continue to update it as your project evolves"; it is a page, not an Atlassian Home project object, and the page does not mention linking to Jira or Home projects.

## Key points

- "Created by Atlassian Team Playbook coaches, the Project Poster Play is a team exercise that helps you validate assumptions and understand the problem you're solving for."
- Reference materials are added with `/link`; images with `/image`; no typed linkage.
