## Relevance to the project
A second, independent checklist-generation evaluator that reports higher correlation with human judgment than G-Eval and GPTScore, supporting the TICK finding that decomposing a standard into checkable items beats asking for a single score. Lower relevance because the abstract gives no figures and the domains (Portuguese legal similarity, SummEval) are far from PM work.

## Themes
- standard-form-spectrum: two-stage LLM method, checklist generation then checklist evaluation, usable with or without a reference; claims "a structured and interpretable assessment" and higher human correlation than G-Eval and GPTScore (abstract).

## Key points
- Validated on Portuguese Legal Semantic Textual Similarity and SummEval.
- No correlation figures on the abstract page.
