## Relevance to the project
Self-reported breakdown of what Anthropic's own engineers delegate, keep, and newly do with a general coding agent. It shows a general harness used for a mix of exploratory and routine work and that full delegation stays small. Survey of the vendor's own staff, self-reported.

## Themes
- work-structure-spectrum: 132 engineers surveyed, 53 interviews, 200k Claude Code transcripts (Feb and Aug 2025); design/planning rose from 1.0% to 9.9% of Claude Code tasks; 27% of Claude-assisted work would not have been done otherwise (exploratory, nice-to-have tools, docs, tests); 8.6% "papercut" fixes.
- human-judgment-pm-jobs: engineers keep high-level design that needs organisational context or "taste", and tasks they enjoy.
- human-review-gates: more than half can "fully delegate" only 0-20% of their work; they validate output on complex or high-stakes work. Concerns: skill atrophy, supervision needing skills that atrophy.

## Key points
- Data collected August 2025; caveats of social desirability and recency bias stated by the authors.
