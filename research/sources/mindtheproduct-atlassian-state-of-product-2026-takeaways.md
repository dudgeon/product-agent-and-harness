## Relevance to the project
Practitioner-site summary (page title 'Four things we learned from Atlassian's State of Product 2026') of a vendor survey: Atlassian State of Product 2026, fieldwork by Wakefield Research, 700 manager-level respondents in the US, Germany and France at 500-10,000-employee companies, plus a separate Atlassian AI survey of 521 PMs. Its 80% finding on engineers excluded from early work is the main evidence in the map that trio collaboration is thin, relevant to J1 and J3 and to what a PM agent should connect.

## Themes
- **pm-ai-usage-surveys**: 80% of teams do not involve engineers in ideation, problem definition or roadmap creation.
- **svpg-four-risks**: The 80% figure is used in the map as evidence that feasibility input arrives late (product trio collaboration is thin).

## Key points
- Summarises Atlassian State of Product 2026. Marketing says '1,000+' (and a Team Europe listing '1,200+'); the report's methodology (p.39) gives 700 respondents (US, DE, FR; manager seniority) plus a separate global AI survey of 521 PMs (700 + 521 = 1,221).
- 80% of teams do not involve engineers in ideation, problem definition or roadmap creation. Used in the J1 pain list.
- Report: 'Roughly half (49%) of surveyed product teams don't have enough time for strategic planning and roadmap development'; the same share can't fit in data analysis and metrics tracking (a separate item). Don't conflate with the separate '49% find internal politics hold back collaboration'.
- Report: 'Overall, 50% of respondents are saving 10-60 minutes of time per day using AI' (from the 521-PM AI survey); 'up to 2 hours a day' is the top bucket.
- 80% confirmed in the report, p.11. Report PDF read via syndicated copy: https://files.ziftone.com/p/fa6726b45a224f6091c59b8f5b222d1f/State+of+Product+2026_FINAL_G.pdf ; Atlassian report page: https://www.atlassian.com/software/jira/product-discovery/resources/state-of-product-2026
