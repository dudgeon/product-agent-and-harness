## Relevance to the project
A single PM's workflow from late 2025, using chat-app projects (Claude and ChatGPT) as one "second brain" per workstream to survive context switching, plus a Claude-guided Reddit scraper and AI analysis of 34,000 rows of public conversation to get an "unbiased" view of what users want from agents. It is a baseline for what PMs do unaided with consumer tools before any harness: hand-curated per-project context, a seed-and-grow loop, and manual spot-checking of synthesis. Mildly relevant to artifact-and-project-structure (the project as the PM's context container) and to discovery synthesis; dated and thin on outcomes, so low.

## Themes
- product-artifact-primitives: each workstream is a chat-app project with instructions and a growing file set ("20 files. It started with two or three or four"); seeded with the kickoff deck, PRD references and company web pages printed to PDF; once a scope doc or narrative is produced he puts it back into the project's knowledge so later answers build on it (0:14, 5:50, 14:23, 17:04).
- feedback-triage-agents: Claude walked him step by step through setting up the Reddit API and a Python script; the scrape returned 34,000 rows about AI agents, Monday.com and competitors; Claude then produced a frequency- and weight-ranked table of top themes to take back to the team (8:35-13:32).
- ai-synthesis-hallucination-risk: he spot-checks by keyword search and always asks for "at least one or two quotes as to where this came up from", then verifies each quote is in the source (13:40).
- status-update-agents: the project "brain" answers ad hoc requests from marketing or comms mid-day (e.g. a two-line description of a release) while he is context switching (17:04); "the ability to be at so many places simultaneously" is what other PMs miss (36:11).
- pm-ai-usage-surveys: Monday.com ranks PMs by AI tool usage; he was 4th of 90 PMs over a six-month window despite two months' leave (34:16). A company-internal leaderboard, not a survey.

## Key points
- Motivation: leading Monday.com's AI agents initiative, he faced "a thousand different voices" internally and wanted outside, unbiased signal on what customers expect from agents (6:34).
- Project instructions tell the model to act as a product expert and to push back rather than be "super supportive" (15:44).
- A custom GPT rewrites his Slack messages to be concise, grounded in Lenny's Newsletter writing advice and related books; shared with colleagues (18:28-21:42).
- Mock product interviews in GPT voice mode (24:08 onward); not relevant here.
