## Relevance to the project
A frontier-lab head of product says PMs matter more now that building is easy: taste, signal from noise and turning speed's chaos into order. Her PM artifact is a behaviour spec translated into eval rubrics, data and reward modelling, which is a concrete form of "spec becomes evals". Mostly AI-product practice; the people and learning points carry over to deterministic products.

## Themes
- human-judgment-pm-jobs: "PM ascendance": taste, insight, consensus-building when anyone can build (20:08-21:25).
- spec-becomes-prototype-and-evals: spec to rubrics to data to reward model (17:46-18:30); "blank is the new PRD" means PMs bring heft (50:04-51:00).
- evals-autonomy-trust: start evals with 5-20 prompts, you as rater; judge and serving stack change results (23:20-29:21).
- bets-and-planning-cadence: 60/20/20 build rule, retest scaffolding with each model (33:30-36:43); AI-product only.
- skill-improvement-loops: shared skills repository, workshops, "if you do something twice, outsource it" (37:57-41:19).

## Key points
- Model behaviour is unpredictable ("Snape's potions class"); the PM shapes by studying output as well as input (03:18).
- PM hiring: model-fluent, can build an eval and dig into data, adaptive (21:44-22:37).
- AI documents for post-launch data pulls; humans provide insight (48:13-49:02).
- Chain-of-thought monitors as a deployment control (10:52-11:39).
- Burnout: from churn and grunt work; her team works "150%" (44:09-47:23).
