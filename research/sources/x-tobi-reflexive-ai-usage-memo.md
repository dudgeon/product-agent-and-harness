## Relevance to the project

Tobi's April 2025 internal memo, a year before River, sets the process frame River later lands in. It names GSD as the project process and says the GSD Prototype phase "should be dominated by AI exploration". It makes AI use part of performance and peer reviews, and requires teams to show why AI can't do the work before asking for headcount. For the lifecycle map, it is the only first-party statement found on where AI sits in Shopify's intent-shaping step and which tools staff had pre-River.

## Themes

- **enablement-by-product**: adoption by mandate, not product alone: AI questions added to performance and peer review; "Everyone means everyone", including executives ("What This Means").
- **org-learning-loop**: "Learning is self directed, but share what you learned"; Slack and Vault channels where people share prompts (#revenue-ai-use-cases, #ai-centaurs) ("What This Means").
- **prototyping-agents-limits**: "AI must be part of your GSD Prototype phase"; "Prototypes are meant for learning and creating information" ("What This Means").

## Key points

- Posted 2025-04-07 because the memo "was in the process of being leaked"; ~2.5M views.
- **GSD:** the project process has a Prototype phase, and that phase should be dominated by AI exploration, to produce "something that other team mates can look at, use, and reason about".
- **Tools available then:** chat.shopify.io (internal chat, "for years"); for developers the proxy, Copilot, Cursor and Claude Code, "pre-tooled". Shared prompts in Slack and **Vault** (Shopify's internal knowledge and people system).
- **Headcount rule:** "Before asking for more Headcount and resources, teams must demonstrate why they cannot get what they want done using AI."
- AI integration time in monthly business reviews and product development cycles.
- This year's themes set the roadmap ("I already laid out a lot of the AI projects in the themes this year"), which matches the yearly themes Glen Coates describes ([Lenny](lenny-how-shopify-builds-product.md)).
