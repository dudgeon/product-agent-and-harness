## Relevance to the project
Anthropic's classification of 1.2 million sampled Cowork sessions (May 2026) shows knowledge-work delegation concentrated in "connective" work: business process and operations (33.4%) and content creation (16.4%) make up about half, ahead of research (6.4%) and data analysis (5.8%). That is usage-derived evidence for which PM-adjacent jobs people already hand to a general agent (status reports, decks, trackers, synthesis) and bears on the activity-classification, sequencing and target-outcome dimensions. It is telemetry from the vendor's own product with an automated classifier and stated limits; it measures what people try, not whether it worked, and has no PM-specific cut.

## Themes
- pm-ai-usage-surveys: category shares from 1.2M sampled sessions, 600,000+ organisations, May 11-31 2026, 20-category taxonomy ("What the data shows", "How we measured").
- work-structure-spectrum: the top categories are connective, cross-role work (assembling scattered updates, onboarding checklists, reconciling spreadsheets, decks) rather than a role's core judgement work ("How knowledge workers are using AI").
- status-update-agents: "drafting a status update" and "pulling scattered updates into a single report" named as typical; status reports, decks and trackers called out as the rising use ("The rise of AI in knowledge work").
- non-engineer-builders: Cowork was built because non-technical users were already using Claude Code for folder and spreadsheet tasks but found the terminal a "black box" (intro).

## Key points
- Shares of sampled sessions: business process and operations 33.4%; content creation and copywriting 16.4%; software development 8.7%; DevOps and infrastructure 7%; research and intelligence 6.4%; data analysis and BI 5.8%; document processing 4.1%; sales and revenue ops 4%; personal assistance 3.8%; education 2.4%; meeting intelligence 1.8%; legal and compliance 1.3%; customer support 0.8%.
- Interpretation offered: people use Cowork to assemble and structure information so they can apply their own expertise (lawyer formatting filings, hiring manager synthesising interview feedback, team lead making the deck that explains a decision).
- Contrast with Claude Code, used by developers for core building work; developers use Cowork for the surrounding communication work.
- Method (stated): privacy-preserving tool, no human read any session, aggregate category stats only; rate-capped sampling (fixed sessions per hour), so numbers are shares, not volumes, and busy hours are under-represented.
- Stated limitations: taxonomy classifies work, not job title, so no marketing, finance, HR or product-management categories; window chosen after a labelling-pipeline change around May 11; about 5% personal or companionship use; automated labels may err.
- Missing for this project: no product-management or design category, no success or completion rates, no data on which tasks were abandoned or redone.
