## Relevance to the project
The CFPB reports it stopped using disparate impact in supervision and enforcement, closed open exams and orders that relied on it, and finalised the April 2026 Regulation B amendment. This changes what US supervisors will test for in model and pricing experiments: the federal disparate-impact lens that shaped test-envelope design has gone, while disparate treatment, proxies, state law and private suits remain.

## Themes

## Key points
- During the reporting period the CFPB stopped using disparate impact in supervision and enforcement, closed exam elements and investigations relying on it, and terminated orders based on it.
- Executive Order 14281 (2025-04-23) directed agencies to stop using disparate impact; the Regulation B final rule followed on 2026-04-22.
- Report also records a past matter involving credit card applications and credit line increases handled by rogue underwriters (disparate treatment style).
