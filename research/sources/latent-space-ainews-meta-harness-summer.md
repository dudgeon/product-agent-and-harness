## Relevance to the project
The most-read independent commentary placing Omnigent in the "meta-harness" category. It supports common usage of the term in the horizontal sense ("the harness of harnesses") and lists the lineage, but it offers no hands-on test and is openly unsure Omnigent will be the winner.
