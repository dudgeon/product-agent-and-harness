## Relevance to the project
Vendor survey of 379 professionals at 500+ employee firms (Productboard sells PM software). Gives per-task time-saver rankings and a split between strategic and non-strategic PMs, but only 40% measure AI ROI by business outcomes, which bears on the measurement gap.

## Themes
- **pm-ai-usage-surveys**: Largest time savings for presentations, PRDs, competitive research and roadmap creation; about 4 hours saved per task on average; 98% have changed or plan to change team structures; top rising skills data literacy 58% and customer-insight synthesis 54%.
- **outcome-measurement-gap**: Only 40% measure AI ROI via business outcomes.
- **human-judgment-pm-jobs**: Strategic and non-strategic PMs are diverging as AI takes over tactical work.

## Key points
- 379 professionals, 500+ employee firms; vendor sells PM software.
- Top time savers: presentations, PRDs, competitive research, roadmap creation; ~4 hours saved per task; 98% changing or planning to change team structures.
- Rising skills: data literacy 58%, customer-insight synthesis 54%.
- Over a third lack a documented AI policy; 65% have one (used in the GPM/CPO persona section).
- A Turnitin PM quoted says AI reduces ticket and story paperwork (J6).
- Presentations are listed first among the top time-savers, but the page does not rank the list (J8); data literacy top rising skill (J9).
- Commissioned by Productboard; fieldwork by UserEvidence, which the page calls an independent research firm. Still vendor-published, hence vendor-claim.
