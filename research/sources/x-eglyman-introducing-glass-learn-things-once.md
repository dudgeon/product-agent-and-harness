## Relevance to the project

The CEO's launch essay and the best single statement of why Ramp built Glass and what it claims it delivers: shared company knowledge, multiplayer sessions, skills, any model via Ramp Router, and a per-project cost meter. All numbers are Ramp's own.

## Themes

- **org-learning-loop**: 'Learn things once': 323 employees made 6,500+ edits to what Glass knows; Finance taught it to exclude US firms with UK offices.
- **cost-control-routing**: $1.5M token week in May; per-project 'taxi meter'; Ramp Router picks the model; 30% bill drop claimed.
- **enablement-by-product**: Feature-request capture 300/month to 20,000/month from a saved prompt; 5,000+ customer emails.

## Key points

- Product list: company knowledge (Linear, Notion, Google, Slack, Gong), multiplayer, skills, any model, meter on every project.
- Early access, onboarding businesses personally, ramp.com/glass.
- Note: the May token-week story post-dates the April Glass articles; likely launch narrative.
