## Relevance to the project
A principal PM (at Spotify since 2011) explains to research scientists how product thinks at Spotify in 2025: Rumelt's strategy kernel (diagnosis, guiding policy, coherent action) for the "why", and Cagan's four risks for discovery. It shows the vocabulary a Spotify PM uses and that tech research is a central team aligning with many product teams, with handoff friction between them.

## Themes
- svpg-four-risks: value, usability, feasibility, viability used in discovery; research mostly de-risks feasibility; he proposes treating research as a product with PM teams as customers.
- opportunity-to-code-chain: strategy kernel used to tie research to "their" strategy; tips: start with the why, connect to existing problems, speak the product team's language and metrics, mind timing, show prototypes or demos.

## Key points
- Personalization Tech Research is a central team of research scientists serving many product teams; author is its product lead.
- Product teams prioritise "why" and "what"; research goes deep on "how".
