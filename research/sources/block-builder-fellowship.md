## Relevance to the project
A paid six-month-plus fellowship (pilot cohort of 10) that places "rising AI talent" in Block's design and engineering teams to "design, prototype, and ship". It has a design track for "designers and builders in product, creative, and design engineering", and selection is by "proof you can build". It shows Block's hiring vocabulary shifting to "builders", but there is no product-manager track.

## Themes
- non-engineer-builders: design-track fellows are expected to prototype and ship; applicants show "code, prototypes, visuals, experiments" (FAQ, selection).
- pm-persona-role-expansion: candidates "talk with our builders across design, engineering, and product"; "builder" is used across roles (FAQ).

## Key points
- Tracks: design (frontier of AI and design; open case by case from age 14) and engineering ("product-facing engineers who see AI as part of the stack").
- Remote-first; NYC kickoff; may convert to full-time roles.
- No tools named.
