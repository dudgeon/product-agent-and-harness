## Relevance to the project
Documents the product development phases used in Spotify's Premium R&D (Understand it, Think it, Build it, Ship it, Tweak it), the kick-off artifact (an "Understand It Brief"), and a Scorecard that names five success factors, each with one named primary evaluator by role. This is a concrete map of who owns which judgment across the lifecycle, and of the phase gates where each judgment is checked. For the project it is a ready-made model of role-owned review gates and of the brief as the first shared artifact.

## Themes
- svpg-four-risks: five factors, each with a primary evaluator: Desirable (user researcher), Viable (product manager), Feasible (engineering manager), Usable (user researcher), Unified with design principles and design system (product designer) (section "Creating a Scorecard").
- human-review-gates: factors are "gates" checked as a project moves phase: Desirable at Think it, Viable and Feasible at Build it, Usable and Unified at Ship it (section "Our success factors explained").
- product-artifact-primitives: three linked artifacts: Thoughtful Execution tree (existing data and insights), Understand It Brief (problem, outcomes, success; a collaborative kick-off doc), Scorecard (distilled success criteria with fail / success / surpass levels). Templates linked as Google Docs and Slides.
- opportunity-to-code-chain: Understand it phase added in front of Gustav Söderström's original four phases; also "acknowledge what you don't know" and plan how to learn it.
- artifact-standardization: released in March 2020 as part of a "product development toolbox" by Premium R&D principal designers to improve ship quality and alignment.

## Key points
- Problem: projects go off course because team members hold different pictures of the goal from the start.
- Scorecard is built together as a team activity; one person is primary evaluator per factor.
- Each factor has fail, success and surpass definitions; illustrated with a reconstructed (not actual) Discover Weekly scorecard.
- Teams may swap in their own success factors.
