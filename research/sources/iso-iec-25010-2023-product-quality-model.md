## Relevance to the project
The standard quality model is the obvious candidate for the "by quality attribute" partition of a requirements taxonomy: nine characteristics, each with sub-characteristics, built to be a reference model "for the quality of the products to be specified, measured and evaluated". It excludes purely functional properties, which is the first sign that a quality-attribute partition cannot be exhaustive on its own: the feature's own behaviour, and the obligations that come from outside the product (law, policy, brand, records), sit outside it. The ISO page itself (paywalled standard; the catalogue page was read) gives the scope; the characteristic list below is from the arc42 quality site summary of the 2023 edition and should be checked against the standard before being relied on verbatim.

## Themes
- requirements-partitions: nine characteristics in the 2023 edition: functional suitability, performance efficiency, compatibility, interaction capability (formerly usability), reliability, security, maintainability, flexibility (formerly portability), safety (arc42 summary of the standard; the ISO catalogue page confirms "nine characteristics" and that "purely functional properties" are excluded but functional suitability is included).
- standard-form-spectrum: the model is a reference list of named qualities, not a checklist with fit criteria; measurement is left to the companion standards in the 25000 series.

## Key points
- Scope (ISO catalogue page): a product quality model for ICT products and software products, composed of nine characteristics subdivided into sub-characteristics; the characteristics "provide a reference model for the quality of the products to be specified, measured and evaluated"; the model excludes purely functional properties but includes functional suitability.
- Sub-characteristics (arc42 summary): security = confidentiality, integrity, non-repudiation, accountability, authenticity, resistance; interaction capability adds inclusivity and user engagement; safety = operational constraints, risk identification, fail safe, hazard warning, safe integration; flexibility = testability, adaptability, scalability, installability, replaceability.
- The 2011 edition's separate "quality in use" model (five characteristics about the outcome of use) is not part of the product model.

## Could not verify
- The standard's own text is paywalled; characteristic names and sub-characteristics are taken from the arc42 quality site (https://quality.arc42.org/standards/iso-25010) and the iso25000.com summary, which agree with each other for the first five characteristics. Treat exact wording as unverified.
