## Relevance to the project
Vendor webinar and event marketing for the Atlassian State of Product 2026 report. Source of the claim that AI time savings are being spent on reactive work while strategy slips, which supports the argument for agents that protect strategy time. Second URL: https://events.atlassian.com/teameurope/session/3225387/state-of-product-2026-ai’s-up-burnout’s-worse-strategy’s-mia

## Themes
- **pm-ai-usage-surveys**: Report headline: teams 'win back up to 2 hours a day with AI' but still lack time for strategy; half save 10-60 minutes a day; reactive, short-term work; many feel burnt out; 84% concerned their products won't succeed in market. (Event page wording 'close to two hours a day' is unverified and overstates the typical saving.)
- **outcome-measurement-gap**: Time saved is not translating into strategy time or demonstrable outcomes (event page).

## Key points
- Two pages: the webinar page and the Team Europe event session page.
- Event-listing wording (search snippet only, page now dead): 'nearly 2 hours a day'. The report itself: 'up to 2 hours a day' as the top bucket; 50% save 10-60 minutes a day; 'moderate productivity gains of up to an hour a day'.
- Report: 'This reactive, short-term approach to work...'; 'Many feel burnt out, spread thin'; burnout is measured in one wave, so 'rising' is not a measured trend.
- 84% confirmed: '84% of product teams are concerned the products they're currently working on won't be successful in the market.'
- Cited in J2 as evidence that strategy time is squeezed.
