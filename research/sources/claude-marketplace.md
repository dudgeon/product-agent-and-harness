## Relevance to the project
Anthropic's Claude Marketplace puts 2,000+ connectors and plugins, third-party Claude-powered agents and products, and consulting partners in one catalogue, and lets customers spend part of their committed Anthropic spend on listed products. For the project this is the vendor's answer to capability distribution: plugins and connectors built on MCP and Agent Skills (open standards) as the unit of distribution, plus a procurement shortcut that tilts buy-vs-build toward Claude-ecosystem products. The launch post says nothing about vetting, security review or admin controls over what can be installed, which is the open question for a regulated company.

## Themes
- skills-marketplace: one destination for connectors and plugins (2,000+ at launch, e.g. Atlassian, Google, Microsoft, Notion, Salesforce), agents/products, and service partners ("For customers").
- skill-supply-chain-security: no listing review, signing, permissions or admin allow-listing is described; the absence is the finding.
- build-vs-buy: customers can apply "a portion of" committed Anthropic spend to buy Claude-powered software (CrowdStrike, Cursor, Harvey, Legora, Lovable, Snowflake, Hebbia), shortening procurement ("For customers"; partner quotes).
- pm-tool-connectivity: Atlassian's Teamwork Graph connector brings Confluence and Jira context; Notion and Salesforce/Slack connectors named (partner quotes).
- skill-standards: builders publish connectors or plugins using MCP and Agent Skills, described as "open standards pioneered by Anthropic" ("For builders and partners").

## Key points
- **Three listing paths:** (1) connector or plugin via MCP and Agent Skills; (2) apply to list a Claude-powered agent or product, purchasable with committed spend; (3) join the Claude Partner Network as a consultant/SI (Accenture, BCG, Deloitte named).
- **Commercial mechanism:** committed-spend retirement against third-party products; customer spotlight says CodeRabbit applied part of its commitment to Vercel, Power Digital and ThoughtSpot to Snowflake.
- **Partner quotes** (Notion, Salesforce, Snowflake, Atlassian, CrowdStrike, Hebbia, Legora) are endorsements focused on context in Claude and procurement speed.
- **Missing:** how listings are reviewed, whether enterprise admins can restrict the marketplace to approved items, how plugin updates are controlled, what data listed products receive, and pricing share for partners.
- **Read with:** the Jamf note (plugins as data-routing guardrails) and the existing plugin-security sources; the marketplace widens supply while the governance story sits elsewhere.
