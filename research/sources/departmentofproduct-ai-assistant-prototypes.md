## Relevance to the project
An early-2025 vibe-coding tutorial about prototyping customer-facing AI assistants, not PM agents: a general chatbot generates ideas and a PRD from business inputs, the PRD becomes a Lovable or Replit prompt, and the tool builds a clone of a known product with the assistant inside. Its lasting value for the project is a five-part typology of where an assistant sits in a product (chat pop-up, contextual actions on a selection, in-product side panel, AI search, full-screen live voice), which can inform the agent-interface dimension for an embedded PM agent. It also shows the idea-to-PRD-to-prototype chain PMs were being taught before coding agents, with no evaluation step.

## Themes
- prototyping-agents-limits: the author's own caveats: inputs were illustrative ("would need to be trained on richer, cleaner data"), and a voice prototype is only "test-worthy" once connected to real conversational capability.
- spec-becomes-prototype-and-evals: each example runs ideas, then PRD, then a prototype prompt, then a clickable clone (Stripe, Shopify, Linear, Uber, Google Maps), with no eval or user test shown.
- prd-agents: the chatbot (Grok) drafts the PRD from codebase, analytics, error logs and support logs, or from a filled value-proposition canvas.

## Key points
- **Chat pop-up (Stripe clone):** ideas derived from mock support and error data; the generated design included an unprompted "tone toggle" between developer and user modes.
- **Contextual actions (Shopify clone):** actions offered on a selected object (Miro's summarise-these-stickies cited); prototype adds competitor price benchmarking when editing a price.
- **Side panel (Linear clone):** assistant answers questions like what is blocking the roadmap and creates tickets from conversation; the author sees side panels as the fit for assistants that take actions.
- **AI search (Uber clone):** natural-language search over dashcam footage, borrowing Ring's video search.
- **Live voice (Google Maps clone):** full-screen takeover pattern borrowed from Perplexity, ChatGPT, Copilot and Gemini.
