## Relevance to the project
A first-party example of a company building its own cross-system assistant for product work rather than buying an enterprise search tool: Amplitude's internal tool **Moda**, reached from Slack, searches many company data sources and is used by PMs to go from customer-feedback themes to a PRD to a prototype "in a single meeting". It is an early (mid-2025) data point for build-vs-buy of a company harness, for feedback triage feeding PRD creation, and for socially engineered adoption. Downgraded from high to medium because no transcript was available: everything below comes from the show notes and chapter titles, so the mechanics and any numbers are unverified. Bears on loop-source / build-vs-buy, context-and-memory, adoption-model and sequencing (which PM jobs first).

## Themes
- build-vs-buy: chapter "The build vs. buy decision for internal AI tools" (02:53); Glean appears in tools referenced, suggesting it was the buy alternative considered (inferred from the list, not confirmed). Built "in just 3 to 4 weeks of engineers' spare time" (show notes, item 1).
- org-context-grounding: Moda "gives employees access to enterprise data across multiple systems" (show notes); the referenced systems include Slack, Confluence, Jira, Salesforce, Zendesk, Google Drive, Productboard, Zoom, Asana, Dropbox, GitHub and HubSpot (chapter 10:58 "Data sources Moda has access to").
- feedback-triage-agents: "Analyzing customer feedback themes with Moda" (12:43); PMs "analyze customer feedback across multiple data sources and identify key themes" (item 3).
- prd-agents: "Creating a PRD from a single customer insight" (23:24) and "How teams actually use AI-generated PRDs" (27:30); a workflow that "compresses research, PRD creation, and prototyping into a single meeting" (item 4), with prototyping tools listed (Bolt, Lovable, v0, Figma, Cursor).
- enablement-by-product: "A social engineering approach that made their AI tool go viral company-wide in just one week" (item 2; chapter 07:19).
- pm-persona-role-expansion: "Engineers, designers, and PMs swapping roles" (32:37); role-swapping exercises "build empathy and cross-functional fluency" (item 5).
- agent-as-service-many-surfaces: demoed "in Slack" (09:17).
