## Relevance to the project
Names the models behind Airbnb's support agent: 13 models from OpenAI, Alibaba, Google and open-source providers, with heavy reliance on Alibaba's Qwen because it is "very good" and "fast and cheap"; OpenAI's latest models are used but little in production. It also records why Airbnb stayed out of ChatGPT's app launch: verified identity and a need to run "almost self-contained". Good independent evidence for model routing by cost and latency, and for platform control over agent access.

## Themes
- cost-control-routing: multi-model agent; cheaper, faster models preferred for high-volume queries (Chesky, Oct 2025).
- build-vs-buy: Airbnb builds its own agent on others' models rather than living inside a chatbot.
- identity-delegation: verified-member community means a ChatGPT app would need to run nearly self-contained.

## Key points
- Oct 21, 2025 update: action buttons and links in the support chat for changing or cancelling reservations.
- Outcome claims (Airbnb): 15% fewer users need a live representative; average resolution time six seconds vs nearly three hours.
- Plan: Spanish and French that fall; 56 more languages in 2026.
- Chesky advised OpenAI on its apps-in-ChatGPT feature and is friends with Sam Altman.
