## Relevance to the project
Dorsey's own public explanation of the 2024 move to a functional organisation: put "engineering and design and product" at the top of the company and move faster with new technologies "such as all of the AI models". It shows AI was already part of the rationale in 2024, 18 months before AI was cited for headcount cuts, and that the reorg was initially a reporting-line change, not a change to product assignments.

## Themes
- ai-flattened-org (precursor): "removing that business unit lead structure, appointing a new functional lead"; "most of the company will not notice a change at all"; "We're not changing our assignments to products and projects that people have." (Dorsey, Q&A)
- leadership-permission-mandate: purpose is "much better technology, much better design, and ultimately, a much better product", and to "move much, much faster with newer technologies such as all of the AI models" (Dorsey).

## Key points
- Nick Molnar (Afterpay co-founder) to lead a centralised sales function across Square, Cash App and Afterpay.
- Dorsey: open-source AI models "really level the playing field for us".
- Gross profit continues to be reported by segment (Ahuja).
- Dorsey: "Over time, as we build confidence around those things, we will evolve our operating model."
- Prepared remarks say the change helps people reach "true mastery of their craft".
