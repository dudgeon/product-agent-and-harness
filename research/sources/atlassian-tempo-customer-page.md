## Key points
- Case 1 (Atlassian Rovo agents, Jira/Confluence): AI "teammates" inside Atlassian tools, including custom agents built in Rovo Studio. Jobs: PRD / roadmap-item drafting, search across tools, ticket work.
- Atlassian banner (verified): "Tempo's Rovo Agents deliver 30% faster task completion and 3x time savings, making advanced AI accessible to every team – no coding required."
- What the numbers are about (from Tempo's own page, https://www.tempo.io/news/atlassian-case-study, "How Tempo works with Atlassian and Rovo Studio", Tempo Team, undated): "30% faster task completion using agents we built with Rovo Studio"; "3x time savings with the Structure View Builder Rovo Agent". So 3x is one agent, not the programme.
- Agents named: Structure Formula Assistant, Sprint Performance Assistant, Structure View Builder. These are agents for Tempo's own products/workflows (Structure is a Jira portfolio app), not PM-job agents in the sense of PRD writing or discovery.
- Neither page gives baseline, sample size, period or measurement method. Named person: Evelyn Watts, Senior Director of Product Marketing, Tempo (quote about Rovo Studio being usable without technical expertise).
- A search summary says the Atlassian story body also phrases it as "up to 30% time savings" for the first batch of agents (unverified: body not visible in fetch).
- Evidence rating for Rovo overall: LOW-MED (named customers with numbers, but self-reported, no method).
