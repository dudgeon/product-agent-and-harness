## Relevance to the project

Atlassian's decision record: a Confluence page with a status/impact/roles/due-date/outcome header, background, options considered (pros, cons, cost), action items and outcome, with DACI roles (Driver, Approver, Contributors, Informed). Decisions therefore live as pages next to PRDs, found by template and labels, not as a graph object. A candidate "decision" primitive for the harness; the DACI single-approver rule is a reusable review-gate pattern.

## Themes

- artifact-standardization: header fields "decision status, the impact, the involved parties, the due date, and the outcome"; sections Background, "Options considered" (pros/cons, cost high/medium/low), "Action items" (@mentions), "Outcome".
- human-review-gates: "assigning a single approver who ultimately makes the call"; "DACI stands for Driver, Approver, Contributors, and Informed."

## Key points

- "Confluence pages are alive": the outcome may be filled in later after action items close.
- Options may come from "another Confluence page or Slack"; the template is for the final record.
- No mention of linking a decision to a Jira work item, JPD idea or Home project.
