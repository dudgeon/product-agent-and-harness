## Relevance to the project
A survey of ten global financial firms on how they keep regulatory obligation inventories and map them to controls. It is the best quantitative picture found of T18's starting point: most have an inventory, only about half map to controls, and upkeep is mostly manual.

## Themes
- obligation-libraries-as-data: over half map inventories to controls; about 90 percent map to business units and policies; 70 percent to risks; most report significant manual effort (survey).

## Key points
- Survey of 10 global financial services firms; banks have moved from monitoring regulation, to detailed inventories, to mapping them to the business.
- Mapping: over half to controls; about 90 percent to business units and business policies; 70 percent to risks; a few to a process library.
- Coverage: 70 percent cover all their geographies; over half include non-financial regulators; 20 percent include guidance and FAQs that impose no obligation.
- Most firms report a significant manual lift in maintaining and mapping; natural-language processing and AI are being explored but adoption is in its infancy.
- Finer-grained mapping gives more value (EY view).
