## Relevance to the project
An enterprise CPO's view from mid-2025. "Prompt sets are the new PRDs" and "demos before memos" put the prototype plus prompt set (an early eval set) forward as the handoff artifact. PMs shift from process to "tastemaking and editing", or else "Frankenstein product". Every enterprise feature has a governance "shadow". The "Frontier" early-adopter programme runs alongside slower change management. She names human-agent co-working (what to delegate, what to inspect, information agents mediate between people) as an open design space. Bears on intent-shaping-and-spec, adoption-model, governance-control-plane and session-persistence-sharing. It's a vision talk: no numbers and no demo of PM tooling.

## Themes
- spec-becomes-prototype-and-evals: "I call it the prompt sets of the new PRDs"; new features "come with prototypes and prompt sets"; "demos before memos" (22:59). Caveat: not for deep changes "in the bowels of Excel" (27:20).
- human-judgment-pm-jobs: process-only PMs ("TPS report") are exposed when AI "can read and write 50,000 meeting notes"; the editing function matters more, but "you have to earn it now" rather than by title (28:41).
- product-coherence-over-automation: more supply of ideas and prototypes means you need "editorial and taste making ... otherwise you just have Frankenstein product" (24:31).
- enterprise-admin-controls: in enterprise "every time you think you have one use case, you have really two": the feature and its governance (security, auditability); avoid both ignoring it and over-crippling UX (7:49).
- enablement-by-product: the "Van Damme split" between weeks-long AI cycles and slow habit change; "the thing not to do is hold back folks who are early adopters"; Frontier gives rough-edged agents (a work deep-research agent) to pioneers while GA rollouts stay careful for "pharma companies, legal companies" (9:14-13:29).
- shared-persistent-sessions: experiences today are "very single player"; the open question is human-agent co-working: "what tasks can we delegate? What can we inspect?" (55:06-55:51).
- evals-autonomy-trust: an agent is defined by autonomy (a spectrum of delegation), complexity (multi-step) and natural interaction or asynchrony; editable plans and "showing the work" at the right verbosity are new UI constructs (14:18-19:44).

## Key points
- Time to first demo is shrinking while time to full deployment grows; the bar to scale rises (23:58-24:31).
- Reflexive AI use is hard because users must keep "updating the priors" on what models can do (31:17).
- GitHub is positioned as a system (repo, context, agent board) rather than one coding tool, in answer to why Copilot lost share to Cursor and others (46:02).
