## Relevance to the project
Press summary of OpenAI's own Frontier launch (OpenAI's page returned 403). Frontier bundles a shared business-context "semantic layer", an agent execution environment, memory, evals, and per-agent identity and permissions. It is the model vendor's version of an enterprise agent platform, competing with Agent 365 and Gemini Enterprise. For our purposes: another place where a governed intent-shaping agent could live, but early-access only with no public pricing.

## Themes
- agent-control-plane: enterprise IAM applied to human staff and agents; full audit logs of agent actions.
- identity-delegation: "Each AI agent gets its own identity with specific permissions."
- memory-architecture: agents build memories from past interactions.
- org-context-grounding: CRM, ticketing and internal apps joined into a "semantic layer for the enterprise".

## Key points
- Reachable through ChatGPT, Atlas workflows or business applications; open standards claimed.
- Built-in evaluation shows what is and isn't working.
- SOC 2 Type II and ISO certifications cited.
- Selected enterprises first; no pricing or GA timeline.
