Lightspeed resolution rates up to 65%; Synthesia over 6,000 conversations resolved and over 1,300 hours saved within six months; Fundrise over 50% of support volume automated within three months.

- Figures differ by date, customer base and definition; all vendor/partner-sourced. Rating LOW-MED for support, not PM.
