## Relevance to the project

Linear's definition of project is the cleanest outcome-oriented one in this set: a bounded grouping of issues with an outcome or date, owned by a lead team. Useful comparison point for what a 'product meta-harness project' might hold.

## Themes

- work-structure-spectrum: strong opinionated structure (one issue in one project; lead team sets statuses).

## Key points

- Project = 'a grouping of issues that have a clear outcome or planned completion date'.
- Can span multiple teams; one lead team sets ownership and available statuses.
- Each issue belongs to at most one project; split via sub-issues.
- Milestones inside projects; project documents live in Resources (Markdown) with external links.
- Projects can be grouped under initiatives; 'Roadmaps' were renamed Initiatives and must be enabled in workspace settings.
