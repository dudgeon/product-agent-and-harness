## Relevance to the project
ChatPRD's only named-customer evidence: three vendor-published stories (LaunchDarkly, Autodesk, CallRail) with time-saved headlines and no stated method. Upgrades case 9 from "no named customers" to "named customers, vendor-published, unmeasured". One story has an undisclosed tie: ChatPRD founder Claire Vo has been LaunchDarkly's CPO (per Mind the Product's interview title in search results; not fetched), and the LaunchDarkly story does not mention her.

## Themes
- prd-agents: all three stories are about first-draft PRDs and documents.
- vendor-claims-vs-independent-evidence: no method, period, baseline or sample for any figure; aggregate stats unattributed.
- outcome-measurement-gap: every metric is drafting time; none on PRD quality or product outcome.

## Key points
- Index page (fetched): LaunchDarkly "~50% less time writing PRDs" (Tim Cook, Senior PM); Autodesk "Hours → minutes for initial PRD drafts" (Eric Sarault, Senior Technical PM: "Getting the draft up is 80% of the job."); CallRail "~90% faster document & presentation creation" (Jason Tatum, VP Product). Unattributed headline stats: 80% less PRD time, 2x faster iteration, 10h saved per PM per week. User claim "250,000+ product managers". No dates beyond "© 2026".
- LaunchDarkly story (https://www.chatprd.ai/customers/launchdarkly, fetched): ~50% less PRD time, "1+ hr" saved per document, "8 projects" covered by one PM, 2x faster iteration; quotes Tim Cook and Cameron Savage (Senior PMs); method not stated; no disclosure of the Claire Vo / LaunchDarkly link.
- CallRail story (https://www.chatprd.ai/customers/callrail, fetched): "~90%" faster; "10x faster presentations" rests on one quote about one presentation ("maybe a tenth of the time"); team-wide adoption with no count; all quotes from Jason Tatum.
- Autodesk story page not fetched individually.
