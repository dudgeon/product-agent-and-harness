## Relevance to the project
Read directly. Torres's error analysis on 17 interview transcripts found invented details (e.g. a job title) and fabricated quotes made by merging two statements. Her mitigations are code that checks each quote exists verbatim and an LLM-judge fact checker. The 'AI sifts and sorts' principle is NOT on this page: it is Product Talk's Vistaly page copy (https://www.producttalk.org/vistaly/: "The AI does the sifting and sorting. The understanding—and the decisions—stay with your team."), not a Torres quote; Torres designs Vistaly's AI. The glossary names ChatGPT as the model analysed. Its 17-transcript figure differs from the podcast's 15 interviews (producttalk-torres-customer-interview-analysis-podcast); whether they are one experiment is not stated anywhere read. A third page (producttalk-customer-interview-analysis-ai) reports about 30% of ChatGPT's direct quotes incorrect.
