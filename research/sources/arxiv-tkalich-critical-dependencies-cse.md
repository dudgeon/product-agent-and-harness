## Relevance to the project
Interview evidence from two Nordic fintech companies (17 semi-structured interviews) that the dependencies causing the most delay in continuous software engineering are not between product teams but between teams and support functions, and between teams and external entities. That is exactly the regulated-enterprise shape the dependency agent targets: security, compliance, infrastructure and outside parties as the slow edges of the graph.

## Themes
- dependency-types-and-practices: preliminary findings name dependencies between software teams and support functions, and between teams and external entities, as the main sources of delays and bottlenecks (abstract).
- coordination-cost-and-waiting: the framing is delay and bottleneck, not effort: the cost of a dependency is waiting time at a function outside the team.
