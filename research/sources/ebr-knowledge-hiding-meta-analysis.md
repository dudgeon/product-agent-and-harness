## Relevance to the project

Meta-analytic evidence (131 studies, 47,348 people) that deliberately withholding requested knowledge is a distinct, common behaviour with its own causes in job design, leadership and motivation. A capture agent that only lowers the effort of sharing addresses knowledge sharing, not knowledge hiding; the two are uncorrelated, so a separate incentive and trust design is needed.

## Themes

- **tacit-knowledge-capture**: "knowledge hiding is not correlated with knowledge sharing", supporting treating them as distinct constructs (abstract); hiding is linked to lower creativity and task performance and to incivility and deviance (abstract).

## Key points

- Builds on Connelly et al. (2012), which defined knowledge hiding as an intentional attempt to withhold or conceal knowledge another person has requested.
- Antecedent categories that hold: job characteristics, leadership, attitudes and motivations, working context, personality, individual differences.
