## Relevance to the project
C2PA is the leading signed-provenance standard for media files. It is the heavy end of metadata/provenance: useful as a design reference for tamper-evident artifact history but aimed at images/video, not documents in a PM workflow.

## Themes
- artifact-standardization: assertions are grouped into a signed claim, bundled into a manifest bound to the asset by hash; each edit adds a manifest referencing the previous one.

## Key points
- Trust is about signer identity and tamper evidence, not truth of content.
