## Relevance to the project
Trade-press framing of Frontier that relies on OpenAI's own figures; its one useful detail is the delivery model: OpenAI pairs forward-deployed engineers with customer teams. That is a reminder that "buy a platform" for agents often means buying services too.

## Themes
- build-vs-buy: forward-deployed engineers work side by side with customers and connect them to OpenAI Research.
- vendor-claims-vs-independent-evidence: outcome figures (six weeks to one day; 90% more customer time; root-cause from four hours to minutes) are OpenAI's unattributed customer claims.

## Key points
- Argument: the bottleneck is how agents are built and run in organisations (fragmented systems, permissions, governance), not model intelligence.
- No independent analyst quotes; no lock-in discussion.

## Notable quotes
- "What's slowing them down isn't model intelligence, it's how agents are built and run in their organizations."
