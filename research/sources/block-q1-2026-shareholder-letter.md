## Relevance to the project
The first letter after the February 2026 cut, and the first to give a quality metric alongside throughput: production code changes per engineer up over 2.5x versus January, and incidents after a production change down over 70% year over year. It states "100% of Block employees are using AI tools" and introduces Builderbot, an internal agent that reviewed over 90% of production change requests and makes 15% of changes "nearly fully autonomously" with a human making the final push decision. Useful as Block's own scorecard for an AI-native operating model and as evidence of where the human gate sits.

## Themes
- leadership-permission-mandate: "As of early April, 100% of Block employees are using AI tools to do their work." (Business Highlights, p.3)
- outcome-measurement-gap: Block reports throughput (code changes per engineer up 2.5x since January, as of mid-April) and one quality signal (incident rate after a production change down >70% vs Q1 2025, >40% vs Q4 2025), but attributes the quality gain jointly to "our AI investments and our broader focus on reliability" (p.1). No product-outcome or PM measure.
- human-review-gates: Builderbot executes over 200,000 operations a day and makes 15% of production code changes "nearly fully autonomously, with people only intervening to make the final decision to push to production" (p.1).
- agent-factory-sdlc: Builderbot is described as "a multiplier for employees and an end to end automation layer" that automates support, reviews code changes and initiates production-ready builds (p.3).
- legible-org-for-agents: "because we are remote-first, decisions, code, designs, and progress are recorded and machine readable, giving AI a useful operating model" (p.2).
- vendor-claims-vs-independent-evidence: Moneybot/Managerbot engagement stats are Block marketing its own products (about 70% of conversations start from proactive prompts; sellers 33% more likely to return after an insight-led session; Cash App users 5x more likely to return after taking a recommended action) (p.3).

## Key points
- Opening restates the February thesis and claims priority: Block began goose "in early 2024", "the first agentic harness to enable foundation models to execute work across an enterprise" (p.1).
- "Protectors": Block's term for proactive AI that watches for patterns and prompts the customer to act, contrasted with assistants or advisors where "a human asks, AI answers" (p.1). Prompts and insight-led sessions beat open-ended chat (p.2).
- Product signal loop: Moneybot customers request automations before the feature ships; Managerbot sellers ask for cross-workflow intelligence "that aligns with our roadmap" (p.3).
- Financials: gross profit $2.91B (+27% YoY); operating loss $(172)M reflecting restructuring; adjusted operating income $728M; full-year gross profit growth guidance raised to 19% (p.1). Payments Dive reports Q1 restructuring spend of about $495M ([paymentsdive-block-leans-into-ai-future](paymentsdive-block-leans-into-ai-future.md)).

- "Internally, AI is helping us move faster and improve quality." (p.2)
