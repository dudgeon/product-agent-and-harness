## Relevance to the project
Airbnb's VP of Design describes the 2025 relaunch from inside: an 18-month project, a 200-person design team, a new design system built for three product lines, and a CEO who is "always in the pixels". It also shows how design reviews carry business impact: Connor mandates that project reviews include a discussion of impact created. For the project it is evidence of where intent is shaped (design reviews with the CEO) and of a design system rebuilt alongside the product, not before it.

## Themes
- leadership-review-cadence: "I never need to advocate for design with Brian"; "he's always in the pixels too, asking about the radius of a button"; Chesky wanted product description pages "to tell a story" top to bottom.
- artifact-standardization: the relaunch "needs a whole new design system"; "create the right building blocks that would work for both users and hosts".
- one-roadmap-functional-org: Design Week describes Airbnb as focusing "the whole company on a single product calendar", with the summer release as the public moment.
- svpg-four-risks: every design review asks "do people need this? Do they want it? If no, then take it away"; reviews must include "a discussion of the impact created" to build designers' commercial awareness.

## Key points
- Project length: "the 18-month project" (Design Week, launch day, 13 May 2025).
- Team: Connor oversees about 200 designers; joined Airbnb from Apple in 2021 as senior director, VP from 2022.
- Design problems named: new homescreen, new profile, itinerary with hourly and daily timelines, product description pages (settled on a 2x2 info grid after considering video).
- Services launched in 260 cities, ten categories; Experiences in 650 cities, five categories (Design Week's figures).
- Connor on leadership at scale: "build trust, to empower teams, and to be decisive".
