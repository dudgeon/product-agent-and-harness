## Relevance to the project

The latest first-party River usage numbers (September 2026) and the only time series: an animated network chart of employees and River threads, March to September 2026, posted in response to Anthropic's "How employment is changing at Anthropic". It shows growth in thread volume and in people co-writing in the same threads, i.e. the multiplayer effect, but it is volume, not outcome.

## Themes

- **shared-persistent-sessions**: "Wrote in the same thread" edges rose from 61,680 (Mar 2026) to 171,853 (Sep 2026): co-participation in River threads, the multiplayer signal (chart first/last frames).
- **org-learning-loop**: Shopifolk in the graph 4,147 → 6,273; River threads 35,418 → 121,804 per month (chart).
- **vendor-claims-vs-independent-evidence**: self-reported counts with no quality, productivity or cost measure; Dealroom's coverage says so explicitly ([dealroom note](dealroom-river-threads-121804.md)).

## Key points

- Post 2026-10-19 01:55 UTC; ~110K views, ~790 likes (fxtwitter API, 2026-10-24). Links anthropic.com/research/employment-at-anthropic. Media: animated GIF "River floods Shopify".
- First frame "March 2026": Shopifolk 4,147; River threads 35,418; wrote in the same thread 61,680.
- Last frame "September 2026": Shopifolk 6,273; River threads 121,804; wrote in the same thread 171,853.
- Implied: threads per participating employee per month ~8.5 (Mar) → ~19 (Sep). (My arithmetic, not Shopify's.)
- The March figure implies River was already heavily used in March 2026, consistent with a launch in early 2026 rather than "two months" before 2026-05-28.
- Compare 7,000+ "people touched" in the 30 days before 2026-05-28 ([Under the River](shopify-engineering-under-the-river.md)) with 6,273 here: different definitions (touched vs wrote in graph); not comparable.
