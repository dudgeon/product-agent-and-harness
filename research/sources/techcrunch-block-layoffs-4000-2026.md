## Relevance to the project
Same-day independent report of the February 2026 cut from over 10,000 to just under 6,000, with the X post link, severance terms and the CFO's AI attribution. Fortune/AP (2026-02-27) adds an analyst calling Block "a public case study" of a CEO explicitly tying AI to job cuts. Use alongside the first-party remarks in [block-q4-2025-earnings-prepared-remarks](block-q4-2025-earnings-prepared-remarks.md).

## Themes
- ai-flattened-org: CFO Amrita Ahuja: the cuts let Block "move faster with smaller, highly talented teams using AI to automate more work".
- leadership-permission-mandate: Dorsey frames the move as proactive and predicts most companies will follow within a year; argues repeated rounds of cuts damage morale, focus and trust.

## Key points
- More than 4,000 roles, nearly half the workforce; shares rose more than 24% after hours.
- U.S. severance: 20 weeks' salary plus one week per year of tenure, equity vesting through end of May, six months of health care, devices, and $5,000; non-U.S. "similar support".
