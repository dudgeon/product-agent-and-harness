## Relevance to the project
The second Puppet State of DevOps report devoted to platform engineering ("The Evolution of Platform Engineering"): organisations average three internal self-service platforms, two-thirds put workflow automation in the platform team's scope, platform teams increasingly own security and compliance enforcement, and half say a product manager is crucial. Relevant because security and compliance enforcement moving into the platform is the regulated-org pattern a dependency agent would sit beside: the platform becomes the place controls are checked.

## Themes
- platform-as-a-product: 52% say a product manager is crucial to platform team success (press release). Evidence: vendor survey.
- process-governance-load: 43% report a dedicated security and compliance team on their platform; 51% say platform teams enforce software and tool versions for security updates.
- platform-onboarding-friction: organisations average 3 internal self-service platforms, so a product team onboards to several, each with its own route in.

## Key points
- About 500 practitioners and leaders working with or on platform teams, plus a control group; survey 24 August to 30 September 2023; NorAm 38%, EMEA 27%, APAC 22%, other 13%; snowball sampling and a panel.
- Sponsors: Continuous Delivery Foundation, Humanitec (a platform vendor), Women in DevOps.
- Top use cases: automation, productivity, standardised processes; 66% say automating workflows and processes is in scope.
- 43% have had a platform team for at least 3 to 5 years; 65% expect continued investment.
