## Relevance to the project
Block's current one-page map of its AI estate, split into customer agents (Managerbot, Moneybot) and "intelligence and autonomy for builders": Buzz (people-and-agent workspace on Nostr), Berd (desktop agent workspace), Builderbot (coordinates software agents to "plan, build, test, and review changes, while keeping people responsible for final approval") and goose ("software development, research, analysis, automation, and other work"). Useful as the canonical list of internal surfaces a Block PM could touch; it says nothing about PM workflows.

## Themes
- agent-factory-sdlc / human-review-gates: Builderbot "gives agents the organizational context they need to plan, build, test, and review changes, while keeping people responsible for final approval" (Builderbot entry).
- shared-persistent-sessions: Buzz is "an open source workspace where people and agents build together", sharing rooms, context, code and workflows, each participant with "a distinct, verifiable identity" (Buzz entry).
- agent-as-service-many-surfaces: Berd brings "projects, conversations, files, models, skills, and tools into one persistent environment" (Berd entry).

## Key points
- Managerbot "proposes actions for the seller to review and approve"; Moneybot answers balance and spending questions and surfaces suggestions.
- Open releases listed: AI Rules, Jupyter MCP, Xcode Index, Council of Mine; goose grant programme; AAIF co-founded with Anthropic and OpenAI.
- The page calls internal users "builders" throughout, not engineers.
