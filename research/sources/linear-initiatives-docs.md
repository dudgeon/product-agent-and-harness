## Relevance to the project
A concrete tool taxonomy: initiative > project > issue, with documents attached as resources. It shows that 'project' in a tool means a time-bound work stream grouped under a strategic initiative, which may differ from how a PM or a harness would define a project.

## Themes
- project-taxonomy: Initiatives are curated groups of projects tied to a company objective; sub-initiatives allowed; projects can be collected by filters; initiative health on track/at risk/off track from updates; documents attach via a Resources property.

## Key points
- Issue is only implied as the unit of completed work on the page.

## Could not verify
- Page does not define levels beyond initiative/project/issue.
