## Relevance to the project
Telemetry (email, chat, calls, meetings; no content) of more than 61,000 Microsoft employees through a natural experiment. Remote work cut cross-group collaboration time by about 25% and shifted to asynchronous channels. It shows how time use can be measured at scale without diaries and that cross-team coordination is the first thing to erode.

## Themes
- `coordination-cost-and-waiting`: cross-group time fell about 25%, networks became more siloed, and new collaborators were added more slowly.
