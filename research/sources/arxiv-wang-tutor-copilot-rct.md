## Relevance to the project
The strongest RCT of an AI that up-levels the human rather than doing the work: Tutor CoPilot gives tutors expert-like suggestions in real time and the students of lower-rated tutors improved most. The mechanism is the point for PM skills: tutors with the tool asked more guiding questions and gave fewer direct answers, so the AI changed the human's practice rather than replacing it. It is the "coach" role made concrete, with a cost figure.

## Themes
- human-thinking-skills: students whose tutors had Tutor CoPilot were 4 percentage points more likely to master topics (p<0.01); students of lower-rated tutors gained 9 points; tutors with access used more high-quality moves such as asking guiding questions and were less likely to give the answer (abstract).
- tacit-knowledge-capture: the tool encodes "a model of expert thinking" from experienced tutors and serves it to novices in the moment (abstract).
- org-learning-loop: one expert's practice becomes every tutor's baseline at about $20 per tutor per year (abstract).

## Key points
- Preregistered RCT; 900 tutors, 1,800 K-12 students from under-served communities; over 550,000 messages classified.
- Tutor interviews flagged suggestions that were not grade-level appropriate.
- Parallel in the library: Anthropic's "discernment nudge" skill and Ramp's shaping questions aim to shift the PM's moves the same way; neither has outcome data.
