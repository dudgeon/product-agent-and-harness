## Relevance to the project
Thread 1, "Prototyping vs. writing PRDs", gives the clearest community statement of what a prototype cannot carry and a written spec must: "the why behind the feature, the constraints, non-obvious edge cases, the non-functional requirements". That list is close to the project's definition of intent that's ready to ship (constraints resolved, dependencies mapped), so it is useful evidence that the handoff artifact is more than a prototype, against the "prototype replaces the PRD" claim. It also notes that an enterprise PM could not use Lovable or Replit at work because of restrictions. Bears on intent-shaping-and-spec and, lightly, trust-privacy-defaults. Other threads are not relevant.

## Themes
- spec-becomes-prototype-and-evals (contradiction): a respondent argues prototyping helps the PM "experiment and validate ideas" while a PRD communicates to engineers; a prototype "can't communicate: the why behind the feature, the constraints, non-obvious edge cases, the non-functional requirements"; with a prototype, the PRD's format changes but it isn't replaced (thread 1). Another: PRD and prototype "complement each other".
- pm-job-stable-methods-change: "your job, as a product manager, isn't writing PRDs. Of course, it's not building prototypes either"; the PRD's value is "better conversations with your engineers", which a prototype can also serve (thread 1, an experienced product leader).
- prototyping-agents-limits: start from the risk you want to reduce, then choose the prototype, which may be a one-pager, role-play or concierge service (thread 1, citing Testing Business Ideas).
- enterprise-admin-controls: "I haven't been able to use them at work due to restrictions" (thread 1, on Lovable and Replit).

## Key points
- Tool chains cited: Perplexity Deep Research → UX Pilot → Lovable → Supabase → Cursor; spreadsheets as prototypes; Lucidchart/Miro wireframes and hand sketches for 0-to-1 (thread 1).
- Prompted by Mihika's guest post recommending prototypes over PRDs (thread 1 question).
