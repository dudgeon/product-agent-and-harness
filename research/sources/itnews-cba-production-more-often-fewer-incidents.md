## Relevance to the project
Commonwealth Bank of Australia's CTO, speaking at AWS re:Invent 2024, reported that the bank went from assuring about 2,500 control attributes a year to more than 12,000 a month, alongside a 4x increase in the velocity of cyber reviews, double the changes delivered to production in a year, and halved incident resolution time. It is a rare first-party figure from a large regulated bank on what automating control assurance does to the throughput of a governance gate. Claims are the bank's own, reported by a journalist attending as AWS's guest; no method is given.

## Themes
- governance-as-code: "We used to perform assurance of around 2500 controls attributes per year ... we are doing more than 12,000 per month"; similar improvement in monitoring compliance with controls.
- process-governance-load: a "4x increase in the velocity of the cyber reviews".
- coordination-cost-and-waiting: "In just a year we have doubled the number of changes delivered to production"; customer-impacting incidents fell proportionally more than changes rose; "our time to resolve incidents has reduced to half".

## Key points
- Supporting practices: a security academy completed by hundreds of engineers; blameless culture; weekly operational reviews; teams measured against a "12-capability model".
- Engineering NPS doubled over four quarters; a slide reported 67% of engineers feel they can work at a pace that does not add technical debt or security vulnerabilities, 82% feel valued for their skills.

## Could not verify
- How control assurance was automated (tooling, which controls, who reviews exceptions) is not described. First-party claims at a vendor conference; no independent verification.
