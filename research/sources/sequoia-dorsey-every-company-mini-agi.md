## Relevance to the project
Dorsey's fullest spoken account of the February 2026 cut and the operating model behind it, with a transcript. It is where he says "product person" is folded into the IC "builder or operator" role, roadmap ownership moves to DRIs, and meetings have switched from slide decks and Google Docs to prototypes. It also gives the sizing method for the layoff and the trigger (December 2025 model upgrades on legacy codebases). For the project it is the best first-party evidence of how one large company redistributes PM work (strategy and roadmap to DRIs, building to everyone, customer insight to conversational interfaces) rather than keeping a PM role.

## Themes
- ai-flattened-org: max depth from Dorsey to anyone "is probably five"; target "two to three this year"; ideal is "no layer", all 6,000 reporting to him, made manageable because "the majority of our work is going through this intelligence layer" (transcript, roles discussion).
- pm-persona-role-expansion: IC = "a salesperson, it's an engineer, it's a designer, product person, whatever it is", augmented so one person covers the breadth of "a team or 10 people"; durable skills "judgment and taste and creativity". DRI owns customer outcomes, strategy and "what road map allows us to solve customer needs"; durable skill "ownership and accountability". Player-coach replaces manager, coaching "by doing the work", in future an assignment, not a reporting line. Leadership team is expected to fill all three.
- spec-becomes-prototype-and-evals: "just two months ago" meetings reviewed "a presentation or a Google Doc"; "Now everyone is bringing a prototype that they built", on simulated or real data, modified live in the meeting. Hardware prototypes "today we can do it in an hour".
- human-judgment-pm-jobs: tools "will build about 80 percent"; the last 20% is "creativity... taste... judgment". Choosing "which thread are we pulling on" is the human job as exploration costs fall toward zero.
- agent-led-intent-shaping: customer insight used to be inferred from research, interviews, support and social feedback; conversational interfaces give "amazing fidelity of what do our customers actually care about". Dorsey: AI is not making most decisions; ideally "our customers are actually making the majority of the decisions" via their queries, with Block deciding fit.
- leadership-permission-mandate: Dorsey says he spent about three hours each morning in 2025 testing AI tools; the decision went from conception to execution "in under three weeks" after a detailed memo to the board (Botha confirms).

## Key points
- Trigger: December 2025, models moved from prototypes and greenfield to understanding "large code bases and legacy code bases like our own"; tool harnesses "suddenly very mature".
- Sizing: minimum people to (1) keep the service up 100%, (2) stay compliant, (3) grow, meet Street commitments and rebuild "as an intelligence"; then a buffer. Dorsey concedes mistakes.
- Rationale: avoid repeated annual cuts; act before being forced ("I didn't want to have to do it with our backs against the wall").
- Botha: a "ground truth" system replaces political jockeying; product approval should follow signals, not who "pounds the table harder".
- Halligan recounts removing titles and org charts at HubSpot and reversing after about nine months; Dorsey notes he normalised all Square titles to "lead" a year after founding.
- Possible objective-function metrics floated by Botha: growth rate, gross profit per employee, net promoter score.
