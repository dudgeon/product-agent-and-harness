## Relevance to the project
A product leader argues the surviving PM skill is influence: learn what executives are optimising for, bring domain expertise, kill things, and shrink change into milestones. Two concrete practices matter for the future-of-product work: PMs rehearse pitches against a model trained on past review transcripts, and a head of design issued a risk-tiered framework within an hour so anyone can ship designs. She also rejects "PRDs are dead". The page URL was not in the data; source_url is the podcast index.

## Themes
- human-judgment-pm-jobs: AI "is not good at being an anthropologist yet"; deep user empathy plus business dynamics set priorities; distribution and trust matter once software is abundant (1:12:36-1:14:06).
- decision-rights-for-agents: direct-report framing for agents: codify product philosophy so they inherit it, and mark where taste and judgment require "don't do this without me" (1:18:18-1:19:20).
- governance-as-code: risk-tiered design changes (high/low risk, blast radius, release process) to let anyone ship designs (38:45).

## Key points
