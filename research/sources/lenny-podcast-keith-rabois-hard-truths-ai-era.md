## Relevance to the project
The strongest dissent in this batch against the PM role as it is. An investor says the PM, as roadmap-building intermediary, "makes no sense in the future": roadmaps are incoherent when capabilities change in months, so the skill is "what are we building and why", like a CEO. He also distrusts simulated or isolated customer testing, which bears directly on B2.3. Opinion backed by anecdotes (Shopify demos, Ramp). The page URL was not in the data; source_url is the podcast index.

## Themes
- pm-persona-role-expansion: the PM idea "makes no sense"; PM, designer and engineer alike now need to answer "what are we building and why?" (35:30-37:51).
- bets-and-planning-cadence: a year-long roadmap is "ridiculous" when something impossible in November is easy in March; notice a capability this week, ship value next (35:52-36:31).
- spec-becomes-prototype-and-evals: Shopify has not let PMs present slides for two years; every product presentation is a working demo (43:12); a Ramp engineering director with 20 reports ships as much code as before, using AI as a second team (40:25).
- synthetic-users-limits: "An isolated fake experiment doesn't give you actionable insights and often is directionally wrong"; of simulated-human startups, "what are they training on?" (44:48-45:44).
- product-team-shape-future: "barrels" (people who deliver an outcome end to end) number 2 to 15 per company; adding headcount adds coordination tax (17:04-19:36); the CMO is the top token consumer in the best orgs (33:15).
