## Relevance to the project
A CEO describes how a small flat design team produces hundreds of ideas a week while design stays an intentional approval bottleneck, and why PMs become coordinators once legal, trust and safety and data science are involved. AI is not the main topic. It supports the idea of a slate of competing ideas and warns that removing approval steps can cost product coherence.

## Themes
- idea-slate-validation-loops: a 9-12 person design team reviews "hundreds of ideas" weekly; first-day presentation of work; no preciousness (0:23:16, 0:42:17-0:43:02).
- product-coherence-over-automation: "things need to be approved by design to ship"; page-by-page ownership yields no through-line (0:36:29-0:37:01).
- pm-coordination-work-automated: at scale "it takes a village" and PMs "play a really important coordination role" (0:32:49).
- product-team-shape-future: a flat innovation unit alongside a hierarchical delivery organisation, with leaders managing the dialogue (the "Loonshots" idea, 0:18:20-0:21:08).

## Key points
- Features are easy to copy so durable advantage is ecosystem, platform and distribution (0:09:55-0:12:02).
- Designers now ship code (0:35:37).
- Deep one-to-two-hour customer conversations, not surveys; empathise, then invent (0:25:28-0:27:52).
