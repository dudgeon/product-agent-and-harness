## Relevance to the project
Zapier's CEO on leading AI adoption from the top and on mining work exhaust for tacit knowledge. Two items matter here: he ran a meeting-recorder prompt over a year of his own meeting transcripts to extract the company's "unspoken culture" and compare it with the written values (a concrete example of passive mining of work exhaust), and he warns against the "delegation trap", where a CEO writes the AI memo and the task falls through layers to one IC. He also builds an interview-evaluation agent in Zapier Agents that gives an AI yes/no/maybe alongside his own, as a "bias check". Bears on adoption-model and context-and-memory (context creation by passive mining); Zapier sells agents, so the demo is also a product showcase.

## Themes
- tacit-knowledge-capture: a Granola "recipe" builds "the unspoken company culture handbook" from all meetings he records; the first output "shocked" him because it caught specifics the written values missed; the output then feeds job descriptions and interview scoring prompts (7:31-10:35). Claire: stress-test aggregate company communication against stated values (10:49).
- enablement-by-product / org-learning-loop: "delegation trap": the memo passes exec → director → manager → IC, "am I figuring this out for the whole company?"; leaders should run hackathons, show-and-tells and Friday mess-arounds (5:44). Zapier built AI fluency rubrics per PM level (6:50). Value: "don't be a robot, build a robot" (3:19).
- human-review-gates: the interview agent recommends yes/no/maybe with 3-5 sentences of reasoning, emailed to him as a second opinion, not a decision (14:13-15:44). He removed candidate PII from the prompt before demoing (15:44).
- pm-persona-role-expansion: merging job families is "one of the harder culture tasks"; "we only have builders now" is "a little overstated in 2025" (37:40).

## Key points
- Agent definition: "If you've ever seen standard operating procedures, you've seen an agent"; people who write good SOPs build good agents; Copilot drafts the SOP from rough intent (21:35).
- The real constraint is ideas, not tools; "so many tasks that are not economically valuable right now" are where agents thrive (21:35, 23:38).
- Coaching bots in many meetings give him feedback that power dynamics would otherwise suppress (12:58). Emoji reactions in Zapier's Slack trigger automations, sometimes by accident (37:40).
- Roles at risk: hyper-specialised task roles done "almost entirely" by AI, e.g. competitive-research analysts, especially "fleets" of them (35:23).
