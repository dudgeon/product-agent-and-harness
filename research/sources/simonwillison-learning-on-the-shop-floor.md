## Relevance to the project

A respected independent practitioner's short take on River: he quotes Tobi's no-DM passage and compares it to Midjourney, whose primary interface for years was public Discord channels. It is the cleanest independent precedent for "public-by-default chat surface as a learning mechanism" and is useful evidence for both the agent-interface and trust-model dimensions. Opinion, not data.

## Themes

- **org-learning-loop**: Willison believes Midjourney's early success was tied to forcing users to share prompts in public Discord, which helped people learn a "weird and finicky" prompting skill; he sees River as the same mechanism.
- **shared-persistent-sessions**: endorses the public channel pattern as a design choice, not just a policy.

## Key points

- Quotes Tobi's #tobi_river and Lehrwerkstatt passages ([Tobi's article](x-tobi-learning-on-the-shop-floor.md)).
- Analogy: public-by-default surfaces matter most when the skill being learned (prompting, or now agent delegation) is new and tacit.
- No critique of privacy costs.
