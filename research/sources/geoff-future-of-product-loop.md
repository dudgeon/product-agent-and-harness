## Relevance to the project
The project lead's own dictated thesis for where the product loop is heading. It is the framing hypothesis this task tests against the sources: six claims, each mapped to a theme slug introduced by this task. It bears on target-outcome, work-structure, review-and-autonomy and intent-shaping-and-spec.

## Themes
- problem-selection-as-core-skill: most human time goes to critical thinking and empathy to prioritise pressing problems, and creativity to devise ambitious, effective solutions (sentence 1).
- validation-pulled-into-intent: prototyping and technical validation of solutions are pulled forward and become part of intent development (sentence 1).
- idea-slate-validation-loops: a slate of competing ideas goes through many shorter validation loops in mock/simulated, small-scale and larger-scale test environments (sentence 2).
- pm-coordination-work-automated: agents displace people in the tasks that fill most of management today: researching existing capabilities, always-on monitoring, negotiating integrations between systems, stakeholder updates, finding information already written down, stewarding platform and integration information; people now insert themselves to verify tasks agents could perform (sentence 3).
- owner-agent-per-process: every platform, customer experience and operational process has a corresponding agent that owns all tasks not needing creativity, critical thinking, or human judgment and accountability (sentence 4).
- intent-to-market-traceability: all in-market tests and features are traceable to the requirements and intents that produced them, available for the past and curated for the future, mostly by agents (sentence 5).
