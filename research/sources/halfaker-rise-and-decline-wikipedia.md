## Relevance to the project

Shows the other end of the curve: a contributor network that tipped into self-sustaining growth (hundreds of active editors in 2001 to a peak of 56,400 in March 2007) then declined because its quality-control machinery, much of it automated, drove away good-faith newcomers. For the dependency agent's context layer the warning is that the controls added to keep owner-published context accurate (review, reverts, rules) can choke off new contributors once the layer matters. It bears on the "what tips it, what un-tips it" question and on how strict the agent should be with owner and feature-team corrections. Peer-reviewed; authors' copy of the published version.

## Themes

- **two-sided-cold-start**: growth was "a self-reinforcing mechanism: As Wikipedia became more valuable, the project attracted more contributors to increase its value" (introduction, citing Suh et al.), then participation declined from early 2007. Independent.
- **two-sided-cold-start**: the proportion of desirable (good-faith) newcomers held steady, but "a decreasing fraction of these newcomers survive past their initial contributions" (introduction).
- **two-sided-cold-start**: quality control was needed at scale, but "the restrictiveness of the encyclopedia's primary quality control mechanism and the algorithmic tools used to reject contributions are implicated as key causes of decreased newcomer retention" (abstract).
- **org-learning-loop**: "the community's formal mechanisms for norm articulation are shown to have calcified against changes—especially changes proposed by newer editors" (abstract).

## Key points

- Wikipedia's active editors grew from hundreds (2001) to thousands (2004) to 56,400 (March 2007), then fell (introduction).
- The decline was not "right-sizing": most articles were still below "good" quality (only 0.362% rated good) (introduction).
- Rejection (reverts) of newcomers' edits significantly predicts lower retention; tool-assisted reverts (Huggle, Twinkle, bots such as ClueBot NG) increased and strengthened the negative effect (Tool use section; hypothesis results).
- Generic welcome messages work worse than personalised ones; mentoring schemes reached few newcomers (introduction, citing prior work).
- For the agent: route owner corrections and feature-team flags to a person who can explain, not to an automated reject; treat first contributions from a new platform team gently.

## Notable quotes

- "several changes the Wikipedia community made to manage quality and consistency in the face of a massive growth in participation have ironically crippled the very growth they were designed to manage" (abstract)
