## Relevance to the project
The clearest independent attempt to define the term and map the stack (MCP, runtime, ACP, collaboration platforms, meta-harness). Key for the question 'is meta-harness an established term': it shows two senses and says there is no settled standard.

## Themes
- [[meta-harness]]: horizontal sense (layer composing many harnesses: Omnigent) vs vertical sense (systems that optimise one harness's code: Stanford Meta-Harness paper, HarnessX, HyperAgents); lists Omnigent, ACP, Vercel HarnessAgent, Cloudflare Flue + Dynamic Workflows, Conductor.
- [[harness-portability]]: five evaluation dimensions: unified access, security/isolation, scheduling/orchestration, observability/audit, state recovery. Advises individuals to skip it; teams should evaluate now; avoid single-vendor lock-in as standards are unsettled.

## Key points
- Notes Vercel (June 12) and Databricks (June 13) launched in the same week and reads that as emerging convergence; says it is speculative whether Omnigent has what made MCP succeed.
- Product table is positioning-level only; author says to check official docs.
