## Relevance to the project
A platform vendor's survey of 1,053 teams claiming "93% of top performers use a platform built and maintained by a platform team" and that top performers complete common tasks (new environments, deploys, resource assignment) without waiting on an operations team. It describes "ticket ops" as the failure mode that a self-service platform removes. Vendor-run, self-reported, and criticised in trade press for conflating self-service platforms with building a PaaS replacement; cite only as a labelled vendor claim.

## Themes
- platform-as-a-product: "93% of top performers use a platform built and maintained by a platform team" following a "Platform as a Product approach" (Executive summary).
- platform-onboarding-friction: developers who depend on Ops for simple tasks produce "ticket ops when developers need" help; top performers create feature or PR environments (83.6%), deploy to dev or staging (93%) and assign resources via golden paths (85%) themselves; 53% can bootstrap a new app in under two hours (Executive summary).
- vendor-claims-vs-independent-evidence: performance segments are built from the same self-reported answers that measure platform use.

## Key points
- 82.59% of low-performing teams report lead time over a day.
- Low performers are 29.93% of the sample.
