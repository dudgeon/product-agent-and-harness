## Relevance to the project
Two months after describing its home-built assistant (Cortana) to AI Engineer, Uber announced a company-wide rollout of OpenAI's ChatGPT Work agent to 30,000+ employees, connected to Drive, Gmail, Calendar and Slack, with developers staying on Codex. It shows Uber buying the general knowledge-worker assistant while building the engineering platform underneath, and leaves open how ChatGPT Work and Cortana's personas divide the non-engineering space. The internal "AI skills library" is named as adoption support.

## Themes
- build-vs-buy: buy for the general employee agent (ChatGPT Enterprise, GPT-6.1 Sol default); own the engineering harness, gateways and skills.
- agent-as-service-many-surfaces: ChatGPT Work works across Drive, Gmail, Calendar and Slack for research, meeting briefs, presentations, data analysis and cross-app workflows.
- non-engineer-builders: target groups named as operations, finance, marketing and product.
- skills-marketplace: rollout supported by "Uber's internal AI skills library, approved integrations, and dedicated employee training".

## Key points
- Pilot of 1,000 employees expanded to 30,000+ worldwide from 22 Oct 2026.
- Developers "will keep using Codex for software development" (the Aug 2026 cost post lists Claude models too, so this is not exclusive).
- Outcome data promised later; no governance details.

## Notable quotes
- "The companies plan to share further insights into adoption, use cases and measurable outcomes as the rollout progresses."
