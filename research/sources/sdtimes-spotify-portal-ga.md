## Relevance to the project
Trade-press note dating Portal's general availability (Oct 2025) and two AI-relevant facts: AiKA became an MCP client and server, and Confidence (Spotify's experimentation platform) was added to Portal, so experiments sit next to the catalog. Mostly a restatement of Spotify's announcement.

## Themes
- agent-as-service-many-surfaces: AiKA can call third-party MCP servers and act in Portal; it is also an MCP server for Cursor or Copilot.
- metrics-review-agents: Confidence lets teams design, run and analyse experiments inside Portal.

## Key points
- Other GA features: Catalog Wizard, Promoted Plugins, Portal Studio, template editor, Soundcheck Tech Insights.
- No metrics.
