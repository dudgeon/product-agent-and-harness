## Relevance to the project
The starting point of Airbnb's public AI story: Chesky frames foundation models (OpenAI, Google, Meta) as roads and bridges and says Airbnb will win at the "application layer" with an adaptive, personal interface, "like the ultimate concierge". It also announces the GamePlanner.AI acquisition (Siri co-founder Adam Cheyer). Useful as a baseline to compare with what was actually built: support first, then AI search in 2026.
