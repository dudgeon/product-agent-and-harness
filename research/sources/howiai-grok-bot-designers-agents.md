## Relevance to the project
Two designers on a vendor's own agent product show personal and work "bots": one runs Figma production work through the Figma MCP from voice memos while at the gym; another turns "shower thoughts" into interactive prototypes in a test repo without first going through a PM or engineer. One designer keeps a group chat of PM, designer and engineer bots that discuss a seed idea from their own perspectives, a small, informal instance of persona sub-agents. Mostly personal life-admin use, so low relevance overall, but it bears on prototyping (designer-led, cheap to discard) and on the work-structure question of one bot per job versus persona agents.

## Themes
- prototyping-agents-limits: "DevBot" builds two interaction variants of a share-agent flow from a screenshot and a rambling voice memo; the designer rejected both as "too derivative" (25:59-28:48). Vo calls this the "trash can method": build assuming it is cheap to throw away (28:49).
- pm-persona-role-expansion: Vo contrasts the old path, where a designer's idea hit a PM asking for "the ROI ... the OKR" and an engineer with tech debt, with designers now testing ideas directly (28:49-30:21).
- one-job-per-agent: Zheng organises many single-purpose bots (chief of staff, calendar, IT, legal-waitlist checker) under "life" and "work", with a chief-of-staff bot that delegates (31:53-35:44).
- non-engineer-builders: a self-updating personal site with the bot as the whole backend, no CMS (04:35-19:35).
- pm-tool-connectivity: Figma MCP plus a bot "skill" holding layout conventions (artboards side by side, fixed spacing) so files come back tidy (22:47).
- memory-architecture: tip to tell the bot to "memorize this" after a failure so it follows the learned script next time (39:43).

## Key points
- Figma Bro: voice prompt with two screenshots, bot places and scales icons across artboards; later, marketing assets for a bot marketplace launch generated from one labelled template plus brand-book backgrounds; some outputs needed re-prodding (20:26-24:42).
- Persona group chat: PM, designer and engineer bots discuss an app idea from their own perspectives in one thread (33:23).
- Troubleshooting is visible to coworkers via session IDs, so the designer has "gotten nicer" to his bot (39:29).
