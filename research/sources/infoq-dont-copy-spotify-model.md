## Relevance to the project
A 2016 report of a Spotify chapter lead telling a London audience there is "no one way" software is built at Spotify and the model should not be copied. It adds two useful details: squads are led by a product / technology / process triad (PO, tech or chapter lead, agile coach) repeated at higher levels, and company strategy ran on DIBBs with a company-wide visible bets board. Mostly corroboration for the operating-model timeline.

## Themes
- autonomy-alignment-placement (proposed): autonomy within clear boundaries; "autonomy fails without shared direction"; teams need access to stakeholders and a clear purpose.
- bets-and-planning-cadence (proposed): strategy uses Data, Insights, Beliefs, Bets with a company-wide board of current bets that everyone can see (2016).

## Key points
- Floryan warned about halo-effect copying; the model is a simplified description of a system.
- Squad leadership triad: product, technology, process; same pattern at tribe level.
- Simon Marcus (2015, cited): at ~1,500 people Spotify is an "ad-hocracy" tolerating some inefficiency.
- Cultural beliefs cited: "whoever learns the fastest wins"; "it's a marathon, not a sprint".
