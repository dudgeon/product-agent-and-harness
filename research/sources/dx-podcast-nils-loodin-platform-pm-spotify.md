## Relevance to the project
A Spotify platform PM describes how internal-platform product work is set: a centralised strategy codified in objectives, strategy documents and long-term roadmaps, with teams "autonomous with how" they reach far-off goals. He also describes the golden path as a living backlog and how developer onboarding is measured. Mostly relevant to engineering-side product management and to how Spotify writes strategy down.

## Themes
- autonomy-alignment-placement (proposed): leadership sets strategy in objectives, strategy docs and long-term roadmaps; teams choose solutions within those constraints.
- legible-org-for-agents: platform ownership moved from per-platform teams (iOS, Android, web) to journey or domain teams (build, local dev experience); risk of "things falling between the chairs".
- outcome-measurement-gap: time-to-first-PR fell fast once a golden path existed; time-to-tenth-PR tried and dropped; now "time to a meaningful PR" against onboarding cohorts; metrics "measure the environment more than how people feel".

## Key points
- Golden path: documented route "from zero to your first pull request", written by a technical writer with engineers; separate discipline guides merged into one mobile doc; "the golden path is our backlog".
- PMs stay close to internal customers by reading internal Slack and docs ("lurking"), and use repo and build-system signals to map developer journeys.
- Mentions Spotify's culture of long strategy documents.
