## Relevance to the project
A representative of the 2025-26 "taste is the moat" genre: when everyone can build, building stops being the advantage and taste, judgment and brand are what remain. Filed as a specimen of practitioner opinion, not evidence; the page gives no definition of taste beyond "what do you believe others don't?" and does not say whether taste can be taught or encoded. A counter-post ("Taste is a Good Compass, Not a Moat", February 2026, anonymous) argues the opposite: AI lets competitors clone tasteful output from a screenshot, so distribution is the remaining scarcity.
