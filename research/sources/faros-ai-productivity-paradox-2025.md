## Relevance to the project
The most-cited measurement of review load from AI-generated work: telemetry from 10,000+ developers on 1,255 teams shows high-AI-adoption teams merge 98% more PRs and complete 21% more tasks, but PR review time rises 91%, PR size 154% and bugs per developer 9%, with no significant company-level improvement. For shared persistent agents producing work overnight or in parallel (catalogue items 4 and 5), this is the "evidence against": more output piles onto human reviewers unless review capacity scales too. Vendor research (Faros sells engineering analytics), observational, AI assistants broadly rather than autonomous agents.

## Themes
- verification-capacity-gap: "Developers on teams with high AI adoption complete 21% more tasks and merge 98% more pull requests" while PR review time rises 91% (report summary, 2025-07-23)
- outcome-measurement-gap: "we observed no significant correlation between AI adoption and improvements at the company level" across throughput, DORA and quality KPIs (summary)
- vendor-claims-vs-independent-evidence: observational telemetry from a vendor's customer base; association, not causation

## Key points
- Other deltas: PRs per day +47%, tasks touched per day +9%, average PR size +154%, bugs per developer +9%.
- Up to two years of data aggregated by quarter.
