## Relevance to the project
Product-leadership opinion on org design with little AI content. Its useful claims are that coordination carries a hidden cost that sets the pace of the whole system, that decision rights must be explicit and single-threaded, and that restraint is the practicable part of taste. It also argues the median PM is weak, which matters for what happens to large PM populations.

## Themes
- coordination-cost-and-waiting: "a hidden cost to collaborating"; teams move "as slow as the slowest node" (0:06:19).
- decision-rights-for-agents: clear structure of "who can be trusted with which decision"; directly responsible individuals; PMs make decisions founders would have made earlier (0:07:07-0:07:28).
- taste-and-homogenisation: restraint, saying no to ideas with prototypes in hand; AI will find it hard to replicate (0:35:09-0:37:20).
- product-team-shape-future: the median PM is weak because the best exit and the weak stay (0:16:34-0:20:11).

## Key points
- Load high performers with more decisions until they err; defend them publicly (0:48:23).
- Optimise for daily use in consumer products (0:51:37).
