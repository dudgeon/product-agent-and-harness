## Relevance to the project
Peer-reviewed evidence for the homogenisation risk that sits under the "up-level judgment and creativity" job: writers given LLM story ideas produced work rated more creative, better written and more enjoyable, with the largest gains for less creative writers, but the AI-assisted stories were more similar to one another than unaided ones. The authors call it a social dilemma. For PM skills this is the argument that a skill which raises the floor of every PRD may also narrow the range of ideas across a team, and that exemplar-heavy encodings are the likeliest cause.

## Themes
- taste-and-homogenisation: individual creativity up, collective novelty down; AI-assisted stories "more similar to each other" than human-only stories; benefit concentrated among less creative writers (abstract).
- product-coherence-over-automation: the pattern "resembling a social dilemma" where each writer gains and the pool of ideas shrinks (abstract).
