## Relevance to the project
The 2024 edition of the Atlassian and DX developer-experience survey, source of the much-quoted "69% of developers lose 8 or more hours a week to inefficiencies". It is the baseline the 2025 Atlassian edition compares against (see atlassian-state-of-devex-2025). Vendor survey, self-reported hours; the page gives no breakdown of causes.

## Themes
- coordination-cost-and-waiting: "69% of developers lose 8 or more hours each week to inefficiencies" (summary). Evidence: vendor survey, self-reported.
- vendor-claims-vs-independent-evidence: run by two vendors that sell developer-experience tooling; method beyond sample size not stated on the page.

## Key points
- Sample: "over 900 developers" per this page; secondary coverage of the full 2024 report gives about 2,100 developers and leaders, so the sample depends on which document is cited.
- The 2025 Atlassian report names the 2024 top friction points as tech debt, insufficient documentation, build processes, lack of time for deep work, lack of clear direction, local environment setup and collaboration with other teams.
