## Relevance to the project
A third-party (but commercially interested: CData sells Claude-compatible connectivity) analysis of Managed Agents trade-offs: speed and security defaults against lock-in, memory/trace portability and reliability. No benchmarks or customer data; useful as a list of questions to ask, not as evidence.

## Themes
- `build-vs-buy`: Benefits: time to market, less harness maintenance, strong defaults. Costs: lock-in, platform reliability, trapped memory and traces, less customisation; recommends portable memory/trace export and bring-your-own-model.
