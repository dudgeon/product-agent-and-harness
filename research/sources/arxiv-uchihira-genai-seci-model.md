## Relevance to the project

A 2026 academic restatement of Nonaka's SECI model for generative AI. Useful mainly as a landscape claim from the KM field: most GenAI knowledge-management work handles explicit knowledge, and tacit-knowledge work is "fragmentary". It offers no empirical evidence that GenAI externalises tacit knowledge.

## Themes

- **tacit-knowledge-capture**: "much of this work focuses on research and practice related to the management of explicit knowledge"; tacit efforts are "fragmentary" and integrated models "remain insufficient" (abstract).

## Key points

- Proposes "Digital Fragmented Knowledge": tacit and explicit fragments held together in cyberspace, plus a system architecture and comparison with earlier models.
- No case study or evaluation in the abstract.

- Full paper not read; how externalisation is supposed to work is not stated in the abstract.
