## Relevance to the project

JPD is the Atlassian product that holds pre-delivery PM artifacts: ideas, insights and delivery links. It is a first-party example of an 'intent' object with typed variants and an explicit link to delivery work items, which maps onto the intent-factory view of shaping then handing off.

## Themes

- agent-ready-intent: an idea is the basic unit; custom idea types (user problem, customer, bet, opportunity, solution), each with its own workflow.
- work-structure-spectrum: connection fields (Premium) link ideas without imposing a fixed hierarchy.

## Key points

- Idea = basic unit of work in a JPD space; fields such as goals, team, priority score, impact.
- Insights (interview notes, support tickets) attach to ideas.
- Delivery links: an idea is linked to one or more Jira work items when engineering is ready.
- Relation of JPD spaces/views to Jira projects was not explained on the page.
