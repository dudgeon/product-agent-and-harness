## Relevance to the project

A 2-minute Notion marketing video on Ramp using Notion custom agents (Q&A bots, enablement agent, sales-feedback triage). It predates Glass and is vendor-produced; useful mainly as evidence of the earlier agent layer Glass replaced or absorbed and of Ramp's 'everyone a builder' goal.

## Themes

- **non-engineer-builders**: Goal for the year: 'turning everybody at Ramp into a builder' (0:09).
- **org-learning-loop**: Sales feedback piped into Notion and agents triage it to the roadmap; account managers act on it hundreds of times (0:40-1:20).

## Key points

- Proliferation of agents: Q&A agents in dozens of Slack channels, 'enablement Eddie', 'referral bonus Roy' (0:25-0:40).
- Thousands of pieces of sales feedback per quarter now mapped to the roadmap by an agent (0:40-1:10).
- Productivity framed as quality and output, not just speed (1:22). Glass is not mentioned.
