## Relevance to the project

Ramp's CPO lays out the 'software factory' where Glass is the define-stage agent (connected to Snowflake, research, strategy, specs, design system, codebase) amid Inspect, Review Buddy, Testo and Gadget. The most direct source on how a PM-facing agent fits into an end-to-end product development loop, and on the changing PM role. Numbers are self-reported.

## Themes

- **agent-factory-sdlc**: Bottleneck-removal framing (F1 pit stop): identify (customer insight agent), define (Glass), build (Inspect), review (Review Buddy), test (Testo), coordinate (Gadget), then autonomous small loops.
- **legible-org-for-agents**: 'The organization needs to be legible to your agents'; 'every question is an API' (Gadget over Notion roadmap/specs, Linear, Slack).
- **non-engineer-builders**: Inspect: 75% of PRs, 1M sessions, ~1,000 PRs/month from non-engineers; PM roles evolve into technical (factory-builder) PM, tastemaker, GM.
- **evals-autonomy-trust**: 60% of UX issues fixed within 24h by autonomous loops with humans 'barely' in the loop; Review Buddy handles 93% of PRs; Testo caught 425 bugs in 30 days.

## Key points

- Glass (~9:00-11:00 in captions): connects to Snowflake, user research, product strategy, spec conventions, code base; 'AI is your tech lead'; output to engineering is data + requirements + working prototype.
- Insight: a 1M-token window is <0.5% of Ramp's Gong transcripts; built a customer-insight agent with ETL, vector search and clustering after a 'hate channel' got out of hand.
- Takeaways: speed = removing the current bottleneck; next one appears; build the factory, not just the product.
