## Relevance to the project
Pre-AI baseline of the habits that later made Stripe "legible" to agents: a strong internal writing culture led by the CEO and CTO, friction logs, and custom internal tools: Home (search-heavy intranet), **Trailhead** ("internal product and documentation system ... based on that of Stripe's external docs ... like a custom wiki") and **Compass** (project management: updates, linked docs, launch readiness, Slack channel per project, run standups). Independent journalist with Stripe-provided screenshots. Bears on the context-creation hypothesis: Stripe had a decade of externalised knowledge before agents.

## Themes
- tacit-knowledge-capture: writing culture: "vastly more people consume the writing than produce it" (Singleton); CEO Patrick Collison "one of the most prolific publishers to Stripe's internal blog"; CTO aims for more than one internal post a month.
- legible-org-for-agents: Trailhead (structured like external docs), Compass (project status with on-track labels, linked docs, standups prompting "anything blocking you"), Home intranet search.
- harness-over-model: friction logs (context, pros/cons, stream of consciousness) as a habit of writing down user experience.

## Key points
- Compass integrates with Slack (creates a channel per project) and runs standups.
- Home intranet built early 2010s; a small team maintains it.
- Engineers wear a "product hat"; Stripe had no PM role for a long time.
