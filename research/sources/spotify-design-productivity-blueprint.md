## Relevance to the project
Spotify's Design Platform team mapped designer workflows across autonomous teams (with interviews of designers, engineers and PMs) and found the main pain points: designer-engineer collaboration, speccing and Figma handoff consistency, and translating motion prototypes into production code. It is evidence of where context breaks between "shape the intent" and "write the code" at Spotify around 2021.

## Themes
- prototyping-agents-limits: designers struggled to translate motion prototypes into production code ("Motion and prototyping").
- work-structure-spectrum: "teams are fully autonomous, which means that the design process will vary"; the blueprint was built to see that variation ("Understanding your workflow").
- outcome-measurement-gap: three productivity measures chosen: time on routine tasks, number of and time lost to bottlenecks, and design system adoption; counting screens or components rejected.

## Key points
- Definition agreed with a working group: productivity is "the ability to deliver measurable value unimpeded by restrictions or unnecessary complexity in the workflow".
- Speccing: need to balance autonomy with "consistent, digestible specs that anyone parachuting into a Figma file can understand".
- Toolkit (Figma community): workshops, interviews, synthesis into a service blueprint, productivity canvas.
