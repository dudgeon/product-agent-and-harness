## Relevance to the project
The only source in the set that rates PM tasks one by one for AI disruption (1-5 "robots"), so it can be set directly against the jobs map's automation ratings. Its most notable call contradicts the common view: Lenny rates strategy and vision and goal-setting as most disrupted (4/5), discovery and specs as considerably facilitated (3/5), and roadmap and design feedback least (2/5). This is the author's opinion informed by an informal social poll, written in April 2024; the "Ship the product" half is paywalled.

## Themes
- **human-judgment-pm-jobs**: Interpersonal work (product sense, alignment, buy-in, coordination) gains value; roadmap trade-offs and design feedback stay human (2/5) (intro; section 1 items 5-6).
- **prd-agents**: Creating specs rated 3/5, "considerably facilitated": AI drafts PRDs from plain descriptions, PM refines; names ChatPRD and WriteMyPRD (section 1 item 3).
- **research-synthesis-agents**: Discovery rated 3/5: AI finds signal in customer data but customers still want to talk to people (section 1 item 4; names Dovetail, Sprig, Kraftful, Maze).
- **pm-job-stable-methods-change**: PM as orchestrator; keep building soft skills while using AI to amplify hard skills (intro disclaimers).

## Key points
- Readers polled on Twitter and LinkedIn mostly expected high-level skills to be hit last; Lenny argues the opposite, because AI is strong at analysing large volumes of data (intro).
- Section 1, Shape the product (overall 3/5): strategy and vision 4, setting goals 4 (PM becomes editor of AI-suggested goals), specs 3, discovery 3, roadmap 2, product/design feedback 2.
- Section 2, Ship the product (overall 2/5): heading visible, contents paywalled.
- Assumes a pre-superintelligence world; supports the strategy claim with AlphaGo's Move 37 and statements by Sam Altman and Jensen Huang.
- A follow-up, "Why PMs are best positioned to thrive in an AI world" (2024-08-20, also partly paywalled), restates that AI is strong at hard skills (code, design, data analysis, research synthesis) and weak at "glue" work.

## Notable quotes
