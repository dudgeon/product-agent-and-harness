## Relevance to the project
Core framework defining PM accountability: value, usability, feasibility and business viability risk, with named owners. The matrix of jobs by persona in the deliverable is anchored on it, and it constrains agent design (an agent should not override engineering feasibility judgments).

## Themes
- **svpg-four-risks**: Discovery addresses value, usability, feasibility and business viability risk; PM owns value and viability, designer usability, lead engineer feasibility.
- **pm-persona-role-expansion**: Persona accountabilities for IC PM (P1), designer (P6a) and engineering lead (P6b) are drawn from this page.
- **human-judgment-pm-jobs**: Engineering owns feasibility and delivery accountability, so an agent should not override engineering estimates (J6).

## Key points
- Four risks: value, usability, feasibility, business viability.
- Owners: PM = value and viability; designer = usability; lead engineer = feasibility.
- Used for J3 outputs (risk assessment across the four risks) and persona accountabilities P1, P6a, P6b.
- PM is 'responsible for the value and viability risks, and overall accountable for the product's outcomes'; 'The Product Lead Engineer is responsible for the feasibility risk'. The page is a note on the new edition of INSPIRED; it does not literally say 'discovery tackles' the risks.
- Companion post 'Discovery – Judgement' (Cagan, 2020-09-10, https://svpg.com/discovery-judgement/), opened in the re-check: calls a PM judging technical feasibility risk without consulting the engineers one of the most common, largely avoidable problems, tied to overruns.
