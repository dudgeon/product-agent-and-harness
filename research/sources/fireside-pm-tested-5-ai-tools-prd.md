## Relevance to the project
The only head-to-head PRD-drafting comparison found; single author and scenario, qualitative, so weak but useful. Also the indirect evidence that Claude ranks first for PRD drafting. Evidence kind: independent (unaffiliated practitioner, no sponsorship or tool affiliation disclosed; author sells PM/executive coaching and consulting, not a PRD tool). Re-check 2026-10-24: original fetched in full and claims checked.

## Themes
- prd-agents: Tested ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, ChatPRD on one PRD task (AI tutor for high-school science/math); ranking Claude 1, ChatPRD 2, Gemini 3, ChatGPT 4, Grok 5 (cases 9, 19)
- vendor-claims-vs-independent-evidence: No tool affiliation, but author sells coaching/consulting; no rubric, single scenario, loose prompting by his own account

## Key points
- Tom Leung (Fireside PM, 2025-12-15) pasted the same rough "back of the napkin" PRD outline (why, target user, problem, key elements, success metrics) into each tool and asked it to expand to a full PRD; also compared visual mockups and a pitch paragraph for a skeptical VP Engineering.
- Final ranking: Claude, ChatPRD, Gemini, ChatGPT, Grok. On ChatPRD: "feels like, A human wrote this PRD."
- Qualitative impressions; no rubric or scores; single scenario; author notes his prompting was loose. Author sells 1:1 executive, career and product coaching plus strategy consulting (tomleungcoaching.com).
- Case 19: indirect evidence that Claude ranks first for PRD drafting; single subjective test.
- Rating LOW-MED for ChatPRD.
