## Relevance to the project
JPMorganChase's own description of JET, its centralised developer toolchain, claiming "two-fold improvement in stability metrics" and, without figures, faster time to market, more frequent releases and fewer critical production security issues. It shows a large regulated bank building compliance checks, security scans and threat modelling into the platform "from the start", but gives no onboarding, dependency or method detail. First-party, thin.

## Themes
- platform-as-a-product: standardising on a common toolchain to avoid "tool sprawl"; platform meant to be "friction-less" and "invisible to our developers".
- process-governance-load: capabilities "must have compliance checks, security scans and threat modeling built in from the start"; "shifting extreme left on our security and compliance focus, right from the design phase".

- Only number: "two-fold improvement in stability metrics with auto-remediation and advanced testing practices".
- Other claims qualitative: "significant improvement in time to market", "greater frequency of releases", "significant reductions in critical security issues in production".
