## Relevance to the project
A peer-style test of whether LLM role-play agents reproduce stated lottery choices in four cities. Both models were more risk-averse than real people in most cities, distributions were too narrow, and results changed with prompt language. It is direct, if small, evidence that simulated respondents do not yet carry weight for financial-risk preferences.

## Themes
- synthetic-users-limits: GPT-4o mean CRRA 1.387 vs 0.420 real in Sydney, 1.620 vs 0.765 in Hong Kong; o1-mini closer but not stable; Chinese prompts moved results further in places

## Key points
- Agents given demographic profiles chose between lotteries; majority of three runs used; real data from Sydney (64), Hong Kong (997), Dhaka (101), Nanjing (145).
- Simulated distributions looked roughly normal while real ones varied more, sometimes bimodal.
- Limits: two models, context-free lotteries, one homogeneous sample, language effect untested; real data not public.
