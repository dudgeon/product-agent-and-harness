## Relevance to the project
The only analyst survey numbers found on how enterprise data catalogues fare once deployed: only a third of early deployments met expectations to a high degree, and lack of user adoption was cited by 39%. It bears on the risk that a shared context store is built and then not used or trusted, and on coverage and cost criteria. Preliminary results, sample size not stated, so weak evidence.

## Themes
- context-trust-mechanisms: 32% said their catalogue met expectations to a "very high" or "high" degree, 43% "moderate", 26% "fair" or "low"; challenges were tool complexity (43%), lack of user adoption (39%) and lack of integration (39%) (results sections).

## Key points
- Deployment: 10% fully deployed, 41% partially, 42% planning, 6% no plans (results).
- Motivations: discovery 72%, curation and governance 68%, find and access data 66%, self-service 60%.
- Respondent advice includes making the catalogue the gate for all data access and installing a data office to own it (quotes section): adoption by making the catalogue the only route, plus a central curator.
- No figures on metadata accuracy, staleness or trust.

## Could not verify
