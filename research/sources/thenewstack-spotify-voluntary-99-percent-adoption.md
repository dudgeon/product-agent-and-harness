## Relevance to the project

An interview with Spotify's head of engineering for Backstage giving both ends of the internal-platform adoption curve: Spotify reached about 99% voluntary adoption after a year of onboarding and promotion, while many other companies "are stuck trying to get past the 10% adoption rate". The first slice was a software catalog for ownership, because nobody knew who owned most services; that is the same seed the dependency agent needs (who owns which platform and gate). Usage was never mandated; the stated levers were incentives, convenience and inner source. The 99% and 10% figures are one person's estimates in a vendor-sponsored article (topic sponsored by Humanitec), so treat them as anecdote.

## Themes

- **two-sided-cold-start**: after "about a year of internal onboarding and promotion" Greul "reckons 99% of Spotify's development team had adopted Backstage — because it was convenient to do so" (Inner source section). Anecdote, interviewee's estimate.
- **two-sided-cold-start**: other adopters stall at about 10%, often after a rocky rollout, and do not get "beyond the POC"; "Developers have to see how this is beneficial for their day-to-day" (Why is Backstage adoption stagnant at 10%?). Anecdote.
- **platform-as-a-product**: "Even at Spotify, Backstage usage is not mandatory"; "We try to work on people's incentives and make it easier to do the right thing" (Inner source section).
- **legible-org-for-agents**: the first goal was "a catalog to create clarity of ownership" because "It was hard to know who owned the majority of microservices"; it then "exploded internally" when opened to other platform concerns (Getting started section).

## Key points

- Inner source: "Everyone is free to contribute to any repository"; more builders means more users who evangelise (Inner source section).
- Incentives cited: promoting wins and sharing developer-survey results internally; golden paths with guardrails built into onboarding (Inner source section).
- Business stakeholders, not only developers, need to be sold on an internal developer portal (closing section).
- For the project: the agent's seed should be the ownership map, and adoption is likelier to come from making the right path the easy path than from a mandate.
