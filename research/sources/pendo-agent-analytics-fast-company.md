## Key points
- Case 4: Agent Analytics measures usage and performance of a company's own AI agents. Initial launch June 2025 after early access; GA announced 2025-12-17 (Pendo GA release). Agent Analytics is a metrics tool for AI features, not itself a PM agent.
- This release (2026-03-24): Fast Company Most Innovative Companies 2026, Enterprise category; "Pendo measures more than 350 agents and 2.5 million prompts per week"; named early customer RF-SMART (Kevin Gaul quote, no figure).
- Pushpay beta story (GA release and CFOtech): during the beta of a conversational search agent for church leaders, users were "prompting only three or four times, then quitting" (Paul Frank, Staff PM, Pushpay). The note's earlier "informing prompt tuning" is a paraphrase, not a stated outcome.
- Novus: a product agent that detects usability issues, links them to the customer's GitHub codebase and opens fix PRs, and sets up analytics instrumentation (see claude-customers-pendo-qa). Early access: Pendo site banners say "Meet Novus ... Sign up now" and a pt-br page reads "get early access" (seen via search only). No named Novus customers.
- Extra URLs folded in: Pendo GA release https://www.pendo.io/news/pendos-agent-analytics-is-now-ga-helping-companies-see-and-optimize-how-users-interact-with-agents/ (2025-12-17); CFOtech https://cfotech.com.au/story/pendo-unveils-agent-analytics-to-optimise-ai-agents (2026-01-12, Sofiah Nichole Salivio; relays the GA release).
