## Relevance to the project
A practical test for which problems to work on: demand belongs to a person with an unavoidable project that existing options do not solve, found by doing the job with them, not by brainstorming. It supplies a concrete criterion for problem selection and a view of what counts as validation evidence. It is startup-focused, so transfer to a large organisation is by analogy.

## Themes
- problem-selection-as-core-skill: the PULL framework (a project on the to-do list, unavoidable, existing options not good enough, stuck); pull is held by a person, not a company (0:04:10-0:16:20).
- idea-slate-validation-loops: the signal is a "pull-shaped action" (commit now), not enthusiasm about Q4; five calls with nothing learned means change segment (0:25:22-0:26:55, 0:44:57).
- prototyping-agents-limits: AI lets founders "get building out of your system in a couple of weeks", but customer conversations must not be delayed; AI-personalised outreach does not work at this stage (0:31:15, 0:38:21).

## Key points
- Immerse: ride along for days rather than short remote interviews; show the person a written hypothesis and ask "is this you?" (0:18:39-0:22:52).
- An AI note-taker for financial advisors found pull because generic tools did not integrate with their CRM and compliance needs were unclear (0:09:56-0:11:14).
