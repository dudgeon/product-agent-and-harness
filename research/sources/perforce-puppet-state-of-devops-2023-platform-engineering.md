## Relevance to the project
The first Puppet report devoted to platform engineering. Its adoption and benefit figures are the most-quoted vendor numbers on platform teams; its more useful finding is that the platform team's top priorities were product-management work: raising awareness of capabilities, setting realistic expectations of what the team does, and tracking feature requirements. That is evidence that consumers often do not know what a platform offers or how to get it, which is a discoverability problem a dependency agent could address.

## Themes
- platform-as-a-product: top platform-team priorities: raising awareness of capabilities (47%), setting realistic expectations for the team's role (44%), tracking trends and feature requirements (37%) (press release). Evidence: vendor survey.
- platform-onboarding-friction: the need to raise awareness and set expectations implies consumers struggle to find and understand platform offerings.

## Key points
- Survey window 14 September to 25 October 2022; respondent count not stated in the release.
- 51% adopted a platform team within the last three years; 93% call adoption a positive step; 71% plan to hire platform talent within 12 months.
- 68% report faster development velocity (42% "a great deal"); benefits cited: reliability (60%), productivity/efficiency (59%), workflow standards (57%).
- Platform team at or near optimal performance: APAC 86%, Americas 73%, EMEA 64%.
- Puppet CTO: firms "must prioritize product management skills, not just core engineering."
- Sponsors include Humanitec, a platform vendor.
