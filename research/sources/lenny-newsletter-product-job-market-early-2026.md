## Relevance to the project
Job-posting data that cuts against a "fewer product people" forecast: PM and engineering openings at tech companies are at multi-year highs in early 2026, while design is flat. It bears on team shape and the PM-to-designer balance.

## Themes
- product-team-shape-future: over 7,300 open PM roles, 75% above the early-2023 low and the highest since 2022; over 67,000 engineering openings and accelerating (sections 1-2).
- product-team-shape-future: design openings flat since early 2023 (~5,700); PM-to-designer open-role ratio has flipped to 1.27x PM; author's guess is engineers move too fast for a design process (section 4).

## Key points
- Source is TrueUp posting counts across 9,000+ tech companies; it excludes non-tech employers and consultancies, so it says little about banks.
- AI roles (including AI PMs and AI engineers) are "hockey-sticking" (section 3). Open recruiter roles near 2022 peak are read as a leading indicator of sustained hiring (section 2).
- Openings are not hires; the author says many people still struggle to find jobs.
