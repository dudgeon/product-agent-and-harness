## Relevance to the project

Glean's description of an ML-inferred graph over people, projects, teams, products and processes plus per-employee personal graphs. It is the 'enterprise context layer' alternative to hand-defined artifact primitives: infer entities from connectors rather than require users to file things in typed objects. The claims are marketing and unverified.

## Themes

- org-context-grounding: graph of high-value entities (projects, people, customers, products) linked to docs, tickets and specs; permissions-enforced via connectors.
- legible-org-for-agents: entities are inferred automatically by machine learning rather than declared.

## Key points

- Enterprise Graph = relationships among people, projects, teams, products and processes, plus indexed content metadata; personal graphs infer the tasks and projects behind a person's activity.
- Built in each customer's single-tenant environment; quality depends on connectors.
- Claims (unverified): 275+ connectors, multi-hop reasoning, understands the business 'better than any individual employee'.
