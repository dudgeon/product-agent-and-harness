## Relevance to the project
Torres's own guidelines for where generative AI belongs in discovery (J1) and where it does damage. It sets a clear line a research-synthesis agent should follow: use it on large datasets (surveys, NPS, tickets) with the human checking its work, never as a substitute for talking to customers, and never as the first-draft author of team artifacts like opportunity solution trees. Opinion backed by her own small worked examples; dated mid-2024, and she warns it may age.

## Themes
- **research-synthesis-agents**: AI helps analyse large datasets (survey responses, analytics, NPS, support tickets) if the human guides and checks it (Guideline 3).
- **human-judgment-pm-jobs**: Artifacts such as opportunity solution trees are by-products of team thinking; generating them in one click skips the work that matters (sections "Why We Do Discovery", Guideline 2).
- **ai-synthesis-hallucination-risk**: Pressed for Netflix customer stories, ChatGPT produced generic, similar, fabricated stories; synthetic interviews give generalisations from training data competitors also have (Guideline 1).
- **feedback-triage-agents**: Grouping 300+ survey responses with ChatGPT took about an hour instead of three to four, but its first themes were weak and she re-sorted using her own (Guideline 3).

## Key points
- Discovery's purpose: set an outcome, find customer opportunities, find solutions that serve customer and business; interviews build empathy and compounding advantage (section "Why We Do Discovery").
- Guideline 1, don't replace talking to real humans: synthetic users lack the messy specifics of real stories (her Olympics-watching contrast).
- Guideline 2, don't use AI to replace thinking: most value is in going from blank page to first draft; an AI draft dominates the team conversation and invites social loafing.
- Guideline 3, use AI on large data sets but check its work: second example, LinkedIn post analysis where she corrected raw counts to normalised averages and verified the Python.
- Guideline 4, treat AI like a team member: each person forms a view first, then AI is one more perspective (brainstorming, draft story maps, critical assumptions, spotting opportunities in transcripts).

## Notable quotes
- "generative AI can't replace interviewing real humans." (Guideline 1)
- "Technology needs more humanity; not less." (closing)
