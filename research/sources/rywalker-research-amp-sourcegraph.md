## Relevance to the project
Third-party profile of Amp's corporate status, business model and model stance. Useful for supplier-risk framing; most figures unsourced; no conflict disclosure.

## Themes
- harness-portability: Amp selects and routes models across Anthropic, OpenAI and Google; users cannot pick; BYOK limited to enterprise regional endpoints.
- build-vs-buy: proprietary; enterprise tier claims SSO/SAML, zero data retention, BYOK, MCP allowlists, analytics API.
- vendor-claims-vs-independent-evidence: ad run rate "$10M+" and profitability "reportedly", both unsourced.

## Key points
- Spun out of Sourcegraph as Amp, Inc. in December 2025 (another source names "Amp Frontier Corporation"); Quinn Slack and Beyang Liu run it; shared board investors with Sourcegraph.
- Ad-supported "Amp Free" (launched October 2025) dropped ads in March 2026; free tier keeps about $10/day, being reduced for less-active users. A separate source says free signups closed by mid-2026 (unconfirmed).
- Pay-as-you-go at zero markup on provider prices.
- "Amp Labs" enterprise programme announced May 2026 (no details).
