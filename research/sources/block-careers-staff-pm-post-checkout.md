## Relevance to the project
The only open product manager role on Block's board on 2026-10-24, and it writes the AI-era PM job into the requirements: use AI to "turn ideas into working prototypes", submit pull requests through GitHub and ship with engineers, automate repeatable work across research, analysis, prototyping and launch, and judge AI-generated work against real customer scenarios. It is first-party evidence of what Square now expects of a PM at each lifecycle step, though a job ad states intent, not practice.

## Themes
- pm-persona-role-expansion / non-engineer-builders: "Use AI tools to turn ideas into working prototypes"; "You can use GitHub and other tools to make a change and submit a PR" (Responsibilities; Qualifications).
- prototyping-agents-limits / human-judgment-pm-jobs: evaluate ideas and "AI-generated work against real customer scenarios" and decide where AI helps and where human judgment matters; "spotting generic or weak AI output" and giving specific feedback (Responsibilities; Qualifications, taste and craft).
- outcome-measurement-gap: outcomes are named product metrics (order-status inquiries, resolution time, satisfaction, repeat purchases), not measures of the PM's AI use.

## Key points
- Lifecycle coverage in one sentence: "Use AI and other tools to connect and automate repeatable work across customer research, analysis, prototyping, product development, and launch."
- Coordination duty "as more people prototype and ship": the PM connects efforts across Square and partners, implying many non-engineers producing prototypes and changes.
- Still asks for customer research and analytical judgment from conversations, research and behavioural data; 6+ years PM; US pay zones $168,300-$297,000.
- No tool is named in this posting (no Goose, Claude Code, Cursor).
- Board snapshot (Greenhouse API, boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/block/jobs, 2026-10-24): 208 open jobs, one titled Product Manager (this one), against 38 engineering titles. Goose is named in four postings: three engineering roles ("Claude Code, Goose (Block's open-source AI agent), Cursor, GitHub Copilot, ... MCP") and the Cash App Growth Manager role ([block-careers-growth-manager-activation](block-careers-growth-manager-activation.md)).
