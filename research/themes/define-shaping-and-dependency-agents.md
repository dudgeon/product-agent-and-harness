# Themes from define-shaping-and-dependency-agents

Filed 2026-10-31. Evidence labels in brackets are the notes' own (first-party, independent, vendor-claim, anecdote). New themes introduced by this task carry a description; bullets under existing slugs add evidence only.


## requirements-partitions: How requirements are partitioned, and why every single-axis partition leaks
The published ways of sorting a feature's requirements (by quality attribute, by obligation source, by level of abstraction, by template section) and the evidence that each has items landing in two classes or none; the reason a structured inquiry needs a hybrid or a faceted scheme. Related: [[risk-review-as-classification]], [[standard-form-spectrum]].
- [ISO/IEC 25010:2023 product quality model](sources/iso-iec-25010-2023-product-quality-model.md): nine characteristics with sub-characteristics; the model excludes "purely functional properties", so it cannot be exhaustive for a feature on its own. (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [On Non-Functional Requirements](sources/glinz-on-non-functional-requirements.md): the classification problem: a requirement's kind depends on how it is written; remedy is a faceted classification (kind, representation, satisfaction, role) with project and process requirements split at the root. (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Are "Non-functional" Requirements really Non-functional?](sources/eckhardt-are-non-functional-requirements-really-non-functional.md): of 530 requirements labelled non-functional in 11 industrial specifications, most describe system behaviour. (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Volere Requirements Specification Template, Edition 13](sources/volere-requirements-specification-template.md): 27 sections mixing quality families (performance, security, usability) with obligation sources (17a legal compliance, 17b standards as "an internal law imposed by your company"); every requirement carries a fit criterion and the legal sections name who validates. (first-party; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Introduction to Understanding WCAG 2.2](sources/w3c-wcag22-understanding-intro.md): accessibility is an experience quality by attribute and a legal and policy obligation by source; criteria are true-or-false and published per criterion. (first-party; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Types of Requirements as per BABOK](sources/techcanvass-babok-types-of-requirements.md): a third partition, by abstraction and beneficiary (business, stakeholder, solution, transition); names transition requirements, which the other schemes lack. (vendor-claim; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Service Standard (GOV.UK)](sources/govuk-service-standard.md): 14 numbered points mixing qualities, process obligations and policy choices; the form an enterprise standard takes when it is one page. (first-party; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)

## standard-form-spectrum: The form a standard takes
- [On Non-Functional Requirements](sources/glinz-on-non-functional-requirements.md): "representation determines verification": operational by review, test or formal verification; quantitative by measurement; qualitative by stakeholder judgement or derived metrics; declarative by review (Table 2). (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Volere Requirements Specification Template, Edition 13](sources/volere-requirements-specification-template.md): fit criterion per requirement; for standards, "the appropriate standard-keeper certifies that the standard has been adhered to". (first-party; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)

## rules-vs-judgment-encoding: Encoding standards as rules vs as reasons and judgment
- [Introduction to Understanding WCAG 2.2](sources/w3c-wcag22-understanding-intro.md): each success criterion "will be either true or false" when tested; the Understanding documents carry the intent and are "not required to meet WCAG". (first-party; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)

## legible-org-for-agents: Make the organisation legible to agents
- [Service Standard (GOV.UK)](sources/govuk-service-standard.md): one page, 14 numbered imperatives, each with a sub-page on how to meet it; a readable form for an enterprise standard an agent must apply. (first-party; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [How Spotify Achieved a Voluntary 99% Internal Platform Adoption Rate](sources/thenewstack-spotify-voluntary-99-percent-adoption.md): Backstage began as "a catalog to create clarity of ownership" because "It was hard to know who owned the majority of microservices". (anecdote; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)

## dependency-term-collision: "Dependency" means packages to engineers and outside parties to programme managers
The same word names two unrelated things: to a software engineer, a package and version range the build resolves; to a programme manager, something the work needs that the team does not control. A module about the second must be named so engineers do not hear the first. Related: [[dependency-types-and-practices]].
- [package.json (npm CLI documentation)](sources/npm-docs-package-json-dependencies.md): "Dependencies are specified in a simple object that maps a package name to a version range", resolved at install time; nothing about people, approvals or calendars (define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Dependency (project management)](sources/wikipedia-dependency-project-management.md): "a link among a project's terminal elements", typed finish-to-start and so on, with causal, resource and discretionary reasons; no mention of software packages (define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)

## dependency-types-and-practices: What a dependency is and how large organisations manage dependencies today
- [Dependency (project management)](sources/wikipedia-dependency-project-management.md): the four sequence types and three reasons for a dependency; the planning sense the project's primer and taxonomy extend (define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)

## context-trust-mechanisms: How trust in written knowledge is created, measured and lost
The evidence from wikis, Q&A sites, documentation, data catalogues and truth discovery on what makes shared written knowledge trusted (provenance, ownership, review, use and correction, expiry, consensus) and how each is measured; the basis for scoring trust mechanisms for context that agent sessions write back. Related: [[stale-records-mislead-agents]], [[certified-metrics-layer]].
- [Internet encyclopaedias go head to head](sources/nature-giles-internet-encyclopaedias-head-to-head.md): blind expert review of 42 science entries: 162 vs 123 errors (about 3.9 vs 2.9 per entry), serious errors 4 each; open editing came close to a curated reference. (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Assessing the value of cooperation in Wikipedia](sources/arxiv-wilkinson-huberman-cooperation-wikipedia.md): featured articles have more edits and distinct editors than others of the same visibility and age; attention concentrates in a heavy tail. (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Assigning Trust to Wikipedia Content](sources/ucsc-adler-assigning-trust-wikipedia-content.md): WikiTrust: bottom-half-trust text is 3.4% of text but 66% of next-revision deletions (33% vs 1.9% deletion probability); validated against stability, not truth. (first-party; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Wikipedia Signpost: Vandalism study](sources/wikipedia-signpost-priedhorsky-vandalism-study.md): damaged-view probability 0.0037; about half of damage repaired by the first or second view, 11% survived 100+ views; bots cut exposure. (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Wikipedia:Verifiability](sources/wikipedia-verifiability-policy.md): burden of citation on whoever adds material; uncited challengeable claims may be removed; exceptional claims need several sources. (first-party; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Help:Ranking (Wikidata)](sources/wikidata-help-ranking.md): references record where a value came from, ranks record which to use; deprecated values kept but excluded from queries. (first-party; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [An Empirical Study of Obsolete Answers on Stack Overflow](sources/arxiv-zhang-obsolete-answers-stack-overflow.md): 58.4% of obsolete answers obsolete when posted; only 20.5% updated once flagged; 118 days average to react. (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Software Documentation Issues Unveiled](sources/icse-aghajani-software-documentation-issues-unveiled.md): 162 issue types from 878 artefacts; correctness, completeness and up-to-dateness defined as distinct failures. (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Software Engineering at Google, Chapter 10: Documentation](sources/abseil-swe-at-google-documentation-chapter.md): owners plus freshness dates with reminders after about three months; "Last reviewed by" byline raised adoption; accuracy and freshness not yet measured by tools. (first-party; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [The importance of a handbook-first approach to communication](sources/gitlab-handbook-handbook-first-communication.md): anyone proposes, code owners and DRIs approve; wikis lose trust when updated by a few. (first-party; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Beyond Accuracy](sources/mit-wang-strong-beyond-accuracy.md): 15 consumer-derived data-quality dimensions in 4 categories; believability, reputation and task-relative timeliness defined. (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Knowledge-Based Trust](sources/arxiv-dong-knowledge-based-trust.md): correctness-based trust is almost orthogonal to PageRank; 14 of 15 popular gossip sites high PageRank, all bottom-half trust; copying undermines agreement. (first-party; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [A Survey on Truth Discovery](sources/arxiv-li-survey-truth-discovery.md): weighted consensus beats voting but assumes independent sources, many claims per source, and ground truth for evaluation. (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Use Certification to Help Users Find Trusted Data (Tableau)](sources/tableau-certified-data-sources.md): role-granted certified badge with certifier and note, higher search rank; no expiry. (first-party; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [How to Verify your Knowledge in Guru](sources/guru-introducing-verification.md): named verifier plus interval per card, stale cards flagged, owner edits count as verification; no outcome data. (vendor-claim; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Databook (Uber)](sources/uber-databook-metadata-platform.md): hand-maintained catalogue replaced by crawled and event-fed metadata; people supply only descriptions. (first-party; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Preliminary Survey Results on Data Catalog Adoption](sources/eckerson-data-catalog-adoption-survey-2021.md): 32% of early catalogues met expectations highly; 39% cite lack of user adoption. (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)

## stale-records-mislead-agents: Stale process records become wrong instructions
- [An Empirical Study of Obsolete Answers on Stack Overflow](sources/arxiv-zhang-obsolete-answers-stack-overflow.md): 44.1% of obsolete answers were accepted answers; 227 days on average to the first update after a comment flags obsolescence. (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Software Documentation Issues Unveiled](sources/icse-aghajani-software-documentation-issues-unveiled.md): up-to-dateness is 39% of content issues; outdated references most frequent. (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Software Engineering at Google, Chapter 10: Documentation](sources/abseil-swe-at-google-documentation-chapter.md): GooWiki audit: about 90% of documents had no views or updates in recent months; no owners, so many became obsolete. (first-party; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [How to Verify your Knowledge in Guru](sources/guru-introducing-verification.md): stale card choices are verify, edit or archive; changing verifier resets to unverified. (vendor-claim; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Databook (Uber)](sources/uber-databook-metadata-platform.md): static HTML catalogue could not keep pace; outdated metadata served by design during collection outages. (first-party; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)

## certified-metrics-layer: Certified metrics layer
- [Use Certification to Help Users Find Trusted Data (Tableau)](sources/tableau-certified-data-sources.md): certification marks data that meets organisational standards; criteria left to each organisation to define. (first-party; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)

## compounding-context-repo: Context that compounds with each shipped feature
- [The importance of a handbook-first approach to communication](sources/gitlab-handbook-handbook-first-communication.md): document first, then announce; 90-day chat retention forces decisions into the single source of truth with a merge-request trail. (first-party; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Why CSCW Applications Fail](sources/grudin-why-cscw-applications-fail.md): "the organization may adapt to the computer system, but an application program must adapt to the organization"; upkeep will not become a job requirement for each new tool. (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)

## two-sided-cold-start: Cold start and critical mass for internal platforms and knowledge bases
Internal tools whose value depends on two groups (users who ask, owners who supply context) face a chicken-and-egg start: each side waits for the other, and the side doing the work is often not the side that benefits. The evidence on what seeds such networks (single-player use, founders' standing, courting the hard side, low-effort contribution) and what tips or un-tips them; it bears on the dependency-mapping agent's cold-start strategy and its context layer. Related: [[platform-as-a-product]], [[tacit-knowledge-capture]].
- [Come for the tool, stay for the network](sources/cdixon-come-for-the-tool-stay-for-the-network.md): single-player tools are "kindling" that builds initial critical mass; the network then supplies the long-term value. (anecdote; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Previewing a full chapter of The Cold Start Problem](sources/andrewchen-cold-start-problem-chapter-one.md): "If a workplace chat product doesn't have all your colleagues on it, it won't be adopted at the office." (anecdote; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Solve a Hard Problem (Tinder)](sources/andrewchen-solve-a-hard-problem-hard-side.md): a minority "hard side" creates disproportionate value and is hardest to win; Uber power drivers about 20% of supply, about 60% of trips. (anecdote; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [The 90-9-1 Rule for Participation Inequality](sources/nngroup-participation-inequality-90-9-1.md): 90% lurk, 9% contribute a little, 1% most; Wikipedia's top 1,000 users (0.003%) made about two-thirds of edits. (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Toward a "Critical Mass" Theory of Interactive Media](sources/markus-critical-mass-theory-interactive-media.md): universal access is a public good and use is reciprocally interdependent, so interactive media are "extremely vulnerable to start-up problems and discontinuance" (abstract). (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Platform Competition in Two-Sided Markets](sources/rochet-tirole-platform-competition-two-sided-markets.md): platforms must "get both sides of the market on board" and set the price structure across sides to do it (abstract). (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Two-Sided Network Effects](sources/parker-van-alstyne-two-sided-network-effects.md): rational to give one side a product free in perpetuity; either content providers or consumers can be the free side (abstract). (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Why CSCW Applications Fail](sources/grudin-why-cscw-applications-fail.md): Problem 1, "the disparity between who does the work and who gets the benefit"; fix by making everyone benefit directly and minimising or rewarding the extra work. (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Design Lessons from the Fastest Q&A Site in the West](sources/mamykina-stack-overflow-design-lessons-chi-2011.md): founders' blogs (about 140,000 readers) and podcasts built critical mass before launch; thousands asking and answering on day one; 1% of users supplied 27.8% of answers. (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [The Rise and Decline of an Open Collaboration System](sources/halfaker-rise-and-decline-wikipedia.md): self-reinforcing growth to 56,400 active editors (March 2007), then decline as tool-assisted rejection of good-faith newcomers cut retention. (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Lurking as personal trait or situational disposition?](sources/muller-ibm-lurking-enterprise-social-media.md): in IBM's internal communities most contributors also lurked elsewhere; contribution depends on stake in the topic or task (abstract). (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Contributing Knowledge to Electronic Knowledge Repositories](sources/kankanhalli-contributing-knowledge-electronic-repositories.md): self-efficacy and enjoyment in helping drive contribution; rewards and reciprocity work only in some contexts (abstract). (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Strategies for adopting (Backstage documentation)](sources/backstage-docs-adopting.md): "platform of platforms" marketplace; 85% of contributions from outside the central team, 135 plugins from 63 teams (Spotify's own, unaudited). (first-party; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [How Spotify Achieved a Voluntary 99% Internal Platform Adoption Rate](sources/thenewstack-spotify-voluntary-99-percent-adoption.md): about 99% voluntary adoption at Spotify after a year of promotion; other adopters often stall near 10% (interviewee's estimates). (anecdote; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Microsoft case study (Stack Overflow for Teams)](sources/stackoverflow-teams-microsoft-case-study.md): old internal Q&A about 900 users; new instance over 70,000 users and 80,000 questions in about 2.5 years, helped by familiarity with public Stack Overflow. (vendor-claim; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)

## platform-as-a-product: Platform as a product, and its evidence
- [Strategies for adopting (Backstage documentation)](sources/backstage-docs-adopting.md): central team runs the portal as a product and treats contributing platform teams as customers (embedding, hack days, usage digests). (first-party; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [How Spotify Achieved a Voluntary 99% Internal Platform Adoption Rate](sources/thenewstack-spotify-voluntary-99-percent-adoption.md): "Even at Spotify, Backstage usage is not mandatory"; incentives and convenience, not mandate. (anecdote; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Solve a Hard Problem (Tinder)](sources/andrewchen-solve-a-hard-problem-hard-side.md): win the hard side by solving its own problems, not by asking it to contribute. (anecdote; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Why CSCW Applications Fail](sources/grudin-why-cscw-applications-fail.md): "the best solution is to try to insure that everyone benefits directly from using the application". (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)

## tacit-knowledge-capture: Capturing tacit knowledge from people for agents
- [The 90-9-1 Rule for Participation Inequality](sources/nngroup-participation-inequality-90-9-1.md): widen contribution by making it "a side effect" of normal use and letting people edit defaults rather than create. (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Lurking as personal trait or situational disposition?](sources/muller-ibm-lurking-enterprise-social-media.md): ask owners narrow questions tied to their own work; disposition toward the topic decides contribution. (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Contributing Knowledge to Electronic Knowledge Repositories](sources/kankanhalli-contributing-knowledge-electronic-repositories.md): loss of knowledge power did not reduce contribution; codification effort matters through context. (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)

## org-learning-loop: One person's win becomes everyone's baseline
- [Design Lessons from the Fastest Q&A Site in the West](sources/mamykina-stack-overflow-design-lessons-chi-2011.md): median first answer 11 minutes, flat as traffic grew; frequent return of answerers mattered more than total users. (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [The Rise and Decline of an Open Collaboration System](sources/halfaker-rise-and-decline-wikipedia.md): norm-making "calcified against changes", especially from newer editors. (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Toward a "Critical Mass" Theory of Interactive Media](sources/markus-critical-mass-theory-interactive-media.md): diffusion inside a community should be modelled as a critical-mass problem, not individual adoption. (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Microsoft case study (Stack Overflow for Teams)](sources/stackoverflow-teams-microsoft-case-study.md): repeat questions answered "three times a month" before; "repeat questions do not happen anymore" after (single interviewee). (vendor-claim; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)

## enablement-by-product: The product is the enablement
- [Come for the tool, stay for the network](sources/cdixon-come-for-the-tool-stay-for-the-network.md): the tool must be worth using with nobody else on it. (anecdote; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
- [Design Lessons from the Fastest Q&A Site in the West](sources/mamykina-stack-overflow-design-lessons-chi-2011.md): meta site and near-daily releases kept a continuous feedback loop with users. (independent; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)

- [Strategies for adopting (Backstage documentation)](sources/backstage-docs-adopting.md): platform teams expose their tools through plugins they own, changing API and UI together. (first-party; define-shaping-and-dependency-agents)
